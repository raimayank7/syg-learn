The keys to learn first

Nobody memorises a table. Learn the few keys you will press hundreds of times a day, and look the rest up when you need them. Press the "Essential" button above to see our selection. In order of use:

  1. Navigation: Enter to go in, Esc to come out, Alt+G to jump anywhere, Ctrl+A to save.
  2. Dates: F2 for the date of the voucher, Alt+F2 for the period of a report. The date is the commonest source of wrong entries, so make F2 a reflex.
  3. The six vouchers: F4 to F9. Say them in order: Contra, Payment, Receipt, Journal, Sales, Purchase.
  4. While entering: Alt+C to create a missing ledger or item on the spot, Ctrl+H to change the voucher mode, Alt+D to delete.
  5. Reports: Alt+F1 for detail, Alt+C for a comparison column, Ctrl+P to print, F12 to configure.
  6. Housekeeping: F11 for company features, Alt+K for the company menu, Alt+Y for backup.

How the keys are organised

TallyPrime follows a pattern that makes keys easier to remember. The function keys alone (F2 to F12) are for the most frequent actions on the screen in front of you. Alt with a letter usually opens something from the top menu or acts on the whole voucher or report: Alt+G Go To, Alt+K Company, Alt+Y Data, Alt+P Print, Alt+E Export, Alt+D Delete. Ctrl with a letter usually acts on the current item at once: Ctrl+A accept, Ctrl+P print this, Ctrl+E export this. The same key can mean different things on different screens (Alt+C creates a master in a voucher and adds a column in a report), which is why the table has a "Where" column.

The available keys are always shown on the screen itself: the right-hand button bar and the bottom bar list what works where you are. When in doubt, look there.

Practising

Unplug the mouse for half an hour a day in your practice company. It is slow for three days and then faster than the mouse ever was. People who enter vouchers for a living work almost entirely from the keyboard.

Source and versions

This table is our own summary, in our own words, of the keys listed on the keyboard shortcuts page of help.tallysolutions.com as read on 12 November 2026, when TallyPrime 7.2 was the current release. It is a selection, not the complete list: Tally's page also lists keys for GST reports, banking, e-invoicing and other features. Keys can change between releases, and Tally.ERP 9 used different keys for several actions. If a key does not behave as described, press Ctrl+F1 in Tally for the help on that screen.

Questions learners ask

What is the shortcut for Go To in TallyPrime?

Alt+G. It lets you open any report or create a master or voucher from wherever you are.

Which keys open the voucher types?

F4 Contra, F5 Payment, F6 Receipt, F7 Journal, F8 Sales, F9 Purchase, Alt+F5 Debit Note and Alt+F6 Credit Note.

Are the shortcuts the same in Tally.ERP 9?

Not all. TallyPrime changed several keys. This table is for TallyPrime; check Tally's help for the older product.
