Lesson 12 of 20

Debit notes and credit notes: returns in TallyPrime

Goods come back and bills get corrected. A credit note reduces what a customer owes you; a debit note reduces what you owe a supplier.

Rates here are assumed. The examples use GST at 18% (CGST 9% + SGST 9%, or IGST 18%) only to show the entries. Actual rates depend on the goods or service and change by notification. For rates and return rules see the official sites gst.gov.in and cbic-gst.gov.in, or ask your accountant.

Which is which

Credit NoteDebit Note
KeyAlt+F6Alt+F5
Used byThe sellerThe buyer
Typical reasonCustomer returns goods; price charged was too highGoods returned to supplier; supplier overcharged
Party's account isCredited (customer owes less)Debited (we owe the supplier less)
EffectReduces sales and output tax; stock comes backReduces purchases and input tax; stock goes out

The names come from the party's account: a credit note credits the customer; a debit note debits the supplier.

Switching them on

If Alt+F6 and Alt+F5 do nothing, the voucher types are not active. Press F10 on the voucher screen to list all voucher types and choose Credit Note or Debit Note; Tally will offer to activate it.

Sales return: Credit Note

City Book Depot returns 10 reams of A4 paper from the sale of 8 April (rate ₹300, GST assumed 18%). Value ₹3,000; CGST ₹270; SGST ₹270; total ₹3,540.

  1. Vouchers > Alt+F6.
  2. Date; then the original invoice number and date when asked. The link to the original invoice is needed for tax reporting.
  3. Party: City Book Depot. Sales ledger: Sales A/c.
  4. Item: A4 paper, 10 reams at ₹300. Tax ledgers as in the sale.
  5. Bill-wise: choose Agst Ref against the original invoice. Save.
Credit Note
LedgerDebit (₹)Credit (₹)
DrSales A/c (A4 paper, 10 reams @ 300)3,000
DrOutput CGST270
DrOutput SGST270
CrCity Book Depot3,540

The customer now owes ₹42,480 − ₹3,540 = ₹38,940, and 10 reams are back in stock.

Purchase return: Debit Note

Shanti Traders returns 20 damaged reams to Mohan Paper Mart from the purchase of 5 April (rate ₹250). Value ₹5,000; CGST ₹450; SGST ₹450; total ₹5,900.

  1. Vouchers > Alt+F5.
  2. Date; original supplier invoice number and date.
  3. Party: Mohan Paper Mart. Purchase ledger: Purchase A/c.
  4. Item: A4 paper, 20 reams at ₹250. Tax ledgers as in the purchase.
  5. Agst Ref against the supplier's bill. Save.
Debit Note
LedgerDebit (₹)Credit (₹)
DrMohan Paper Mart5,900
CrPurchase A/c (A4 paper, 20 reams @ 250)5,000
CrInput CGST450
CrInput SGST450

We now owe the supplier ₹59,000 − ₹5,900 = ₹53,100, and stock falls by 20 reams.

Price corrections without goods moving

If the customer was charged ₹300 a ream when ₹290 was agreed, a credit note for the difference is passed in accounting invoice mode (no stock movement): 120 reams × ₹10 = ₹1,200 plus tax. Likewise a debit note when a supplier overcharged.

Why not just change the original voucher?

Once an invoice has been issued, and especially once it has been reported in a tax return, altering it breaks the trail. The return is a separate event on a later date, with its own document. Notes keep both events visible: the sale, and the return against it.

Under GST

Credit and debit notes are reported in GST returns and reduce or increase the tax liability, subject to conditions and time limits in the law. Tally includes them in its GST reports when they are linked to the original invoice. For the conditions and time limits, rely on your accountant and the GST portal, not on this page.

Checks

  • The party's ledger shows the reduced balance.
  • Stock Summary shows the quantity adjusted.
  • The note is tagged against the right bill in the Outstandings report.
  • The paper credit or debit note, and the goods return slip, are filed with the voucher number.

Common mistakes

  • Using a debit note for a sales return because "the customer's goods came in". Think of the party's balance, not the goods.
  • Entering the return at today's selling price instead of the rate on the original invoice.
  • Leaving out the original invoice reference.
  • Forgetting the tax lines, so that only the value is reversed.

Questions learners ask

Which voucher is used for sales return in Tally?

A Credit Note, opened with Alt+F6. It credits the customer and reduces sales.

Which voucher is used for purchase return?

A Debit Note, opened with Alt+F5. It debits the supplier and reduces purchases.

Why are Alt+F5 and Alt+F6 not working?

The Debit Note and Credit Note voucher types may not be active. Press F10 on the voucher screen, choose the type, and activate it.
