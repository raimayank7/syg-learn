Lesson 9 of 20

Journal voucher (F7)

The Journal is for everything that has no cash and no bank in it: adjustments, transfers between ledgers and corrections.

When to use it

  • Year-end adjustments: depreciation, outstanding expenses, prepaid expenses, accrued income.
  • Discount allowed or received on settlement.
  • Bad debts written off.
  • Goods taken by the owner, given as samples or lost.
  • Transfer from one ledger to another: correcting an entry made under the wrong ledger.
  • Purchase or sale of a fixed asset on credit, in offices that record these by journal.
  • Tax adjustments and set-offs.

By default Tally does not let you use Cash or Bank ledgers in a Journal, which protects you from using it where a Payment, Receipt or Contra belongs.

Steps

  1. Gateway of Tally > Vouchers, press F7.
  2. F2 for the date. Adjustments are usually dated the last day of the month or year.
  3. Enter the debit line: "Dr" (shown as By in some layouts), the ledger and the amount.
  4. Enter the credit line: "Cr" (To), the ledger and the amount.
  5. Add more lines if needed. Tally shows the totals; debit and credit must be equal before it will save.
  6. Narration, then Ctrl+A.

Examples

1. Depreciation of ₹6,000 on furniture for the year.

Journal
LedgerDebit (₹)Credit (₹)
DrDepreciation6,000
CrFurniture6,000

Depreciation is a ledger under Indirect Expenses.

2. Salaries of ₹30,000 for March are due but will be paid in April.

Journal
LedgerDebit (₹)Credit (₹)
DrSalaries30,000
CrSalaries Payable30,000

Salaries Payable is under Current Liabilities. When the salaries are paid in April, the Payment voucher debits Salaries Payable, not Salaries.

3. Insurance of ₹12,000 was paid in January for a year; ₹9,000 relates to next year.

Journal
LedgerDebit (₹)Credit (₹)
DrPrepaid Insurance9,000
CrInsurance9,000

Prepaid Insurance is under Current Assets (Loans & Advances).

4. A customer's balance of ₹3,500 is irrecoverable.

Journal
LedgerDebit (₹)Credit (₹)
DrBad Debts3,500
CrCity Book Depot3,500

5. Discount of ₹1,000 received from a supplier on settlement.

Journal
LedgerDebit (₹)Credit (₹)
DrMohan Paper Mart1,000
CrDiscount Received1,000

6. The owner took goods costing ₹2,000 for personal use.

Journal
LedgerDebit (₹)Credit (₹)
DrDrawings2,000
CrPurchase A/c2,000

Where stock items and GST are maintained, such a withdrawal also needs the stock to be reduced and the input tax on those goods to be reversed. Ask your accountant for the office's method.

7. Electricity of ₹2,400 was wrongly debited to Shop Rent.

Journal
LedgerDebit (₹)Credit (₹)
DrElectricity Charges2,400
CrShop Rent2,400

For a simple mistake like this you could instead open the original voucher from the Day Book and change the ledger. A correcting journal is used when the period is closed or the office wants corrections to be visible.

8. A compound entry: furniture bought on credit for ₹40,000 plus a ₹1,500 delivery charge, from Modern Furnishers.

Journal
LedgerDebit (₹)Credit (₹)
DrFurniture41,500
CrModern Furnishers41,500

Delivery and installation are part of the cost of the asset.

Thinking through a journal

  1. Name the two ledgers affected.
  2. Say what kind each is: asset, liability, income or expense.
  3. Say whether each has gone up or down.
  4. Apply the rule: assets and expenses increase by debit; liabilities, capital and income increase by credit.

If this is slow, practise on our neighbour site's debit and credit quiz.

Common mistakes

  • Using a Journal for a transaction that involved cash or bank.
  • Passing an adjustment and forgetting its reversal or settlement in the next period, as with outstanding salaries.
  • Unequal totals from a typing slip; Tally will not save until they agree, so look for the wrong figure, do not force a balancing line.
  • Vague narrations. For adjustments, the narration is the only explanation an auditor will have.

Questions learners ask

When is a journal voucher used in Tally?

For entries with no cash or bank: depreciation, outstanding and prepaid items, discounts, bad debts, corrections and transfers between ledgers.

What is the shortcut key for a journal voucher?

F7 on the voucher screen.

Can I use Cash in a journal voucher?

Not by default. Cash and bank transactions belong in Payment, Receipt or Contra vouchers.
