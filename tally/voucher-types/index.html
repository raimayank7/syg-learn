Voucher types in TallyPrime

Every transaction is entered through one of a small set of voucher types. Choosing the right type is half of entering it correctly.

What a voucher is

In accounting, a voucher is the document that supports an entry. In Tally, a voucher is the entry itself: a screen on which you record one transaction, with its date, the ledgers debited and credited, the amounts and a narration. To record vouchers, choose Vouchers from Gateway of Tally. Tally opens a voucher screen, and the function keys on the right change it to the type you want.

How to choose the type

Ask three questions in order.

  1. Is it a sale or a purchase of goods or services? Use Sales (F8) or Purchase (F9), whether paid at once or on credit. A return uses a Credit Note or Debit Note.
  2. If not, did cash or bank move? Money out: Payment (F5). Money in: Receipt (F6). Money only moving between your own cash and bank accounts: Contra (F4).
  3. If no money moved, it is a Journal (F7).

Parts of a voucher screen

  • Voucher type and number at the top left. Numbers run automatically for each type.
  • Date at the top right. Press F2 to change it. Tally keeps the last date used, so check it for every voucher.
  • Ledgers and amounts in the body.
  • Narration at the bottom: a line saying what the transaction was. Write one always; in a year's time nobody will remember.

Voucher modes

Several voucher types can be entered in more than one layout. Press Ctrl+H (Change Mode) on the voucher screen to see the choices. Sales and purchase vouchers can be entered as an Item Invoice (with stock items, quantities and rates), as an Accounting Invoice (ledgers only, used for services), or As Voucher (plain debit and credit lines). Payment, receipt and contra vouchers can be entered in single entry mode, where you choose the cash or bank account once and list the other ledgers, or in double entry mode with Dr and Cr lines. The accounting result is the same; the mode only changes how the screen is laid out.

The lessons that follow show each voucher as debit and credit lines, because that is the form that makes the accounting clear whichever mode your office uses.

Saving, changing and removing vouchers

  • Save: Enter through the last field, or press Ctrl+A.
  • See and change: open the Day Book from Gateway of Tally, select the voucher and press Enter.
  • Delete: Alt+D on the voucher. Cancel (keeps the number but removes the effect): Alt+X.
  • Duplicate a voucher from a report: Alt+2. Insert one: Alt+I.

Questions learners ask

Which key opens the sales voucher in TallyPrime?

F8 opens the Sales voucher and F9 the Purchase voucher. F4 is Contra, F5 Payment, F6 Receipt and F7 Journal.

Which voucher is used for depositing cash in the bank?

A Contra voucher (F4), because money only moves between your own cash and bank accounts.

What is the difference between a Payment voucher and a Journal voucher?

A Payment voucher records money going out of cash or bank. A Journal voucher records adjustments in which no cash or bank account is involved.
