Tally questions and answers

The questions beginners ask most, answered briefly, with links to the lessons.

Starting

What is TallyPrime used for?

For keeping the accounts of a business: recording sales, purchases, receipts and payments, tracking stock, and producing reports and tax statements.

Do I need to know accounting to learn Tally?

Yes, the basics: debit and credit, journal entries, ledgers and the trial balance. Tally records what you tell it; it does not decide the entry for you.

How long does it take to learn?

About a month of daily practice for day to day entries, if you know basic accounting.

Where do I get TallyPrime?

Masters

How many groups are there in Tally?

28 predefined groups: 15 primary groups and 13 sub-groups.

Under which group is a customer created?

Sundry Debtors. A supplier is created under Sundry Creditors.

Can I change the group of a ledger later?

Yes. Alter the ledger and change "Under". Its vouchers move with it.

How do I create a ledger during voucher entry?

Press Alt+C in the ledger field.

Vouchers

Which voucher for cash deposited in the bank?

Contra (F4).

Which voucher for paying a supplier?

Payment (F5), debiting the supplier.

Which voucher for a sales return?

Credit Note (Alt+F6).

How do I change the date of a voucher?

Press F2 on the voucher screen.

How do I delete a voucher?

Open it and press Alt+D. To cancel it while keeping the number, press Alt+X.

How do I see what I entered today?

Open the Day Book from Gateway of Tally.

GST, reports and data

How do I enable GST?

Press F11 and set "Enable Goods and Services Tax (GST)" to Yes, then enter the state, registration type and GSTIN.

Why is IGST being charged instead of CGST and SGST?

The party's state differs from the company's state. Check both.

Why does the Trial Balance always agree?

Because Tally will not save an unbalanced voucher. Use it to check whether the balances make sense.

How do I take a backup?

Alt+Y (Data), then Backup. Keep copies on a separate drive.

Can these lessons replace an accountant?

No. They teach how to enter and read accounts. Tax treatment and filing are for a qualified accountant.
