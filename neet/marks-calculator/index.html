NEET UG marks calculator

Enter your counts for each subject. The total follows the official scheme: +4 for each right answer, −1 for each wrong one, 0 for skipped.

How it works

For each subject the tool multiplies your right answers by 4 and subtracts one mark for each wrong answer. Physics and Chemistry have 45 questions each and Biology has 90, so the paper is out of 720. The scheme is from the NEET (UG) 2026 bulletin (read on 14 November 2026).

Using it well

  • Use it on mock papers: count right, wrong and skipped in each subject.
  • Try changing one number. Turning a wrong answer into a skipped one adds exactly 1 mark; turning it into a right one adds 5.
  • These are raw marks. Ranks come from the marks of all candidates.

See also the score planner and negative marking.

The fast attempt tries all 180 questions but gets 113 right, and its 67 wrong answers pull the total down. The careful attempt tries fewer, with few mistakes, and does better. The balanced attempt tries most questions with low errors and scores best: attempting more helps only while accuracy stays high.

Questions people ask

Does this give my rank or percentile?

No. It gives raw marks only. Ranks depend on every candidate.

Is a skipped question marked down?

No. The bulletin gives 0 marks for an unanswered question.

What if a question is found to be wrong?

The bulletin says candidates may get +4, as explained on the pattern page.
