How this set works

Ten four-option questions a round drawn from all three subjects, as in a mixed paper. Questions are made new each time. A right answer scores +4, a wrong one −1 and a skipped one 0, as in the NTA bulletin. After each answer you see the working.

A mixed round is closer to the real paper, where subjects follow one another. Notice how switching between subjects affects your speed and care.

How to review a round

A mixed round shows where you lose marks across subjects. Sort the wrong answers by subject and by kind of slip. If one subject accounts for most of the lost marks, spend your next session on it with its own set. If the slips are the same across subjects (signs, units, reading the question), the fix is a habit: write units on every line and underline what is asked.

Repeat the mixed set after a week and compare the number of wrong answers, not the score.
