NEET UG practice questions

Original four-option questions with +4 and −1 marking. Four sets: physics, chemistry, biology and mixed.

  • PhysicsTen questions, +4 and −1 marking
  • ChemistryTen questions, +4 and −1 marking
  • BiologyTen questions, +4 and −1 marking
  • Mixed: all three subjectsTen questions, +4 and −1 marking

What the sets are for

Practice builds habits that the NEET marking rewards: working carefully, writing units, checking signs, and deciding when to skip. Each round has ten questions with four options, as in the real paper.

Our questions are original and easier than the real thing. They are made by a small program, so there is no question bank to memorise, and each answer is checked by a second program when the site is built. They cover only the parts of the subjects that fit a short calculation; most of NEET biology is knowledge, diagrams and reasoning that you must study from your textbooks. Use full past papers from NTA for a true measure.

How to use them

  1. Pick a set. Start with the subject you like least.
  2. Do a round of ten without help. Skip when you are unsure.
  3. Read the working for every wrong answer and write the reason in a notebook.
  4. Repeat the set next day. Aim for fewer wrong answers, not more attempts.

Why the marking is +4 and −1

The sets use the marking of the 2026 bulletin so that skipping feels like a real choice. Play a round once answering everything and once skipping what you are unsure of, and compare the scores.
