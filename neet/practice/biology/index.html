How this set works

Ten four-option questions a round on DNA base pairing, cell division, crosses, Hardy-Weinberg, hydrogen bonds, energy flow and glycolysis. Questions are made new each time. A right answer scores +4, a wrong one −1 and a skipped one 0, as in the NTA bulletin. After each answer you see the working.

These questions practise the calculation side of biology: Chargaff's base pairing, mitosis and meiosis counts, chromatids, monohybrid crosses, Hardy-Weinberg, hydrogen bonds in DNA, the 10 per cent law and ATP from glycolysis. They are a small part of biology. The rest of the subject is knowledge and diagrams, which are better studied with notes. For the topics, see the biology syllabus guide.

What the questions test

The set draws on these topics. Each row says what is asked and the slip that most often goes with it.

How to review a round

Biology calculation questions look easy and are full of small traps: chromosomes against chromatids, a ratio used for the wrong cross, q² taken as q. After a wrong answer, write the definition or the rule you should have used (A pairs with T; each round of mitosis doubles; carriers are 2pq) and one sentence on the slip.

Most of NEET biology is knowledge and diagrams, not calculation, so use this set as a supplement to your notes and tables. See the biology study guide and the biology syllabus guide.
