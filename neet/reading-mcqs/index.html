NEET MCQ strategy: reading and eliminating

All 180 questions in NEET are multiple choice, so reading the options well is a skill of its own. This page gives habits that help. It is our own advice.

Read the stem twice

The question stem often contains a small word that changes the answer: "not", "except", "only", "first", "most". Underline it. Many wrong answers come from answering a different question than the one asked.

Predict before you look

Try to think of the answer before reading the options. If one of them matches, you can be faster and less easily tempted by a wrong option that looks familiar.

Eliminate by reason

Cross out options you can prove wrong. Look for options that contradict each other (only one of two can be right), options with absolute words ("always", "never") and options that are right facts but do not answer the question.

Match-the-following and statement questions

  • For match-the-column questions, find the pair you are surest about first; it often eliminates options at once.
  • For "statement I and statement II" questions, judge each statement alone as true or false before looking at the choices.
  • For assertion and reason, check the assertion first, then the reason, then whether the reason explains the assertion.

Numbers and units

In Physics and Chemistry, check the unit of your answer against the unit asked. If the options are close, redo the arithmetic, especially powers of ten.

Marking

Because the paper is on paper, mark each answer carefully and check, every ten questions, that the number you are marking matches the number of the question. A shift of one row can ruin a section.

Practise it

Our practice sets use four options and +4/−1 marking. Use them to practise elimination and skipping. For the rules of the real paper see the pattern page.
