NEET UG syllabus

The NTA syllabus has three subjects, with 20 units of physics, 20 of chemistry and 10 of biology. Choose a subject for the unit-by-unit guide.

Source: NTA, Information Bulletin NEET (UG) 2026, and other NTA documents on neet.nta.nic.in, read on 14 November 2026. On that day the NEET site showed documents for NEET (UG) 2026 and none for 2027, so these figures are the latest we could read. Rules can change by public notice: check neet.nta.nic.in. This site is independent and is not connected with NTA, the National Medical Commission or any coaching institute.

  • Physics20 units
  • Chemistry20 units
  • Biology10 units

What the syllabus covers

Physics runs from units and mechanics through heat, waves, electricity and magnetism to optics, modern physics, electronic devices and experimental skills. Chemistry covers physical chemistry (mole concept, atomic structure, thermodynamics, solutions, equilibrium, kinetics), inorganic chemistry (periodic trends, blocks, coordination compounds) and organic chemistry (principles, hydrocarbons, functional groups, biomolecules, practical chemistry). Biology, which is Botany and Zoology together, covers diversity, structure, cells, plant and human physiology, reproduction, genetics and evolution, human welfare, biotechnology and ecology.

A suggested order

StagePhysicsChemistryBiology
Start withUnits, kinematics, laws of motion, work and energyBasic concepts, atomic structure, periodic trendsDiversity in the living world, cell structure, structural organisation
ThenRotation, gravitation, solids and liquids, thermodynamicsBonding, thermodynamics, solutions, equilibriumPlant physiology, human physiology, reproduction
LaterElectrostatics, circuits, magnetism, induction, optics, modern physicsRedox, kinetics, p-, d- and f-block, organic chemistryGenetics and evolution, human welfare, biotechnology, ecology

This order is only a suggestion. If the order your school uses is different, follow that one and fill gaps as you find them.

Using the syllabus well

  • Study only what is listed. Textbooks contain more than the syllabus asks.
  • Tick a unit only after solving questions from it.
  • Check the NTA site for a new syllabus when the new bulletin appears; the one we read was titled for 2025.
