NEET UG twelve-week study plan

This is a twelve-week plan you can adjust. It assumes you have read each unit once, and have three to five hours on a school day and more on a holiday. It does not promise a score. Change it to fit your boards, your school schedule and your own strengths.

Weeks 1 to 4: close the gaps

  • Rate each unit in the syllabus pages: strong, fair or weak.
  • Give about two-thirds of the time to weak units, with half of the study time to Biology.
  • After each unit, do about 30 questions and note your wrong answers.
  • Once a week, take a half-length timed test of about 90 questions.

Weeks 5 to 8: speed and accuracy

  • Take a full 180-question paper every week in the afternoon hours of the real exam.
  • Spend a day on analysis: where did you lose time? Which mistakes repeat?
  • Make short daily revisions of Biology tables and Chemistry facts.
  • Use the time planner to set your minutes for each subject.

Weeks 9 to 11: sharpen

  • Two full papers a week, with a detailed review the next day.
  • Revise only from your own tables, diagrams and mistake notebook.
  • Fix your rule for skipping (see negative marking).

Week 12: calm and ready

  • One light paper early in the week. No new topics in the last three days.
  • Check the admit card, ID, route and the dress code in the bulletin and notices.
  • Sleep on schedule and eat normally.

Working backwards

Once NTA announces the date, count the weeks from today. If you have fewer than twelve, shorten the first block. If your board examinations fall in the middle, pause for them and restart; never drop board preparation for a mock test.

Tracking

Keep a table of week, paper score, accuracy and the two most frequent mistakes. After four weeks the trend is visible. If it is flat, change one thing and see what happens.
