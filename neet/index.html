What is on this site

NEET (UG) is the national entrance test conducted by the National Testing Agency for admission to undergraduate medical, dental and AYUSH courses. This site gathers what a candidate needs: the pattern and marking, the dates and what is known about the next exam, the fee table and eligibility rules, and the syllabus unit by unit.

Alongside the facts are practice sets and tools. The sets use the same +4 and −1 marking as the real paper. The calculators turn your counts into marks, a target into the number of attempts, and 180 minutes into minutes per question.

Where the facts come from

Every figure about the exam comes from NTA's Information Bulletin for NEET (UG) 2026, its syllabus document and its notices, all on neet.nta.nic.in, read on 14 November 2026. The 2027 bulletin was not out on that day. Rules and dates change by public notice, so check the NTA website before you act. Our study advice is our own and makes no promise about results.

Common questions

What is the NEET UG pattern?

The NEET (UG) 2026 paper had 180 multiple-choice questions and 720 marks: 45 in Physics, 45 in Chemistry and 90 in Biology (Botany and Zoology), in 3 hours, in pen and paper mode, per the NTA bulletin read on 14 November 2026.

How is NEET UG marked?

A correct answer scores +4, an incorrect answer −1 and an unanswered question 0.

When is NEET UG 2027?

On 14 November 2026 the NTA NEET site showed no bulletin or date for 2027. Check neet.nta.nic.in. Our dates page lists what the 2026 bulletin and notices said.

Is there an upper age limit?

The 2026 bulletin says there is no upper age limit. The minimum is 17 years completed on or before 31 December of the year of the exam.

Are your practice questions from past papers?

No. They are our own, made by a program, and are easier than real NEET questions. Use them for habit and accuracy.

Are you connected with NTA?

No. This is an independent site, not connected with NTA, the National Medical Commission or any coaching institute.
