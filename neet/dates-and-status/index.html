NEET UG dates: 2026 and what is known for 2027

This page lists the dates of NEET (UG) 2026 as the bulletin and NTA notices give them, and what the NTA website showed about NEET (UG) 2027 on the day we read it. Keep a close eye on the NTA site: the bulletin for a new year is usually published a few months before the application window opens.

Source: NTA, Information Bulletin NEET (UG) 2026, and other NTA documents on neet.nta.nic.in, read on 14 November 2026. On that day the NEET site showed documents for NEET (UG) 2026 and none for 2027, so these figures are the latest we could read. Rules can change by public notice: check neet.nta.nic.in. This site is independent and is not connected with NTA, the National Medical Commission or any coaching institute.

What happened after

NTA's site lists several later documents. A public notice of 14 July 2026 concerns the refund of the examination fee of NEET (UG) 2026 for the examination held on 3 May 2026: it says that only 11,46,401 candidates had submitted bank details by that date, that NTA had started refunding, and that others could update bank details by 31 July 2026. Other documents on the site are headed "NEET (UG) 2026 Re-Examination", including admit cards and answer keys, and an NTA press release of 16 July 2026 says the result of NEET (UG) 2026 was declared, for the examination held on 21 June 2026.

The press release says close to 20 lakh candidates appeared at 5,440 centres in 551 cities in India and 14 cities abroad, that 11.21 lakh qualified, and that the paper was held in 13 languages. We have not read an NTA statement of the reason for the re-examination, so we do not give one.

NEET (UG) 2027

On 14 November 2026 the NTA NEET site showed no bulletin or dates for NEET (UG) 2027. Until NTA publishes them, no one can give you the date, the fee or the exact form of the paper for 2027 with certainty. What is likely to stay the same, such as the syllabus topics, is on our syllabus pages, but treat that as a guide and confirm it against the new bulletin when it appears.

How to use this

  • Put the NTA site on your list of pages to check each week from the new year.
  • When a new bulletin appears, read the "dates at a glance" and "fee" boxes first, then eligibility.
  • Plan your preparation backwards from a likely May exam, but do not pay any coaching fee or any website that claims a "confirmed" 2027 date before NTA announces it.
