NCERT reading pace planner

Enter the chapters left and the days you have. The tool gives the chapters a day.

Use the chapter tracker to count chapters left across your books.

How it works

The planner divides the chapters you still have to read by the days you have. It gives the number of chapters a day and the number of days for each chapter. The sums are plain division, so the planner can only tell you how heavy the load is.

An example

Chapters leftDaysChapters a dayDays a chapter
40301.330.75
24122.00.5
10200.52.0

With 40 chapters in 30 days you need about one and a third chapters a day. If a chapter takes you a whole evening, that load is too heavy, so you would need more days or fewer chapters.

Tips

  • Plan 5 days in 6 and keep one day free for catching up.
  • Put the long chapters on days with more time.
  • Do not plan revision in the same days as first reading: plan it separately.

Questions people ask

How do I count the chapters left?

Use the chapter tracker: for each book the number of boxes you have not ticked is the number left.

What if a chapter is much longer than the others?

Count it as two. The planner only divides, so adjust the numbers to match your real load.
