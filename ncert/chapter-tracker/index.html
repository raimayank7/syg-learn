NCERT Books Guide

NCERT chapter tracker

Choose a class, subject and book, then tick chapters as you finish them.

The book list comes from the official NCERT page (read on 14 November 2026).

How it works

Choose the class, the subject and the book, then tick each file number as you finish it. Your ticks are saved in this browser only, and nothing is sent to us. The list under the tool shows every book you have ticked, with the share finished.

What the numbers mean

The boxes are the files 1 to N of the book on the NCERT page (N is the number we copied from the official list). For most books these are chapters. If a book also has an appendix or answers, they may be among the files; check the contents of the book.

Tips

  • Tick a chapter only when you have done its exercises.
  • Do not tick a chapter you have only read once if you want to revise it later: leave it unticked and note it.
  • If you change phone or browser, your ticks do not follow you. Keep a photo of the list if you need it.

Questions people ask

Is my progress private?

Yes. It stays in your browser. We cannot see it.

Why are there extra books such as exemplars?

The official list includes exemplar problem books and lab manuals along with the textbooks. They are extra practice, so tick them only if you use them.

Can I reset a book?

Untick its boxes one by one. The saved list shows only books that have at least one tick.
