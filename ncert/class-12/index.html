NCERT Class 12 books: subjects and chapters

This page lists the NCERT textbooks that the official NCERT textbook page shows for Class 12, copied on 14 November 2026. We show the English-medium books and, for language subjects (Hindi, Sanskrit, Urdu and the regional languages), the language books. The page lists 56 books in 23 subjects, with 517 file numbers in all.

The number in the third column is the last file number of the book on the NCERT page: files 1 to N after the front pages. For most books these are the chapters. In a few books some of the files may be other parts, such as an appendix, so check the contents page of the book.

How to read this list

  • Some subjects show more than one book for the same class, for example a main textbook and an exemplar or a lab manual. The exemplar and lab manual are extra books, not the main textbook.
  • For some classes the page shows both an older and a newer book for the same subject. Which of them your class uses depends on your school and the curriculum year, so ask your school before you buy or download.
  • All books are free to read on the NCERT site. This site only points to the official pages.

Core subjects for Class 12 on this list

Accountancy, Biology, Business Studies, Chemistry, Economics, English, Geography, Hindi, History, Mathematics, Physics, Political Science.

How to use a textbook (our own advice)

Read one chapter at a time. Read it once for the idea, then again with a pencil, marking terms and diagrams. Solve the exercises at the end before you look at any guide. Keep a one-page summary of each chapter: five lines of what it said and any formulas or dates. Revise the summaries before a test rather than the whole chapter.

Use the chapter tracker to tick chapters off and the pace planner to see how many chapters a day you need.
