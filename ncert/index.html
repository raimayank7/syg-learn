What is on this site

NCERT publishes the textbooks used across CBSE schools and many state schools, and many examinations are based on them. This site gathers the list of books by class and subject, points to each official book page, and adds two small tools: a chapter tracker that stays in your browser and a reading-pace planner.

Where the facts come from

The list comes from the data of the official NCERT textbook page, read on 14 November 2026. We copy no book and no chapter text. NCERT revises books, so check the official site and your school's list. Our study advice is our own and makes no promise about results.

Common questions

Where can I read NCERT textbooks?

On the official NCERT site, ncert.nic.in, which lets you read them online. This site finds the book and links to its official page.

How many books does this site list?

The list we copied from the official NCERT textbook page on 14 November 2026 has 237 books for Classes 6 to 12 (English-medium books and language books).

Does it show chapter titles?

No. NCERT loads chapter titles from the books themselves, so we show the number of chapter files only. Open the book for its contents.

Why do some subjects show two books?

The official list shows both main books and extra books such as exemplars and lab manuals, and in some classes both an older and a newer book. Ask your school which one you need.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, in your own browser only, so it does not follow you to another device. Nothing is sent to us.

Are you connected with NCERT?

No. This is an independent site, not connected with NCERT.
