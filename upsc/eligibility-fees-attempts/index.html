UPSC CSE eligibility: age, attempts, fee and vacancies

This page follows the 2026 notice. The 2027 notice is not out, so check its eligibility rules when it appears.

Nationality

For the Indian Administrative Service, the Indian Foreign Service and the Indian Police Service a candidate must be a citizen of India. For the other services a candidate may also be a subject of Nepal or Bhutan, a Tibetan refugee who came to India before 1 January 1962 to settle, or a person of Indian origin who migrated from the countries the notice lists with the intention of settling in India, with a certificate of eligibility from the Government of India in the last cases.

Age (2026 notice)

A candidate must have reached 21 years and not reached 32 years on 1 August 2026, so born between 2 August 1994 and 1 August 2005. The upper limit is relaxed as the notice says:

GroupRelaxation of the upper limit
Scheduled Caste / Scheduled TribeUp to 5 years
Other Backward Classes (eligible for reservation)Up to 3 years
Defence services personnel disabled in operationsUp to 3 years
Ex-servicemen, including commissioned officers (with 5 years' military service, released in the cases the notice names)Up to 5 years
Persons with benchmark disability (the categories in the notice)Up to 10 years

The notice adds that a candidate of SC, ST or OBC who is also an ex-serviceman or has a benchmark disability can get both relaxations.

Education

A graduate degree of a university established by an Act of the Centre or a State, or of an institution set up by an Act of Parliament or declared a deemed university, or an equivalent qualification. Candidates who have appeared in the final examination and are waiting for the result, and those who will appear, may take the written part; they must produce proof of passing by the time the notice names.

Number of attempts

CategoryAttempts
General and EWS6
OBC9
SC / STUnlimited
Persons with benchmark disability9 for General, EWS and OBC; unlimited for SC and ST

The notice states that an attempt at the Preliminary Examination counts as an attempt at the CSE, and that if a candidate actually appears in any one paper it is counted. It also says attempts are restricted by Rule 12 of the Civil Services Rules 2026.

Fee

The fee is Rs 100 for the Preliminary Examination. Candidates who are admitted to the Main Examination pay a further Rs 200 through a ten-day window after the Preliminary result. Women, SC, ST and persons with benchmark disability are exempt from both. Payment is only by net banking, Visa, Master or RuPay card or UPI, and the fee is not refunded or kept for another examination.

Vacancies

The 2026 notice said about 933 vacancies, including 33 for persons with benchmark disability (7 for blindness and low vision, 11 for deaf and hard of hearing, 8 for locomotor disability and similar, 7 for multiple disabilities), with the final number open to change.

Details to check

The notice warns that admission is provisional, that the Commission checks eligibility with original documents only after a candidate qualifies for the interview, and that a candidate cannot withdraw or change an application after submitting it. Read the Rules and the notice in full before you apply.

Source: Union Public Service Commission, Examination Notice No. 05/2026-CSE dated 4 February 2026 for the Civil Services Examination 2026, and the UPSC programme of examinations for 2027 (as on 20 May 2026), from upsc.gov.in, read on 14 November 2026. The notice for 2027 was not out on that day, so figures for 2027 are 2026 figures unless the page says otherwise. Rules can change: check upsc.gov.in and the Rules published by the Government.
