Common UPSC Civil Services mistakes and fixes

Before the examination

  • Starting without reading the notice. The notice has the scheme and the syllabus. Do not rely only on summaries, including ours.
  • Ignoring Paper II. It is only qualifying, but a score under 33% ends the attempt.
  • Treating the Preliminary Examination as a separate subject. It shares ground with the Main General Studies papers.
  • Waiting to write. The Main Examination is written, so writing practice must start early.
  • Choosing the optional on a rumour. Choose on your own base and interest.

On the application

  • Waiting for the last day. The notice says fee, photo and signature rules apply, and it does not allow changes after submission.
  • Mismatched details. Enter name and date of birth as in your documents.
  • Not carrying the photo ID. The notice asks you to bring the ID whose details you entered.

In the exam hall

  • Blind guessing at the end. Under the one-third rule it adds nothing on average.
  • Marking two answers. The notice says more than one answer counts as wrong.
  • Carrying banned items. The notice bans mobile phones (even switched off), smart watches and other devices at the venue.
  • Arriving late. The notice asks candidates to enter in time for face authentication and frisking.

How to keep a mistake from coming back

Write each mistake with its reason in a notebook and review it every week. A mistake you name is easier to stop.

Source: Union Public Service Commission, Examination Notice No. 05/2026-CSE dated 4 February 2026 for the Civil Services Examination 2026, and the UPSC programme of examinations for 2027 (as on 20 May 2026), from upsc.gov.in, read on 14 November 2026. The notice for 2027 was not out on that day, so figures for 2027 are 2026 figures unless the page says otherwise. Rules can change: check upsc.gov.in and the Rules published by the Government.
