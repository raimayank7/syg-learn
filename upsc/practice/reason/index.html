How this set works

Ten four-option questions a round on number series, direction sense, clocks, calendars, ages and odd one out. Questions are made new each time. A right answer scores +1 mark, a wrong answer −1/3 and a skipped one 0, which follows the Commission's rule of taking off one-third of a question's marks. After each answer you see the working.

What the questions test

The set draws on these topics. Each row says what is asked and the slip that most often goes with it.

TopicWhat is askedThe usual slip
Number seriesThe next number in an adding, multiplying or squares series.Checking only the first gap.
Direction senseDistance after two walks at right angles.Adding the walks instead of using Pythagoras.
ClocksThe angle between the hands at an exact hour.Giving the larger angle.
CalendarThe day of the week after some days.Counting the days instead of the remainder after sevens.
AgesAn age from the sum and the difference.Dividing the sum by 2 and stopping.
Odd one outThe number that is not a multiple of k.Checking only two of the numbers.

How to review a round

In reasoning, wrong answers often come from checking too little: one gap of a series instead of all, one step of a walk instead of both. After a wrong answer, write down what you checked and what you should also have checked.

Reasoning gets faster with a small daily dose: ten to fifteen questions a day work better than a long weekly session.
