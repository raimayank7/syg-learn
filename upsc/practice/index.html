UPSC CSAT practice

Original four-option questions with one-third negative marking. Three sets: numeracy and data, reasoning and mixed.

  • Numeracy and dataTen questions, +1 and −1/3 marking
  • ReasoningTen questions, +1 and −1/3 marking
  • Mixed: all topicsTen questions, +1 and −1/3 marking

Why only these topics?

The Preliminary Paper II lists comprehension, communication skills, reasoning, decision making, mental ability, numeracy and data interpretation. Of these, numeracy, data reading and reasoning are the ones a program can make honestly and a second program can check. Comprehension passages need real text, and decision-making questions need judgement we cannot check by machine. General Studies questions depend on facts that change, which we would not write from memory.

What the sets are for

Practice builds habits the Preliminary rules reward: working carefully, checking the unit, and deciding when to skip, because a wrong answer takes off one-third. Each round has ten questions with four options. The topics follow the numeracy and data areas of the notice: percentages, ratio, averages, interest, speed, profit, area, HCF and LCM, and reading a small table, plus number series, directions, clocks, calendars and ages for reasoning.

Our questions are original and easier than the real thing. They are made by a small program, so there is no question bank to memorise, and each answer is checked by a second program when the site is built.

How to use them

  1. Pick a set. Start with the one you like least.
  2. Do a round of ten without help. Skip when you have no basis for an answer.
  3. Read the working for every wrong answer and note the reason.
  4. Repeat the next day. Aim for fewer wrong answers, not more attempts.
