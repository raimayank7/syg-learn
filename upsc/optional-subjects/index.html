UPSC optional subjects list and how to choose one

For Papers VI and VII of the Main Examination you choose one optional subject from the list in the 2026 notice, which has 25 subjects and the literature of 23 languages. Both papers are in the same subject, each of 250 marks and three hours.

Subjects

  • Agriculture
  • Animal Husbandry and Veterinary Science
  • Anthropology
  • Botany
  • Chemistry
  • Civil Engineering
  • Commerce and Accountancy
  • Economics
  • Electrical Engineering
  • Geography
  • Geology
  • History
  • Law
  • Management
  • Mathematics
  • Mechanical Engineering
  • Medical Science
  • Philosophy
  • Physics
  • Political Science and International Relations
  • Psychology
  • Public Administration
  • Sociology
  • Statistics
  • Zoology

Literature of one language

You may instead choose the literature of any one of these languages:

Assamese, Bengali, Bodo, Dogri, Gujarati, Hindi, Kannada, Kashmiri, Konkani, Maithili, Malayalam, Manipuri, Marathi, Nepali, Odia, Punjabi, Sanskrit, Santhali, Sindhi, Tamil, Telugu, Urdu, English.

Points the notice makes

  • The papers are of conventional (essay) type and each lasts three hours.
  • The scope of the syllabus is broadly honours degree level. For Engineering, Medical Science and Law it matches a bachelor's degree.
  • The notice prints the syllabus of every optional subject, in the same document as the rest of the rules.
  • You can write the optional papers in English even if you write other papers in another language of the Eighth Schedule.

How to choose (our own advice)

  1. Start from what you already know. A subject you studied for three years needs less new learning than a new one.
  2. Read the notice's syllabus for the two or three subjects you are thinking about and count topics you have never met.
  3. Check that you can find the study material and, if you want it, a teacher or study group you can use.
  4. Look at the overlap with General Studies. Some subjects share ground with papers II to IV, which saves time.
  5. Decide before you start long preparation, and change only for a strong reason.

We do not rank subjects or say which gives higher marks, because the notice gives no such data.

Source: Union Public Service Commission, Examination Notice No. 05/2026-CSE dated 4 February 2026 for the Civil Services Examination 2026, and the UPSC programme of examinations for 2027 (as on 20 May 2026), from upsc.gov.in, read on 14 November 2026. The notice for 2027 was not out on that day, so figures for 2027 are 2026 figures unless the page says otherwise. Rules can change: check upsc.gov.in and the Rules published by the Government.
