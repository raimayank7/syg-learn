What is on this site

The Civil Services Examination is held by the Union Public Service Commission in two stages to fill posts in services such as the IAS, IFS and IPS. This site gathers the scheme and syllabus, the age, attempts and fee rules, the optional subject list, the 23 services and the dates, with plain guides to the negative marking, the CSAT paper and a preparation plan.

Alongside the facts are practice sets for the numeracy, data and reasoning topics of Paper II, and tools that turn your counts into marks, a target into attempts, and two hours into seconds per question.

Where the facts come from

Every figure about the exam comes from UPSC's Examination Notice No. 05/2026-CSE for the Civil Services Examination 2026 and UPSC's Programme of Examinations 2027, on upsc.gov.in, read on 14 November 2026. The 2027 notice was not out on that day. Rules and dates change, so check the official site before you act. Our study advice is our own and makes no promise about results.

Common questions

What is the UPSC Civil Services Exam pattern?

Two stages: a Preliminary Examination of two objective papers of 200 marks each (the second only qualifying at 33%), and a Main Examination of nine written papers (seven count: 1,750 marks) plus a 275-mark interview. Source: the 2026 notice, read on 14 November 2026.

Is there negative marking in UPSC Prelims?

Yes. One-third of the marks assigned to a question is deducted for a wrong answer, and nothing is deducted for a blank.

When is UPSC CSE 2027?

The UPSC calendar (as on 20 May 2026) lists notification on 13 January 2027, last date 2 February 2027, Prelims on 23 May 2027 and Mains from 20 August 2027, all liable to alteration. The notice was not out on 14 November 2026.

What is the age limit and number of attempts?

In the 2026 notice: 21 to 32 years on 1 August 2026, with relaxation up to 5 years for SC and ST, 3 for OBC and 10 for persons with benchmark disability; 6 attempts for General and EWS, 9 for OBC, unlimited for SC and ST.

What is the fee?

Rs 100 for the Preliminary Examination and a further Rs 200 for the Main Examination, with women, SC, ST and persons with benchmark disability exempt, per the 2026 notice.

Are you connected with UPSC?

No. This is an independent site, not connected with the Union Public Service Commission or the Government of India.
