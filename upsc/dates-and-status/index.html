UPSC CSE dates: 2026 stages and the 2027 calendar

The 2026 cycle (finished stages)

ItemWhat the notice says
NoticeExamination Notice No. 05/2026-CSE dated 4 February 2026
Last date to apply24 February 2026, 6:00 PM (online at upsconline.nic.in)
Preliminary Examination24 May 2026
VacanciesAbout 933, including 33 for persons with benchmark disability

The Commission's home page on 14 November 2026 also listed the written result of the Civil Services (Main) Examination 2026, so the written part of that cycle has finished and later stages are for the Commission to announce.

The 2027 programme

UPSC's Annual Calendar 2027 (as on 20 May 2026) gives these dates for the Civil Services Examination 2027. The calendar itself says dates are liable to alteration.

StageDate in the calendar
Notification13 January 2027
Last date to receive applications2 February 2027
Preliminary Examination23 May 2027 (Sunday), one day
Main Examination begins20 August 2027 (Friday), five days

The calendar says the Indian Forest Service (Preliminary) Examination 2027 is held through the Civil Services (Preliminary) Examination 2027, and lists the Indian Forest Service (Main) Examination from 21 November 2027 for seven days.

What to do with this

  • Treat 13 January 2027 as the day to look for the official notice, not as a promise. The notice carries the real rules, the age cut-off date and the vacancies.
  • Do not assume the age date, fee or number of attempts for 2027 is the same as 2026 until the notice is out.
  • The Commission says on the home page that all candidates appearing in UPSC examinations undergo face authentication at the venue, so carry the photo ID you used while applying.

When the admit card comes

The 2026 notice says the e-admit card is issued on the last working day of the week before the examination, and is available only on the website (nothing by post or email). Candidates who changed their scribe seven days before get it three days before the exam.

Source: Union Public Service Commission, Examination Notice No. 05/2026-CSE dated 4 February 2026 for the Civil Services Examination 2026, and the UPSC programme of examinations for 2027 (as on 20 May 2026), from upsc.gov.in, read on 14 November 2026. The notice for 2027 was not out on that day, so figures for 2027 are 2026 figures unless the page says otherwise. Rules can change: check upsc.gov.in and the Rules published by the Government.
