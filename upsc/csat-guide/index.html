UPSC CSAT Paper II guide: topics, level and routine

General Studies Paper II is the aptitude paper of the Preliminary Examination. The notice lists seven areas: comprehension; interpersonal skills including communication skills; logical reasoning and analytical ability; decision making and problem solving; general mental ability; basic numeracy (Class X level); and data interpretation (Class X level). You need 33% (66 of 200) to qualify, and the marks do not count for merit.

How the areas split in practice

The notice does not say how many questions come from each area, so we do not either. What the list tells you is the level: Class X for numeracy and data. A candidate who did mathematics only to Class X, or not for years, can reach it with steady practice.

Numeracy and data

Practise percentages, ratios, averages, speed and distance, profit and loss, areas, HCF and LCM, and reading values from a table. Our practice sets cover these at a simple level, and each answer shows its working.

Logical reasoning

Practise number series, directions, clocks, calendars, ages and odd-one-out questions. The method is to check the rule against every term, not only the first two.

Comprehension

Read editorials and passages and answer what the passage says, not what you know. Mark the line that supports your answer.

A study routine for CSAT

  1. Fifteen to thirty minutes of sums a day, with the unit written on every line.
  2. One comprehension passage a day with a timer.
  3. A weekly full paper, scored with the one-third penalty.
  4. A notebook of mistakes sorted by kind: careless, formula, concept.

Time per question

If a mock paper has 80 questions, you have 120 minutes for 80, or 90 seconds a question, before any check. Use the time planner.
