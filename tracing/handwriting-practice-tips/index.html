Handwriting practice tips for parents and teachers

These are our own suggestions for parents and teachers.

Set up well

  • A chair where the feet touch the floor and a table at elbow height.
  • Good light, from the side opposite the writing hand.
  • Paper tilted a little; left-handed children often like it tilted the other way.

Practise a little each day

Ten minutes every day does more than an hour once a week. Choose a few letters or one name each day.

Look at a few letters, not all

After a row, choose the best letter and circle it. Praise the best, and fix only one thing at a time.

Common problems

What you seeWhat may help
Letters of very different sizesUse the lined practice rows; help the child to start on the top line
Letters floating off the linePoint to the red baseline and say "letters sit on the line"
Child grips very hardShake out the hand, use a softer pencil and shorter sessions

Keep the sheets

Date a sheet once a month and keep it. Looking back at an old sheet shows a child how much they have improved.
