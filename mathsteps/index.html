Fraction calculator with steps: add, subtract, multiply, divide

Type two fractions, choose the operation, and read the working.

How to use it

  1. Type the top and bottom of the first fraction.
  2. Choose +, −, × or ÷.
  3. Type the top and bottom of the second fraction.
  4. Read the answer and the steps below. They update as you type.

A worked example

3/4 + 5/6: the common denominator of 4 and 6 is 12, so 3/4 = 9/12 and 5/6 = 10/12. Add the tops: 9 + 10 = 19, giving 19/12. It cannot be simplified, and as a mixed number it is 1 and 7/12.

What the steps show

  • For + and −, the common denominator and the changed fractions.
  • For ×, the tops multiplied and the bottoms multiplied.
  • For ÷, the second fraction turned upside down.
  • Always, simplifying by the HCF and the mixed number.

The explanations here are our own, and the answers are checked by program against separate code, but follow the method your teacher asks for in an exam. Whole numbers only, up to 1,000,000.
