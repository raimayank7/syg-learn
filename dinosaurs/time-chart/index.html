How to read the chart

  • Each bar starts at the oldest possible age for the first appearance and ends at the youngest possible age for the last appearance, as the database gives them.
  • The scale is in million years ago. Older is on the left and younger on the right; the whole chart covers the Mesozoic Era, from 251.902 to 66 million years ago.
  • The colour shows the diet the database lists. The words beside each name say the same, so you do not need the colours to read it.
  • A short bar means the dates allow only a short time. A long bar means they allow a long time. It does not mean the animal lived that long.

The chart is not a picture of a size. The database gives no sizes for these dinosaurs, which is why there is no size chart on this site.
