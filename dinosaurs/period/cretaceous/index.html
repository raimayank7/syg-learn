Cretaceous Period dinosaurs in the fossil data

The Cretaceous Period is the last period of the Mesozoic Era. It began 143.1 million years ago and ended 66 million years ago, a span of 77.1 million years, according to the time scale used in the database.

The epochs inside it

The database divides the Cretaceous Period into 2 epochs: Early Cretaceous (143.1 to 100.5), Late Cretaceous (100.5 to 66).

Dinosaurs placed here

On this site a dinosaur is placed in the period of its earliest interval. That gives 40 genera for the Cretaceous Period. A genus can continue into a later period; its page shows both ends.

The earliest interval is the name of the slice of time that holds the oldest possible start of the genus. The last column counts fossil records in the database, so a big number means the genus has been found often, not that it was bigger or better.
