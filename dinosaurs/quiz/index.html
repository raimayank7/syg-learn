Which dinosaur is it?

A ten-question quiz from the fossil database.

The quiz needs JavaScript.

How the quiz works

You get ten questions. Each has either two or four answers. Press an answer and you see at once whether it is right, then press Next. At the end you see your score. Your best score is kept on this device only.

Where the questions come from

Every question is made from a number or a word in the database, and the question asks only about something the database gives. There are five kinds:

  • Diet: which dinosaur has the diet "carnivore" or "herbivore" in the database.
  • Family: which family a dinosaur is listed in.
  • Older: which of two has the older earliest age.
  • More records: which of two has more fossil records.
  • Which dinosaur is it? three clues: diet, earliest interval and latest interval. Only one of the four answers fits all three.

The questions are about what the database says, not about what a dinosaur looked like. If you do not know an answer, open a dinosaur page or the time chart and look. When the site is built, every possible question is worked out a second time from the saved data, and a wrong answer stops the build.

Data: The Paleobiology Database (paleobiodb.org), data service version 1.2, licence CC BY 4.0, downloaded 14 November 2026. Scientists revise names, ages and groups as they learn more, so a later download of the database can differ from this page. Where the database gives no value for something, this site says "not given in the data" and does not fill it in.
