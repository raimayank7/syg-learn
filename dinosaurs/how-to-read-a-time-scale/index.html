How to read a geologic time scale

Earth's long history is cut into named slices of time, like a calendar with years, months and days. The Age of Dinosaurs is in the slice called the Mesozoic Era, which the database dates from 251.902 to 66 million years ago.

From big slices to small ones

  • Era: the biggest slice used here. Example: the Mesozoic.
  • Period: a part of an era. The Mesozoic has three: Triassic, Jurassic and Cretaceous.
  • Epoch: a part of a period, named Early, Middle or Late.
  • Age (or stage): a smaller part of an epoch. Examples: Campanian and Maastrichtian.

How to read "Ma"

"Ma" means million years ago. A bigger number is older, so 150 Ma is older than 100 Ma. That is why the time chart puts big numbers on the left.

Most scientists agree that the dinosaurs other than birds died out at about the end of the Cretaceous, which this time scale puts at 66 million years ago. Birds are living dinosaurs. This site, however, only shows what the fossil records say about each genus.
