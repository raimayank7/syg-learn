What you will find on each page

  • Family and the next group up in the database.
  • Earliest and latest interval with their ages in million years ago.
  • First and last appearance, as the database gives them.
  • Number of fossil records in the database.
  • Diet, when the database lists one.

What you will not find

You will not find a length, a height or a weight, because the database does not give one for these dinosaurs. You will not find colours, sounds or stories about how a dinosaur behaved. Scientists usually cannot tell those things from fossils alone, so we do not guess. Where the database has no diet for a genus, the page says "not given in the data".

Start with the ideas

Dinosaurs lived a very long time ago, and the numbers are big. Read how long is a million years, what a fossil is, how scientists date rocks and how to read a time scale. They make every dinosaur page easier to read.

The three periods

All the dinosaurs here are placed by the period of their earliest interval: Triassic (4 genera), Jurassic (12) and Cretaceous (40). The genus with the most fossil records among these 56 is Richardoestesia, with 239.
