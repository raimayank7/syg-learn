Dinosaur Facts

Learn about dinosaurs

These pages explain the ideas behind the numbers on the dinosaur pages. They are our own writing in plain words.

  • What is a fossil? How a bone or a footprint can last millions of years.
  • How do scientists date rocks? Layers, ash and slow clocks inside atoms.
  • How to read a time scale Eras, periods, epochs and ages, with the real dates from the database.
  • How long is a million years? Comparisons you can picture.
  • About the data Where the numbers come from and what is left out.

If you are a parent or teacher, read the pages together and then open a dinosaur page. Ask the child to find three numbers and say what each means.

Data: The Paleobiology Database (paleobiodb.org), data service version 1.2, licence CC BY 4.0, downloaded 14 November 2026. We chose and re-worded the data and wrote the explanations; the Paleobiology Database does not endorse this site. This site is independent and not affiliated with the Paleobiology Database, any museum, university or film.

Scientists revise names, ages and groups as they learn more, so a later download of the database can differ from this page. Where the database gives no value for something, this site says "not given in the data" and does not fill it in.
