Printable materials checklist

Choose experiments and print one combined list.

How to use it

  1. Tick the experiments you want to do in the box above.
  2. The list below it joins all their materials, with each thing once.
  3. Press Print this list, or print the page, and tick each thing as you find it.

Things you need for several experiments show how many. Most are ordinary kitchen and stationery items: you probably have them already. Check anything that a child might swallow before you start, and keep small things away from young children.
