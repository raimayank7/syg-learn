Multiplying by 9

Nine is one less than ten, so a times nine is a times ten minus a.

Loading...

The shortcut in three steps

  1. Write the number with a 0 after it. For 46, that is 460.
  2. Subtract the number: 460 − 46 = 414. So 46 × 9 = 414.

More worked examples

ProblemWorking
37370 − 37 = 333
85850 − 85 = 765
1241240 − 124 = 1116

Why it works

9n = 10n − n, and 10n only adds a 0.

Things to watch

The subtraction is the step to practise. For a row of nines, you can also remember that the digits of a multiple of 9 add up to a multiple of 9, but that is a check, not the method.

Check your answer

The digits of any multiple of 9 add up to a multiple of 9. For 414, 4 + 1 + 4 = 9. If they do not, the subtraction has a slip.

How to learn it

  1. Read the steps once with the example.
  2. Try the trainer for ten problems. Work each by the shortcut, then check the steps it shows.
  3. Repeat next day until you can do five in a row without looking.
