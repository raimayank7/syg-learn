What is on this site

Eight shortcuts for multiplying, squaring and subtracting in your head: squaring a number that ends in 5, multiplying by 11, multiplying numbers just below 100, multiplying by 5 and by 9, subtracting from 1000, multiplying numbers with the same tens whose units add to 10, and squaring numbers near 50. Each has a page with the steps, worked examples, the algebra behind it, and its own trainer.

How the answers are checked

Every answer a shortcut gives is recomputed by plain arithmetic when the site is built, so the trainer cannot show a wrong answer. The pages say when a shortcut does not fit.

Common questions

Is Vedic maths really ancient?

We make no claim about history. The shortcuts are ordinary algebra, and each page shows the algebra that makes it work.

Do these shortcuts work for every number?

No. Each one fits only numbers of a certain form, such as numbers ending in 5. The show-me tool tells you which shortcut, if any, fits two numbers.

How long to learn them?

One shortcut a day for ten minutes is enough for most people to learn all eight in about two weeks, then use the mixed set to keep them fresh.

Are my scores saved?

In your own browser only. Nothing is sent to us.

Are you connected with any maths course?

No. This is an independent site.
