CTET fees, eligibility and photo size

Fee for the September 2026 bulletin (22nd edition)

CategoryOnly Paper I or only Paper IIBoth Paper I and Paper II
General / OBC (NCL)Rs 1,000Rs 1,200
SC / ST / differently abled personRs 500Rs 600

The bulletin adds that GST as applicable is charged extra by the bank, payment is online only (debit card, credit card or net banking), and a fee sent by money order, demand draft or postal order is rejected. Fee once paid is not refunded or adjusted for a later test.

Eligibility

The bulletin does not list the qualifications itself. It says the minimum qualifications are those notified by the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE), and it asks you to check the NCTE website. It adds that the recruiting agency finally verifies eligibility, so being allowed to sit the test does not mean your eligibility has been checked. If you apply without being eligible, you are personally responsible.

For reserved categories the bulletin notes the NCTE relaxation of up to 5% in the qualifying marks of the minimum educational qualification.

Photo and signature

  • Both are required, in JPG or JPEG format.
  • Photograph: 10 to 100 KB, 3.5 cm wide by 4.5 cm high.
  • Signature: 3 to 30 KB, 3.5 cm long by 1.5 cm high.
  • Use a recent photograph: it is matched with the person at the centre.

Details to type exactly

Enter your name, father's name, mother's name and date of birth as they are on your Class X certificate. The bulletin says a correction window is given once, covering name, parents' names, date of birth, category, differently abled category, paper opted, subject for Paper II, languages, correspondence address and the college of your B.Ed. or diploma. The city of examination cannot be corrected, and it is allotted by CBSE at random.

One application only

If you submit more than one application, the bulletin says your candidature may be cancelled and you may be debarred from future examinations.

Help with writing

For a person with a benchmark disability the bulletin allows a scribe, reader or lab assistant and extra time of 50 minutes in the cases it describes, if the person shows the required certificate on the day. Read section 19 of the bulletin before you apply, because the conditions depend on the kind of disability.
