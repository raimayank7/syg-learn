CTET pattern and marking: Paper I and Paper II

The Central Teacher Eligibility Test (CTET) is held by the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE). The bulletin explains that the test exists because the National Council for Teacher Education (NCTE) made passing a Teacher Eligibility Test one of the minimum requirements for teaching classes I to VIII. Qualifying CTET does not give anyone a right to a job: the bulletin says in so many words that it is only one of the eligibility criteria for appointment.

Two papers

  • Paper I is for a person who wants to teach classes I to V (primary stage).
  • Paper II is for a person who wants to teach classes VI to VIII (elementary stage).
  • A person who wants to teach both levels has to take both papers.

What is the same in both papers

  • Every question is a multiple-choice question with four options, one of which is the most appropriate answer.
  • Each question carries one mark and there is no negative marking.
  • Each paper has 150 questions and lasts two and a half hours (150 minutes).
  • The main question paper is bilingual (Hindi and English).

The bulletin says the questions are based on the NCERT syllabus for classes I to V, but their difficulty and linkages could go up to the secondary stage.

Paper II: classes VI to VIII (150 questions)

In Paper II the third part depends on what you teach. A mathematics and science teacher takes the Mathematics and Science part. A social studies or social science teacher takes the Social Studies part. For any other teacher, the bulletin says either one will do.

Passing

The bulletin quotes the NCTE rule: a person who scores 60% or more in the TET is considered to have passed. Sixty per cent of 150 marks is 90 marks. See validity and passing for the rest.

Two languages

You choose two languages when you apply: Language I and Language II, which must be different. The bulletin lists 27 languages with codes; see the language list. Language I tests the proficiencies related to the medium of instruction. Language II tests the elements of language, communication and comprehension abilities.

Timing on the day

The bulletin asks candidates to report 120 minutes before the start and does not allow entry after the paper has started at the times it names. The exact shift timings for the next edition are in the dates page.
