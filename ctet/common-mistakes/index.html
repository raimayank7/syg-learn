Common CTET mistakes and how to avoid them

Before you apply

  • Wrong paper. Choose the paper that matches the classes you want to teach. See choosing between the papers.
  • Applying twice. The bulletin warns that more than one application may cancel your candidature.
  • Names that do not match. Type your name, parents' names and date of birth exactly as on your Class X certificate.
  • Photo and signature in the wrong size. Photo 10 to 100 KB, signature 3 to 30 KB, both JPG.
  • Missing a window. Dates for this edition changed several times; read the notices.

While studying

  • Only the content half. Half of each subject is pedagogy.
  • Memorising names without ideas. Know what Piaget, Kohlberg and Vygotsky each said, and how they differ.
  • Skipping the passages. Reading speed in your two languages decides the language parts.
  • No timed practice. A paper with 150 questions needs rhythm.

On the day

  • Leaving blanks. No negative marking: mark an answer for every question.
  • Late arrival. The bulletin says a candidate arriving after the stated time is not allowed in.
  • Forgetting the admit card. Download it when it appears, two days before.
  • Thinking the fee can be moved. The bulletin says fee paid is not refunded or adjusted.

How to keep a mistake from coming back

Write each mistake with its reason, and review the list weekly. A mistake you only notice comes back. A mistake you name is easier to stop.

Source: CBSE, Information Bulletin CTET-September 2026 and CBSE public notices, from ctet.nic.in, read on 14 November 2026. Notices after the bulletin changed some dates (see the dates page).
