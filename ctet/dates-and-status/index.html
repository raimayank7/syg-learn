CTET dates: 22nd edition on 12 and 13 December 2026

This page shows the dates for the 22nd edition of CTET. The information bulletin was first published for an examination on 6 September 2026, but later CBSE public notices changed the dates. The notices, in time order, are below.

Date of noticeWhat it says
11 May 2026Online applications for the 22nd edition open from 11 May to 10 June 2026 (bulletin dated for an exam on 6 September 2026; if candidate numbers rose, the bulletin said the exam could also be held on 5 September).
25 August 2026The portal is opened again from 25 August to 1 September 2026 for eligible candidates who had not applied earlier. Those who already applied need not apply again. CBSE says this follows a judgment of the Supreme Court that asks for a reasonable opportunity for eligible teachers to get the TET qualification. The revised exam date would be announced after the portal closes.
7 September 2026Online correction in particulars from 7 to 10 September 2026, also open to those who applied in the first window. No correction after 10 September.
14 September 2026The 22nd edition of CTET will be conducted on 12 and 13 December 2026. The city and date allotted to each candidate will be shown on the CTET portal 15 days before the exam, and the admit card two days before.

What this means for you

  • The exam is on 12 and 13 December 2026. Which of the two days you get is allotted by CBSE, and the bulletin says a request to change the allotted date or city will not be accepted.
  • Applications and corrections for this edition are closed on the notices we read. We cannot see your own application, so use the CTET portal.
  • Watch the portal about 15 days before the exam for your city and date, and download the admit card two days before.

The 21st edition

CBSE's August notice says 21 editions had been conducted and the result of the 21st edition was declared on 30 March 2026. The index page of ctet.nic.in also listed notices on that edition's key challenge and OMR copies. That edition is finished.

Where to look

All of this comes from ctet.nic.in, the official site. Dates have already changed once for this edition, so look at the "Public Notice" list there before you plan. If a notice we have not read has appeared, it wins over this page.

Source: CBSE, Information Bulletin CTET-September 2026 and CBSE public notices, from ctet.nic.in, read on 14 November 2026. Notices after the bulletin changed some dates (see the dates page).
