CTET mathematics practice

Original four-option questions, one mark each and no negative marking. Three sets: Paper I, Paper II and mixed.

  • Paper ITen questions, 1 mark each
  • Paper IITen questions, 1 mark each
  • Mixed: both papersTen questions, 1 mark each

Why only mathematics content?

The CTET syllabus has several parts: child development and pedagogy, languages with unseen passages, environmental studies or science or social studies, and mathematics. Questions for the language and subject parts need real passages and textbook text, and the pedagogy questions depend on exact theory and wording. We do not copy anyone's text and we do not want to teach wrong theory, so we offer only what a program can make honestly and a second program can check: mathematics content questions at the level of the classes concerned.

What the sets are for

Practice builds habits that matter in CTET: reading each question carefully, working neatly, and always choosing an answer. Each round has ten questions with four options. Paper I topics follow the content list for classes I to V (numbers, operations, time, money, measurement, patterns, geometry and data). Paper II topics follow the list for classes VI to VIII (integers, fractions, ratio, algebra, geometry, mensuration, playing with numbers and data handling).

Our questions are original and easier than the real thing. They are made by a small program, so there is no question bank to memorise, and each answer is checked by a second program when the site is built.

How to use them

  1. Pick the set for your paper.
  2. Do a round of ten without help.
  3. Read the working for every wrong answer and note the reason.
  4. Repeat the next day. Aim for fewer wrong answers.
