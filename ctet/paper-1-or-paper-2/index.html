CTET Paper I or Paper II: how to choose

The first decision in CTET is which paper to take. The bulletin ties the papers to the classes you want to teach, so the choice is not about difficulty but about your career.

If you want to teachTakeThird part
Classes I to VPaper IMathematics and Environmental Studies are both compulsory
Classes VI to VIII, maths or sciencePaper IIMathematics and Science
Classes VI to VIII, social studiesPaper IISocial Studies / Social Science
Both levelsPaper I and Paper IIThe bulletin says you must take both papers

Questions to ask yourself

  1. Which classes do I want to teach in my first job? Check the posts you plan to apply for: the recruitment notice will say which paper it asks for.
  2. Do I have the educational qualification NCTE requires for that level? The bulletin leaves this to NCTE, so read the NCTE notification.
  3. Which subject will I teach at classes VI to VIII? The third part of Paper II follows your answer.
  4. Which two languages can I read fast enough for passages in 150 minutes?

Both papers on one day or two?

The bulletin listed Paper II in the morning and Paper I in the evening of one exam day. The 22nd edition runs on 12 and 13 December 2026, and CBSE allots each candidate a date, so read your admit card for your own timings rather than guessing.

Fee when taking both

The fee for both papers (Rs 1,200 general, Rs 600 reserved) is less than twice the fee for one paper, so if you may need both, taking both in one application is cheaper than two separate attempts.

Source: CBSE, Information Bulletin CTET-September 2026 and CBSE public notices, from ctet.nic.in, read on 14 November 2026. Notices after the bulletin changed some dates (see the dates page). Planning for a school job

CTET is only one step. A school posts its own recruitment notice with the qualifications it wants, and the bulletin says the recruiting agency finally verifies eligibility. Before you spend a fee, read two or three recent notices of the schools you want and note which paper and which subject they ask for. Then choose the paper that fits most of them.
