CBSE Class 12 Physics 2026-27: units, marks, practicals

The Class XII Physics course (code 042) has a theory paper of 70 marks in 3 hours and practicals of 30 marks.

Theory: 70 marks

UnitsTopicsMarks
I and IIElectrostatics (Electric Charges and Fields; Electrostatic Potential and Capacitance); Current Electricity16
III and IVMagnetic Effects of Current and Magnetism; Electromagnetic Induction and Alternating Currents17
V and VIElectromagnetic Waves; Optics (Ray Optics and Optical Instruments; Wave Optics)18
VII and VIIIDual Nature of Radiation and Matter; Atoms and Nuclei12
IXElectronic Devices (Semiconductor Electronics)7
Total70

The curriculum groups units for marks as shown: marks are given to the groups and not to each unit separately.

Practicals: 30 marks

ItemMarks
Two experiments, one from each section7 + 7
Practical record (experiments and activities)5
One activity from any section3
Investigatory project3
Viva on experiments, activities and project5
Total30

The record has to include at least 8 experiments (four from each section), at least 6 activities (three from each section) and a project report.

Planning

Optics and Electromagnetic Waves is the heaviest group at 18, and the Magnetism and Induction group is 17. Use the planner to share your hours by these marks.

How the practical record is judged

Of the 30 practical marks, 14 are for two experiments performed in the examination (7 each), 5 for the record, 3 for an activity, 3 for the project and 5 for the viva. So 16 of the 30 marks come from work you can prepare in advance: the record, the activity, the project and the viva.

A note on method (our own advice)

Keep a table of the formulas for each unit group, with units. Practise numericals under time, and draw ray diagrams neatly with arrows.
