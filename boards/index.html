What is on this site

The CBSE curriculum sets out, for each subject, the units and marks of the Board paper and the internal assessment. This site gathers that for the core subjects of Class X and Class XII, explains how the assessment works, and turns the unit marks into a study-time plan.

Alongside the facts are Class 10 maths practice sets and tools that check the 33% line and share your study hours by marks.

Where the facts come from

Every figure comes from the CBSE Curriculum 2026-27 on cbseacademic.nic.in, read on 14 November 2026. We did not read date sheets, sample papers or marking schemes. The curriculum changes every year, so check the official site before you act. Our study advice is our own and makes no promise about results.

Common questions

What is the CBSE Class 10 board exam pattern?

A board paper of 80 marks plus 20 marks of internal assessment by the school, 100 in all for the major subjects, with at least 33% needed separately in each part and grades on a nine-point scale. Source: the CBSE Curriculum 2026-27, read on 14 November 2026.

How are Class 10 Maths marks divided?

Number Systems 6, Algebra 20, Coordinate Geometry 6, Geometry 15, Trigonometry 12, Mensuration 10, Statistics and Probability 11, making 80, plus 20 internal.

How many questions are competency-based?

The curriculum says approximately 50% of the questions in Board papers are competency-focused: case-based, source-based, integrated, data interpretation, situational and application-oriented.

What is the Class 12 Physics pattern?

A theory paper of 70 marks in 3 hours and 30 marks of practicals, with the theory marks in unit groups: Electrostatics and Current Electricity 16, Magnetism and Induction 17, Waves and Optics 18, Dual Nature, Atoms and Nuclei 12, Electronic Devices 7.

Is there negative marking?

The curriculum text we read does not mention negative marking for the Board papers. Check the sample paper of each subject.

Are you connected with CBSE?

No. This is an independent site, not connected with CBSE.
