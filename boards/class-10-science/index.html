CBSE Class 10 Science 2026-27: units and marks

The Class X Science curriculum has a board paper of 80 marks in 3 hours, with 20 marks of internal assessment, for a total of 100.

Course structure: 80 marks

UnitTopicMarks
IChemical Substances: Nature and Behaviour25
IIWorld of Living25
IIINatural Phenomena12
IVEffects of Current13
VNatural Resources5
Total80

Chapters by unit

  • Unit I: Chemical Reactions and Equations; Periodic Classification of Elements; Acids, Bases and Salts; Metals and Non-metals; Carbon and its Compounds.
  • Unit II: Life Processes; Control and Co-ordination in Animals and Plants; Reproduction; Heredity; Evolution.
  • Unit III: Natural Phenomena (light: reflection by curved surfaces, refraction, lenses and the human eye).
  • Unit IV: Effects of Current, including magnetic effects of current.
  • Unit V: Natural Resources (Our Environment).

An important note in the curriculum

The Science PDF says some topics are in the syllabus but are assessed only formatively, "without adding to summative assessments". It lists these four topics of Unit I: Periodic Classification of Elements; Acids, Bases and Salts; Metals and Non-metals; and Carbon and its Compounds. Read the PDF to see exactly what this means for your school's assessment, and ask your teacher how the school applies it.

Question types and internal assessment

  • Question types: very short answer (including objective type and assertion-reasoning), short answer, long answer, and source, case or passage-based integrated questions.
  • An internal choice of approximately 33% is provided.
  • Internal assessment of 20 marks: periodic assessment 5, multiple assessment 5, portfolio 5 and subject enrichment (practical work) 5.
