How this set works

Ten four-option questions a round on number systems and algebra, one mark each. Questions are made new each time. A right answer scores 1 mark and a wrong one 0. After each answer you see the working.

What the questions test

The set draws on these topics. Each row says what is asked and the slip that most often goes with it.

TopicWhat is askedThe usual slip
Real numbers: HCF and LCMHCF or LCM of two numbers.Giving the LCM when the HCF is asked.
Quadratic equationsThe larger or smaller root of a quadratic that factorises.Getting a sign wrong when factorising.
Quadratic equations: discriminantThe value of b² − 4ac.Forgetting the minus sign of b or c.
Arithmetic progressionsThe nth term from the first terms.Using n instead of n − 1.
Arithmetic progressions: sumThe sum of the first n terms.Mixing the two formulas for the sum.
Pair of linear equationsx or y from x + y and x − y.Adding when the equations call for subtracting.

How to review a round

After each wrong answer, redo the question without looking at the working. If you now get it right, the slip was careless: note the kind. If you get it wrong again, the method is shaky: do five similar questions from the NCERT exercises of that chapter.

Board papers carry no negative marking in the curriculum text we read, so always give an answer in practice. The working shown after each question is where the learning is.

Please note

  • These are our own questions, made by a program. They are easier than real board questions and are for practice and habit.
  • Board papers also have longer answers, proofs, graphs and case studies, which a program of this kind cannot make honestly.
  • Your best score is kept in your browser only. Nothing is sent to us.
