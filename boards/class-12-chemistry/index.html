Planning

Organic chemistry (units 6 to 9) carries 26 of the 70 marks, and Electrochemistry is the single biggest unit at 9. Practise equations and mechanisms regularly, and prepare the salt analysis well in advance since it is a full 8 marks.

The sums come from the unit table above (7 + 9 + 7, 7 + 7 and 6 + 6 + 8 + 6 + 7). Organic chemistry with biomolecules is nearly half of the paper, so it deserves steady revision of reactions and named conversions.

A note on method (our own advice)

Chemistry rewards a daily short drill: five reactions or five numerical problems a day work better than a long weekly session. Keep one page of formulas for physical chemistry and one page of conversions for organic chemistry.

Practicals in more detail

The 30 practical marks are divided as volumetric analysis 8, salt analysis 8, a content-based experiment 6, project work 4 and class record with viva 4. Salt analysis and volumetric analysis together are 16 marks, more than half, so practise them until they are routine.
