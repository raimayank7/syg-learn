CBSE Class 10 board exam pattern 2026-27: 80 + 20

The CBSE curriculum for 2026-27 sets out how Classes IX and X are assessed. This page summarises that scheme for Class X, which ends in the CBSE Board Examination.

Two parts

  • Board (external) Examination in Class X, conducted by CBSE: 80 marks for the major subjects (or the marks stated in the syllabus of the subject).
  • Internal Assessment, conducted by the school: 20 marks for the major subjects (or the marks stated in the syllabus).

The final result in a subject combines both parts, and the curriculum says students have to appear in and complete both. It states that to be declared successful in a subject a student must secure a minimum of 33% marks separately in external and internal assessments, with the words "calculated on the combined total of Internal Assessment and External Examination marks, as per the prescribed scheme". We read this as: at least 33% in the board paper and at least 33% in the internal part. The school and CBSE decide how it is applied to a result, so treat our calculator as a guide only.

What the Board Examination covers

The Board Examination in each subject covers the entire syllabus of Class X.

Grades

Grades are on a 9-point scale. CBSE puts all the passed students of a subject in rank order and awards the grades in eight equal bands of one-eighth each, from A-1 (top one-eighth of the passed candidates) through A-2, B-1, B-2, C-1, C-2, D-1 and D-2, and E for essential repeat. The curriculum notes this method is used where more than 500 candidates pass the subject, with a similar pattern for smaller subjects.

What the question papers look like

  • The curriculum says Board question papers are designed to assess application, analysis and evaluation, and that approximately 50% of the questions are competency-focused: case-based, source-based, integrated, data interpretation, situational and application-oriented.
  • Papers are structured in sections: for example Section A for objective questions and MCQs, B and C for short answers, D for long answers, and E for case studies in certain subjects, with internal choices where suitable.
  • Sample question papers and marking schemes are released in advance on cbseacademic.nic.in.

No negative marking is mentioned

The curriculum text we read describes marks for correct answers and says nothing about taking marks off for wrong answers. Check the sample paper of each subject for the rules of that paper.

Source: CBSE Academics Unit, Curriculum 2026-27, from cbseacademic.nic.in/curriculum_2027.html, read on 14 November 2026.
