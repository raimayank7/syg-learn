CBSE Class 12 Maths 2026-27: units and marks

The Class XII Mathematics paper is one paper of 80 marks in three hours, with 20 marks of internal assessment.

Course structure: 80 marks

UnitTopicMarks
IRelations and Functions8
IIAlgebra10
IIICalculus35
IVVectors and Three-Dimensional Geometry14
VLinear Programming5
VIProbability8
Total80

Calculus alone is 35 of the 80 marks, more than 40% of the paper. Relations and Functions includes inverse trigonometric functions; Algebra covers matrices and determinants.

Planning

A student short of time should not neglect any unit, but the marks suggest where the hours should go: well over a third of your time on calculus. Use the planner.

What Calculus and the other units contain

  • Relations and Functions (8 marks): types of relations, one-to-one and onto functions, and inverse trigonometric functions.
  • Algebra (10 marks): matrices, and determinants.
  • Calculus (35 marks): continuity and differentiability, applications of derivatives, integrals and differential equations.
  • Vectors and Three-Dimensional Geometry (14 marks)
  • Linear Programming (5 marks)
  • Probability (8 marks)

The unit table gives the marks; the chapter names under each unit come from the curriculum PDF, which lists them in full. Check the PDF for the exact chapter titles and any topics it removes.

A note on method (our own advice)

Practise integration and differentiation daily until the standard forms are automatic, then move to applications. Solve every exercise of the NCERT calculus chapters at least once.
