CBSE Class 10 Social Science 2026-27: marks and skills

Class X Social Science has four parts of 20 marks each in the board paper, which makes 80 marks, plus 20 marks of internal assessment.

Course structure

PartBook (NCERT)MarksNotes in the curriculum
HistoryIndia and the Contemporary World II2018 + 2 for map pointing
GeographyContemporary India II2017 + 3 for map pointing

EconomicsUnderstanding Economic Development20
Total80

The curriculum also lists Disaster Management ("Together, towards a safer India", Part III, from CBSE) among the prescribed books.

How the 80 marks are weighted by skill

Half the marks are for analysing, evaluating and creating, so learning dates and definitions is only a third of the paper. Practise answering from a source, a map or a case.

Internal assessment: 20 marks

PartWhat it isMarks
Periodic assessmentPen and paper test5
Multiple assessmentQuiz, debate, role play, viva, group discussion and similar, through an interdisciplinary project5
Subject enrichment activityProject work on Consumer Rights, Social Issues or Sustainable Development (interdisciplinary)5
PortfolioClasswork, reflections, journals and the student's achievements through the year5

Parts assessed differently

The curriculum marks a few parts differently: in History, part of "The Making of a Global World" is assessed internally through the interdisciplinary project (for 5 marks) and "The Age of Industrialisation" only in periodic assessment, and in Geography "Lifelines of National Economy" is assessed in the Board Examination by map pointing only. Using this scheme to study

Because half the marks are for analysing, evaluating and creating, practise reading a source, a map or a case and writing a clear answer. Write short paragraphs with a reason in each, and mark the main point. Learn dates and definitions, but treat them as a third of the paper, not all of it.
