Common CBSE board exam mistakes and how to avoid them

While studying

  • Skipping the course structure. It shows where the marks are.
  • Learning answers by heart. About half the questions are competency-based.
  • Leaving the internal assessment to the last week. A minimum is needed in it.
  • Ignoring formative-only topics. The Class X Science PDF says some topics are assessed only formatively; ask your teacher what that means for your school.

In the exam

  • Not reading the whole paper first. Spend two minutes on planning.
  • Writing too long on a short question. Marks tell you how much to write.
  • Leaving map work or diagrams for last. They are quick marks.
  • Not using the internal choice. About a third of the questions in Science offer a choice.

How to keep a mistake from coming back

Write each mistake with its reason in a notebook and review it every week. A mistake you name is easier to stop.

A word on comparing yourself with others

Classmates study in different ways. Compare your own tests over time, not with a friend's marks.

Keep a weekly review

Once a week, sit with your mistake notebook and ask three questions: which unit is weakest, which kind of slip repeats, and what will I do differently next week. Write each answer in one line.
