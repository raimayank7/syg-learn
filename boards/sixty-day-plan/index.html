CBSE boards sixty-day study plan

This is a sixty-day plan of our own. It is an example to adjust, not an official schedule. Count back from the date of your first paper on the date sheet CBSE publishes.

DaysFocusWhat to do
1 to 5Set upCollect the course structure of each subject, the textbooks and one sample paper. Take a baseline test in your weakest subject.
6 to 30First revisionRevise one unit a day in proportion to marks. Solve its textbook exercises. Mark weak topics.
31 to 45Second revision and applicationCase-based and data questions. One timed chapter test every two days. Fix weak topics.
46 to 55Sample papersOne full sample paper every second day, in the exam time. Mark with the marking scheme.
56 to 60CalmLight revision of formulas and diagrams. Check your admit card and stationery. Sleep well.

Each week

  • Two timed tests
  • One review of the mistake notebook
  • One rest day

If you have less time

Drop the second revision of the smallest units, not the sample papers. Practice shows you what you do not know.

A sample weekday

  1. Morning, 90 minutes: one unit from the weakest subject.
  2. Afternoon, 60 minutes: textbook exercises.
  3. Evening, 60 minutes: one timed practice set, then review.
  4. Night, 15 minutes: plan tomorrow.

Source: CBSE Academics Unit, Curriculum 2026-27, from cbseacademic.nic.in/curriculum_2027.html, read on 14 November 2026. Subject PDFs of the curriculum, not date sheets or sample papers: for the dates of the board examinations and for sample papers read the notices on cbse.gov.in and cbseacademic.nic.in. This site is independent and is not connected with CBSE.

What to do on a bad test day

A weak score on one test says little. Look at the pattern over three tests. If one unit is low every time, give it an extra session that week.

Plan for the practicals and internal marks

If your subject has practicals or internal assessment, put them in the calendar too: record work, projects and portfolios take hours that the theory plan does not count.

Checklist for the week before

  • Admit card and school ID ready
  • Stationery and instruments packed (pens, pencils, geometry box, calculator only if the instructions allow it)
  • Route to the centre known
  • Formulas, diagrams and your mistake notebook reviewed once
