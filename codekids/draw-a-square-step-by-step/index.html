Turtle Coding

Draw a square step by step

This page follows the turtle round a square. The numbers are worked out by a program, so they are exact. The turtle starts at (0, 0), facing up; going up makes the second number smaller, as on a screen.

After stepCommandPosition (x, y)Facing (degrees)
1forward 100(0, -100)0
2right 90(0, -100)90
3forward 100(100, -100)90
4right 90(100, -100)180
5forward 100(100, 0)180
6right 90(100, 0)270
7forward 100(0, 0)270
8right 90(0, 0)0

What to notice

  • Facing is 0 for up, 90 for right, 180 for down and 270 for left.
  • After the fourth forward the turtle is back at (0, 0).
  • After the fourth turn it faces 0 again, the way it started. Four turns of 90 make 360, a whole turn.

Questions to ask

What if the second turn was 60? What if forward 100 was forward 50? Predict first, then run it. Predicting is the heart of learning to code.
