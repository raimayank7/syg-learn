Rubric maker: make a printable scoring rubric

Type your criteria, choose the levels and print the rubric.

How to use it

  1. Type a title and your criteria, one on each line.
  2. Choose how many levels you want, from 2 to 6.
  3. Print the rubric. Each box under a level is for you to write what that level looks like.
  4. Mark each criterion and add up the score; the maximum is shown at the bottom.

Points

The top level is worth the most points: with four levels, Excellent is 4, Good 3, Developing 2 and Beginning 1. The maximum score is the number of criteria multiplied by the number of levels.

Read how to make a good rubric for what to write in the boxes.

The comments and advice here are our own wording, not official guidance, and they are starting points: change every comment so that it is true of the child in front of you.
