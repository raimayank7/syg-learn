Who takes it

Series V-B is a foundation-level examination on mutual funds. Its curriculum, as listed on NISM's page, includes a chapter on "Mutual Fund Products for the New Cadre of Distributors", which shows whom it was designed for: people entering distribution in a limited way. Whether V-B or V-A is the right examination for the work you intend to do is fixed by the current rules of the regulator and the industry body, and those rules have changed over the years. Confirm on NISM's page for the module and with the fund house or distributor you will work with before you enrol.

How to prepare for this paper

The paper is half the length of V-A, with the same two hours, so time is generous: over two minutes a question. There is no negative marking and the pass mark is 50%, that is 25 marks out of 50.

  • Read the V-B workbook, not the V-A one. The chapters overlap in title, but each examination is set from its own workbook.
  • Be sure of the basics: what a mutual fund is, the sponsor, trustee and asset management company, open-ended and closed-ended schemes, NAV, and the documents an investor receives.
  • Use our V-A question set for practice on the shared topics. The questions on concept, legal structure, NAV, investor services and performance apply to both; ignore those that go beyond your workbook.
  • Attempt every question. There is no penalty for a wrong answer.

Practice questions

We have not written a separate set for V-B. The Mutual Fund Distributors questions on concept, legal structure, NAV, investor services and performance cover the shared topics. Take them as a test

After passing

The certificate validity shown on NISM's page is three years. For renewal, read the FAQ on the module's own page. If you expect to distribute the full range of schemes, look at Series V-A as well, and see which exam.
