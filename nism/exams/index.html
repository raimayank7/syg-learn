How to read the table

Pass mark is a percentage of total marks: 60% of a 150-mark paper is 90 marks. Negative marking of 25% means a quarter of the marks of a question is deducted for a wrong answer: 0.25 for a one-mark question and 0.5 for a two-mark question. A question left unanswered costs nothing. The practical effect is large: in a 100-mark paper with a 60% pass mark and negative marking, a candidate with 64 correct answers and 36 wrong ones scores 55 and fails. Our page on negative marking has a calculator and explains when to leave a question.

What the table shows about difficulty

The two mutual fund examinations and the foundation examination have no negative marking. Every specialised examination we read has it. The mutual fund papers and the Securities Operations and Risk Management paper pass at 50%; the rest at 60%. Three papers, Common Derivatives and the two Investment Adviser levels, are longer: 150 marks in three hours, at double the fee. The adviser and research analyst papers include case-based questions, in which a short passage with figures is followed by several questions. None of this says how hard the subject matter is for you, but it does show where careless guessing is punished.

This is not NISM's full list

NISM conducts many more examinations than these thirteen, including those for registrars and transfer agents, compliance officers, merchant bankers, interest rate derivatives, retirement advisers, alternative investment funds and portfolio managers, and it adds and revises examinations. We listed the ones whose pages we read ourselves. For any other, and to confirm these, go to the "Certification Examinations" section of nism.ac.in. For the PMS Distributors examination, NISM's page indicated negative marking but we did not capture the exact wording, so we point you to the page instead of stating a figure.

Fees change

The fee of ₹1,003 for the Securities Markets Foundation examination was shown as effective from 1 June 2026, which is a reminder that fees are revised. Look at the figure on NISM's page on the day you enrol, and enrol only on NISM's own portal. See registration, fees and validity.

Choosing

The examination you need is decided by the work you will do and by what the regulator or your employer requires for it. Which exam should I take? groups the examinations by subject.

Questions candidates ask

Which NISM exams have no negative marking?

On the pages we read on 12 November 2026: Mutual Fund Distributors (V-A), Mutual Fund Foundation (V-B) and Securities Markets Foundation (XII).

What is the pass mark in NISM exams?

50% for some examinations and 60% for others. The table shows the figure for each of thirteen examinations.

How long is a NISM certificate valid?

Each of the thirteen pages we read showed a certificate validity of three years.
