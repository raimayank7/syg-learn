Full forms: banking, tax and money

53 full forms, each with a line on what it means.

Banking and tax papers are thick with short forms: on a cheque, a passbook, a salary slip, a loan letter, a GST bill and every payment app. This page explains the ones an ordinary account holder, employee or small business owner in India is most likely to meet, from ATM and IFSC to TDS and NACH. Each entry says what the thing is in one line. It does not give rates, limits or charges, because those change and differ from bank to bank; take them from your bank or the regulator. A word of caution runs through this subject: a PIN, CVV or OTP is never to be shared with anyone, whatever a caller says.

Explained in full: ATM GST UPI EMI KYC PAN IFSC GDP

  • ATM

    Automated Teller Machine

    A machine that lets bank customers take out cash and check balances with a card or phone, without a cashier.

    ATM explained

  • IFSC

    Indian Financial System Code

    An 11-character code that identifies a bank branch for electronic transfers in India.

    IFSC explained

  • MICR

    Magnetic Ink Character Recognition

    The technology that reads the special numbers printed at the bottom of a cheque; the 9-digit MICR code identifies the branch.

  • NEFT

    National Electronic Funds Transfer

    A Reserve Bank of India system for sending money between bank accounts, settled in batches.

  • RTGS

    Real Time Gross Settlement

    A Reserve Bank of India system for large transfers that are settled one by one, at once.

  • IMPS

    Immediate Payment Service

    An instant, round-the-clock bank transfer service run by NPCI.

  • UPI

    Unified Payments Interface

    The instant payment system of NPCI that moves money between bank accounts through a phone app.

    UPI explained

  • NPCI

    National Payments Corporation of India

    The organisation that runs retail payment systems in India, including UPI, RuPay and IMPS.

  • KYC

    Know Your Customer

    The identity and address check that banks and financial companies must do before opening an account.

    KYC explained

  • PAN

    Permanent Account Number

    The ten-character number issued by the Income Tax Department to identify a taxpayer.

    PAN explained

  • TAN

    Tax Deduction and Collection Account Number

    The number that anyone who deducts or collects tax at source must obtain.

  • GST

    Goods and Services Tax

    The tax on the supply of goods and services in India, which replaced many earlier indirect taxes in 2017.

    GST explained

  • GSTIN

    Goods and Services Tax Identification Number

    The 15-character registration number of a business under GST.

  • TDS

    Tax Deducted at Source

    Income tax that the payer cuts from a payment, such as salary or interest, and deposits with the government.

  • ITR

    Income Tax Return

    The form in which a person or business reports income and tax for a year to the Income Tax Department.

  • EMI

    Equated Monthly Instalment

    The fixed amount paid every month to repay a loan, covering both interest and principal.

    EMI explained

  • FD

    Fixed Deposit

    Money kept with a bank for a fixed period at an agreed rate of interest.

  • RD

    Recurring Deposit

    A deposit into which a fixed sum is paid every month for an agreed period.

  • PPF

    Public Provident Fund

    A long-term government savings scheme with a 15-year term.

  • EPF

    Employees' Provident Fund

    The retirement savings scheme to which an employee and employer both contribute each month.

  • EPFO

    Employees' Provident Fund Organisation

    The government body that runs the provident fund and pension schemes for employees.

  • UAN

    Universal Account Number

    The 12-digit number that stays with an employee across jobs and links all provident fund accounts.

  • NPS

    National Pension System

    A government-run, market-linked retirement savings scheme.

  • SIP

    Systematic Investment Plan

    A way of investing a fixed amount in a mutual fund at regular intervals.

  • NAV

    Net Asset Value

    The price of one unit of a mutual fund scheme.

  • IPO

    Initial Public Offering

    The first sale of a company's shares to the public.

  • NSE

    National Stock Exchange

    One of the two main stock exchanges of India, based in Mumbai.

  • BSE

    Bombay Stock Exchange

    The older of India's two main stock exchanges; its official name today is BSE Limited.

  • SEBI

    Securities and Exchange Board of India

    The regulator of the securities market in India.

  • RBI

    Reserve Bank of India

    India's central bank, which issues currency and regulates banks.

  • SBI

    State Bank of India

    The largest public sector bank in India.

  • NABARD

    National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development

    The apex development bank for agriculture and rural credit in India.

  • NBFC

    Non-Banking Financial Company

    A company that gives loans or other financial services without being a bank.

  • LIC

    Life Insurance Corporation of India

    The state-owned life insurance company.

  • IRDAI

    Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India

    The regulator of insurance in India.

  • CVV

    Card Verification Value

    The three-digit security number on the back of a debit or credit card. Never share it.

  • POS

    Point of Sale

    The place or machine where a customer pays, such as a card swipe machine.

  • CIBIL

    Credit Information Bureau (India) Limited

    The original name of India's first credit bureau, now TransUnion CIBIL; its score is widely used by lenders.

  • UTR

    Unique Transaction Reference

    The reference number that identifies a bank transfer.

  • DD

    Demand Draft

    A prepaid payment instrument issued by a bank, payable to a named party.

  • MSME

    Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises

    The official grouping of smaller businesses, defined by investment and turnover.

  • HUF

    Hindu Undivided Family

    A family treated as a separate taxpayer under Indian income tax law.

  • CASA

    Current Account Savings Account

    A bank's current and savings deposits taken together.

  • NRI

    Non-Resident Indian

    An Indian citizen who lives outside India.

  • MRP

    Maximum Retail Price

    The highest price, including taxes, at which a packaged product may be sold in India.

  • COD

    Cash on Delivery

    Paying for an online order when it arrives.

  • GDP

    Gross Domestic Product

    The total value of goods and services produced in a country in a period.

    GDP explained

  • FDI

    Foreign Direct Investment

    Investment by a foreign company or person in a business in another country.

  • AEPS

    Aadhaar Enabled Payment System

    A system that lets people use basic banking with their Aadhaar number and fingerprint.

  • DBT

    Direct Benefit Transfer

    The government's method of paying subsidies and benefits straight into bank accounts.

  • BHIM

    Bharat Interface for Money

    The UPI payment app developed by NPCI.

  • ECS

    Electronic Clearing Service

    An older system for repeated payments such as bills and salaries, now largely replaced by NACH.

  • NACH

    National Automated Clearing House

    The NPCI system that handles repeated payments such as EMIs and SIPs.

How to read an entry

The letters in bold are the short form as it is normally written. Under it is the full form, and then a line of meaning in plain words. Where the same letters mean something else in another field, the entry mentions it. "Copy" puts the short form, the full form and the meaning on your clipboard, ready to paste into notes or a message.
