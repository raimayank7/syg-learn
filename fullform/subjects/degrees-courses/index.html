Full forms: degrees and courses

33 full forms, each with a line on what it means.

Degree names are almost always written as short forms, on certificates, visiting cards, name plates and job advertisements. Many come from Latin, which is why the letters do not always match the English words: LLB has two Ls because the Latin plural "laws" doubles the letter, and MBBS follows the Latin order of words. This page covers the common first degrees, master's degrees, research degrees and professional qualifications in India. Some short forms change meaning with the field: MD is a medical degree in a hospital and a managing director in a company; MS is a surgical degree in India and a master of science abroad. Write degree abbreviations in capitals without full stops, after the name, with the highest or most relevant first.

Explained in full: MBBS PhD

  • BA

    Bachelor of Arts

    An undergraduate degree in subjects such as languages, history, political science and economics, usually of three years.

  • BSc

    Bachelor of Science

    An undergraduate degree in science subjects such as physics, chemistry, mathematics or biology.

  • BCom

    Bachelor of Commerce

    An undergraduate degree in commerce subjects such as accountancy, business studies and economics.

  • BTech

    Bachelor of Technology

    A four-year undergraduate degree in engineering.

  • BE

    Bachelor of Engineering

    A four-year undergraduate engineering degree, equivalent to B.Tech.

  • BBA

    Bachelor of Business Administration

    An undergraduate degree in business and management.

  • BCA

    Bachelor of Computer Applications

    An undergraduate degree in computer applications and software.

  • BEd

    Bachelor of Education

    The professional degree required to teach in schools.

  • MBBS

    Bachelor of Medicine, Bachelor of Surgery

    The basic degree that qualifies a person as a doctor. The letters come from the Latin name, Medicinae Baccalaureus, Baccalaureus Chirurgiae.

    MBBS explained

  • BDS

    Bachelor of Dental Surgery

    The undergraduate degree that qualifies a person as a dentist.

  • BAMS

    Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery

    The undergraduate degree in Ayurveda.

  • BHMS

    Bachelor of Homoeopathic Medicine and Surgery

    The undergraduate degree in homoeopathy.

  • LLB

    Bachelor of Laws

    The undergraduate law degree. The letters come from the Latin Legum Baccalaureus; the double L marks the plural, "laws".

  • LLM

    Master of Laws

    The postgraduate law degree, from the Latin Legum Magister.

  • MA

    Master of Arts

    A postgraduate degree in arts and humanities subjects.

  • MSc

    Master of Science

    A postgraduate degree in a science subject.

  • MCom

    Master of Commerce

    A postgraduate degree in commerce.

  • MTech

    Master of Technology

    A postgraduate degree in engineering.

  • MBA

    Master of Business Administration

    A postgraduate degree in business management.

  • MCA

    Master of Computer Applications

    A postgraduate degree in computer applications.

  • MD

    Doctor of Medicine

    A postgraduate medical degree in India, taken after MBBS. In business, MD also means Managing Director.

  • MS

    Master of Surgery

    A postgraduate surgical degree in India. Abroad, MS usually means Master of Science.

  • PhD

    Doctor of Philosophy

    The highest university degree, awarded for original research in any subject. From the Latin Philosophiae Doctor.

    PhD explained

  • MPhil

    Master of Philosophy

    A research degree between a master's degree and a PhD.

  • CA

    Chartered Accountant

    A qualified accountant who is a member of the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI).

  • CS

    Company Secretary

    A professional qualified through the Institute of Company Secretaries of India, responsible for a company's legal compliance.

  • CMA

    Cost and Management Accountant

    A professional qualified through the Institute of Cost Accountants of India.

  • UG

    Undergraduate

    A student studying for a first degree, or the course itself.

  • PG

    Postgraduate

    A student or course after the first degree. In everyday Indian use PG also means paying guest.

  • DPharm

    Diploma in Pharmacy

    A two-year diploma that qualifies a person to work as a pharmacist.

  • BPharm

    Bachelor of Pharmacy

    The undergraduate degree in pharmacy.

  • ANM

    Auxiliary Nurse Midwife

    A trained village-level health worker, and the diploma course that qualifies her.

  • GNM

    General Nursing and Midwifery

    A diploma course in nursing.

How to read an entry

The letters in bold are the short form as it is normally written. Under it is the full form, and then a line of meaning in plain words. Where the same letters mean something else in another field, the entry mentions it. "Copy" puts the short form, the full form and the meaning on your clipboard, ready to paste into notes or a message.
