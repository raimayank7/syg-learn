Full forms: world bodies, countries and sport

33 full forms, each with a line on what it means.

International organisations are known almost entirely by their initials, and questions on them are a fixture of school general knowledge and of competitive examinations. This page covers the United Nations and its agencies, the main regional groupings, the best-known foreign agencies, and the governing bodies and terms of cricket, football and the Olympic movement, along with the short forms of world time. Some of these are said as words, NATO, UNESCO, FIFA, and some letter by letter, WHO, IMF, EU. A few keep old letters after a change of name: UNICEF still carries the I and E of "International" and "Emergency" from its original title.

  • UN

    United Nations

    The international organisation of countries founded in 1945 to keep peace and promote cooperation.

  • UNO

    United Nations Organisation

    An older name for the United Nations, still common in Indian textbooks.

  • UNESCO

    United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization

    The UN agency for education, science and culture, known for its list of World Heritage Sites.

  • UNICEF

    United Nations Children's Fund

    The UN agency for children. It keeps the letters of its original name, United Nations International Children's Emergency Fund.

  • WHO

    World Health Organization

    The UN agency for international public health, based in Geneva.

  • WTO

    World Trade Organization

    The organisation that deals with the rules of trade between countries.

  • IMF

    International Monetary Fund

    The organisation that works for monetary stability and lends to countries in financial difficulty.

  • ILO

    International Labour Organization

    The UN agency that sets international labour standards.

  • FAO

    Food and Agriculture Organization

    The UN agency that leads efforts to defeat hunger.

  • NATO

    North Atlantic Treaty Organization

    A military alliance of North American and European countries, founded in 1949.

  • SAARC

    South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation

    The regional organisation of South Asian countries, founded in 1985.

  • ASEAN

    Association of Southeast Asian Nations

    The regional organisation of Southeast Asian countries.

  • BRICS

    Brazil, Russia, India, China, South Africa

    A grouping of major emerging economies, named after its first five members.

  • EU

    European Union

    The political and economic union of European countries.

  • OPEC

    Organization of the Petroleum Exporting Countries

    A group of oil-producing countries that coordinates petroleum policy.

  • NASA

    National Aeronautics and Space Administration

    The space agency of the United States.

  • ESA

    European Space Agency

    The space agency of European countries.

  • FBI

    Federal Bureau of Investigation

    The main federal investigating agency of the United States.

  • CIA

    Central Intelligence Agency

    The foreign intelligence agency of the United States.

  • USA

    United States of America

    The country of 50 states in North America.

  • UK

    United Kingdom

    The United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland.

  • UAE

    United Arab Emirates

    A country of seven emirates on the Arabian Peninsula, including Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

  • ICC

    International Cricket Council

    The world governing body of cricket.

  • BCCI

    Board of Control for Cricket in India

    The governing body of cricket in India.

  • IPL

    Indian Premier League

    The Twenty20 cricket league of India, first played in 2008.

  • FIFA

    Fédération Internationale de Football Association

    The world governing body of football. The name is French for International Federation of Association Football.

  • IOC

    International Olympic Committee

    The body that organises the Olympic Games. In India, IOC is also Indian Oil Corporation.

  • ODI

    One Day International

    A cricket match between national teams of 50 overs a side.

  • LBW

    Leg Before Wicket

    A way of being out in cricket, when the ball hits the batter's leg and would have hit the stumps.

  • ISO

    International Organization for Standardization

    The body that publishes international standards. "ISO" is a chosen short name, from the Greek isos, equal, not initials.

  • UTC

    Coordinated Universal Time

    The world's time standard, from which all time zones are counted.

  • GMT

    Greenwich Mean Time

    The time at the Greenwich meridian in London, the older base for world time.

  • IST

    Indian Standard Time

    The time used across India, five and a half hours ahead of UTC.

How to read an entry

Where the same letters mean something else in another field, the entry mentions it.
