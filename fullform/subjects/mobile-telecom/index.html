Full forms: mobile and telecom

27 full forms, each with a line on what it means.

These are the short forms printed on SIM packs, phone boxes, recharge plans and telephone bills, and the ones that appear in your phone's settings. Knowing them is practical. If you know that an OTP is a one time password meant only for you, a fraudster's call asking for it sounds wrong at once. If you know the IMEI is the identity of your handset, you know what to give the police and your operator when a phone is lost. The page also covers the generations of mobile networks, radio terms and the older telephone words, STD, ISD and PCO, that still appear in general knowledge papers.

Explained in full: OTP SIM GPS

  • SIM

    Subscriber Identity Module

    The small card, or built-in chip, that identifies your mobile connection to the network.

    SIM explained

  • eSIM

    Embedded Subscriber Identity Module

    A SIM built into the phone that is set up by software, with no card to insert.

  • SMS

    Short Message Service

    The ordinary text message service of mobile phones.

  • MMS

    Multimedia Messaging Service

    A message service that can carry pictures and sound over the mobile network.

  • OTP

    One Time Password

    A code sent to your phone or email that works only once, used to confirm a login or payment. Never share it.

    OTP explained

  • GSM

    Global System for Mobile Communications

    The mobile phone standard used by most of the world for second-generation networks.

  • CDMA

    Code Division Multiple Access

    A mobile network technology that was an alternative to GSM.

  • LTE

    Long Term Evolution

    The technology behind 4G mobile networks.

  • VoLTE

    Voice over LTE

    Phone calls carried over the 4G data network, giving clearer sound.

  • 5G

    Fifth Generation

    The fifth generation of mobile network technology, faster than 4G.

  • IMEI

    International Mobile Equipment Identity

    The unique 15-digit number of a phone handset. Dial *#06# to see it.

  • GPS

    Global Positioning System

    The satellite system of the United States that lets a device find its position anywhere on Earth.

    GPS explained

  • NavIC

    Navigation with Indian Constellation

    India's own regional satellite navigation system, run by ISRO.

  • STD

    Subscriber Trunk Dialling

    Direct dialling of long-distance calls within the country, using an area code.

  • ISD

    International Subscriber Dialling

    Direct dialling of calls to other countries, using a country code.

  • PCO

    Public Call Office

    A booth or shop with a public telephone, once common across India.

  • TRAI

    Telecom Regulatory Authority of India

    The regulator of telephone, internet and broadcasting services in India.

  • DND

    Do Not Disturb

    A setting or registration that blocks unwanted calls, messages or notifications.

  • USSD

    Unstructured Supplementary Service Data

    The system behind short codes such as *123#, which work without internet.

  • NFC

    Near Field Communication

    Very short-range wireless used for tap-to-pay cards and phones.

  • BSNL

    Bharat Sanchar Nigam Limited

    The government-owned telecom company of India.

  • DTH

    Direct to Home

    Television delivered straight from a satellite to a small dish at the viewer's home.

  • FM

    Frequency Modulation

    A way of sending sound by radio that gives clear reception; FM radio uses it.

  • AM

    Amplitude Modulation

    An older way of sending sound by radio. In clock time, AM means ante meridiem.

  • PIN

    Personal Identification Number

    A secret number for a card, phone or account. In Indian addresses, PIN means Postal Index Number.

  • APK

    Android Package Kit

    The file format of an Android app. Install apps only from trusted stores.

  • App

    Application

    A program for a phone or computer. A short form, not an abbreviation of several words.

How to read an entry

The letters in bold are the short form as it is normally written. Under it is the full form, and then a line of meaning in plain words. Where the same letters mean something else in another field, the entry mentions it.
