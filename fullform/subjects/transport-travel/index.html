Full forms: transport and travel

24 full forms, each with a line on what it means.

Vehicle papers, railway tickets and road signs have a language of their own. This page explains the short forms on a driving licence and registration certificate, on an Indian Railways ticket and its status messages, on highways and at airports, and the terms used to describe a car or motorcycle. Understanding a ticket status alone makes the page worth a look: RAC and WL mean very different things for your journey. For the registration code of any Indian district, such as PB 10 or MH 12, see our sister site RTO Codes.

  • RTO

    Regional Transport Office

    The government office that registers vehicles and issues driving licences.

  • DL

    Driving Licence

    The official permit to drive a motor vehicle. DL is also the vehicle registration code of Delhi.

  • LL

    Learner's Licence

    The temporary licence that allows a person to learn to drive.

  • RC

    Registration Certificate

    The document that shows a vehicle is registered.

  • PUC

    Pollution Under Control

    The certificate that a vehicle's exhaust is within the permitted limits.

  • HSRP

    High Security Registration Plate

    The tamper-proof number plate now required on vehicles in India.

  • PNR

    Passenger Name Record

    The 10-digit number on an Indian railway ticket that identifies the booking.

  • IRCTC

    Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation

    The railway company that runs online ticket booking and catering.

  • RAC

    Reservation Against Cancellation

    A railway ticket status that guarantees a seat and may become a berth if others cancel.

  • WL

    Waiting List

    A ticket status that means no seat is confirmed yet.

  • TTE

    Travelling Ticket Examiner

    The railway official who checks tickets on the train.

  • EMU

    Electric Multiple Unit

    A self-propelled electric train, such as a suburban local.

  • NH

    National Highway

    A main road maintained by the central government.

  • NHAI

    National Highways Authority of India

    The authority that builds and manages national highways.

  • ETA

    Estimated Time of Arrival

    The time at which a vehicle or person is expected to arrive.

  • ATC

    Air Traffic Control

    The service that directs aircraft in the air and on the ground.

  • IATA

    International Air Transport Association

    The trade body of airlines, which also assigns three-letter airport codes.

  • SUV

    Sport Utility Vehicle

    A car with a high body and ground clearance.

  • ABS

    Anti-lock Braking System

    A system that stops the wheels locking under hard braking.

  • CC

    Cubic Centimetres

    The measure of engine size, as in a 150 cc motorcycle.

  • BHP

    Brake Horsepower

    A measure of the power of an engine.

  • KMPL

    Kilometres per Litre

    A measure of how far a vehicle travels on one litre of fuel.

  • FASTag

    no full form

    FASTag is the brand name of India's electronic toll tag. It is a name, not an abbreviation.

  • OCI

    Overseas Citizen of India

    A status that lets people of Indian origin with foreign citizenship live and work in India.

How to read an entry

The letters in bold are the short form as it is normally written. Under it is the full form, and then a line of meaning in plain words. Where the same letters mean something else in another field, the entry mentions it. "Copy" puts the short form, the full form and the meaning on your clipboard, ready to paste into notes or a message.
