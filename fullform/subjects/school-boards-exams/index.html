Full forms: school, boards and exams

47 full forms, each with a line on what it means.

These are the short forms a student in India meets from the first day of school to the last entrance examination: the boards that set the syllabus, the bodies that write textbooks and hold tests, the examinations themselves, and the everyday words of report cards and admission forms. Several of them look alike and are easy to confuse. CBSE is a board that holds examinations, while NCERT writes textbooks. SSC is a class 10 certificate in some states and also a central recruiting body. Each entry gives the full form and a line on what it is. Rules, dates and syllabuses change every year, so use these pages to understand a name, and go to the body's own website for anything you must act on.

Explained in full: CBSE NEET CGPA

  • CBSE

    Central Board of Secondary Education

    The national school board of India that sets the syllabus and holds the class 10 and class 12 board examinations for its affiliated schools.

    CBSE explained

  • ICSE

    Indian Certificate of Secondary Education

    The class 10 examination conducted by the Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations (CISCE).

  • ISC

    Indian School Certificate

    The class 12 examination of the CISCE board, taken two years after ICSE.

  • CISCE

    Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations

    The private national board that conducts the ICSE (class 10) and ISC (class 12) examinations.

  • NCERT

    National Council of Educational Research and Training

    The government body that prepares model school textbooks and the national curriculum framework; CBSE schools mostly use its books.

  • SCERT

    State Council of Educational Research and Training

    The state-level counterpart of NCERT, which prepares textbooks and teacher training for state board schools.

  • NIOS

    National Institute of Open Schooling

    The national open school board that lets learners study and take class 10 and 12 examinations without attending regular school.

  • UGC

    University Grants Commission

    The statutory body that recognises universities in India, sets standards for higher education and gives grants.

  • AICTE

    All India Council for Technical Education

    The national council that regulates technical education such as engineering, management and pharmacy courses.

  • NTA

    National Testing Agency

    The agency set up by the Government of India to conduct entrance examinations such as JEE Main, NEET UG, CUET and UGC NET.

  • JEE

    Joint Entrance Examination

    The national engineering entrance test. JEE Main is for NITs and other colleges; JEE Advanced is for admission to the IITs.

  • NEET

    National Eligibility cum Entrance Test

    The single national entrance examination for MBBS, BDS and other medical courses in India.

    NEET explained

  • CUET

    Common University Entrance Test

    A common entrance test for admission to undergraduate and postgraduate courses in central and other participating universities.

  • NET

    National Eligibility Test

    The examination that decides eligibility for assistant professor posts and research fellowships in Indian universities.

  • GATE

    Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering

    A national examination in engineering and science subjects, used for M.Tech admissions and recruitment by public sector companies.

  • CAT

    Common Admission Test

    The entrance test conducted by the IIMs for admission to their management programmes; many other institutes accept its score.

  • CLAT

    Common Law Admission Test

    The entrance examination for admission to the national law universities.

  • IIT

    Indian Institute of Technology

    One of a group of autonomous public engineering and technology institutes, declared institutes of national importance.

  • IIM

    Indian Institute of Management

    One of a group of autonomous public business schools offering postgraduate management education.

  • NIT

    National Institute of Technology

    One of a group of centrally funded engineering institutes found in most states of India.

  • AIIMS

    All India Institute of Medical Sciences

    A group of autonomous public medical colleges and hospitals; the first was set up in New Delhi in 1956.

  • IGNOU

    Indira Gandhi National Open University

    The central open university that offers degrees and diplomas through distance learning.

  • ITI

    Industrial Training Institute

    An institute that gives vocational training in trades such as electrician, fitter and welder, usually after class 8 or 10.

  • NCC

    National Cadet Corps

    The youth wing of the Indian armed forces, open to school and college students, which trains them in drill, discipline and service.

  • NSS

    National Service Scheme

    A government scheme through which college and senior school students do voluntary community service.

  • PTM

    Parent Teacher Meeting

    A meeting at school where parents and teachers talk about a child's progress.

  • PTA

    Parent Teacher Association

    A body of parents and teachers of a school that works together on matters affecting the students.

  • GPA

    Grade Point Average

    The average of the grade points earned in the subjects of one term or semester.

  • CGPA

    Cumulative Grade Point Average

    The average of grade points over all terms or all subjects, used by boards and universities in place of a percentage.

    CGPA explained

  • SGPA

    Semester Grade Point Average

    The grade point average for a single semester.

  • MCQ

    Multiple Choice Question

    A question that offers several answers, of which the candidate picks the correct one.

  • OMR

    Optical Mark Recognition

    The technology that reads the bubbles darkened on an answer sheet; the sheet itself is commonly called an OMR sheet.

  • TC

    Transfer Certificate

    A certificate issued by a school or college when a student leaves, needed for admission elsewhere.

  • KG

    Kindergarten

    The pre-school classes, lower and upper (LKG and UKG), before class 1. The word is German for "children's garden".

  • LKG

    Lower Kindergarten

    The first of the two kindergarten years, usually for children of about four.

  • UKG

    Upper Kindergarten

    The second kindergarten year, just before class 1.

  • RTE

    Right to Education

    Short for the Right of Children to Free and Compulsory Education Act, 2009, which gives children aged 6 to 14 a right to schooling.

  • TET

    Teacher Eligibility Test

    A qualifying test for those who want to teach classes 1 to 8; the central version is called CTET.

  • CTET

    Central Teacher Eligibility Test

    The teacher eligibility test conducted by CBSE for central government and other schools.

  • SSC

    Secondary School Certificate

    The class 10 public examination in several state boards. The same letters also stand for the Staff Selection Commission.

  • HSC

    Higher Secondary Certificate

    The class 12 public examination in several state boards.

  • SSLC

    Secondary School Leaving Certificate

    The class 10 certificate in states such as Karnataka, Kerala and Tamil Nadu.

  • PCM

    Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics

    The subject group taken in classes 11 and 12 by students aiming at engineering.

  • PCB

    Physics, Chemistry, Biology

    The subject group taken in classes 11 and 12 by students aiming at medicine.

  • GK

    General Knowledge

    Facts about the world, current affairs and everyday things, tested in school quizzes and competitive examinations.

  • IQ

    Intelligence Quotient

    A score from standard tests meant to measure reasoning ability compared with others of the same age.

  • ISBN

    International Standard Book Number

    A unique 13-digit number printed on a book to identify its edition and publisher.

How to read an entry

Where the same letters mean something else in another field, the entry mentions it.
