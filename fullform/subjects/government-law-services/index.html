Full forms: government, law and services

75 full forms, each with a line on what it means.

Newspapers, government forms and competitive examination papers assume that the reader knows these short forms: the commissions that recruit, the services and ranks, the investigating agencies, the forces, the offices of a district, and the terms of law and welfare schemes. This page is useful both for general knowledge preparation and for making sense of an official letter. Some laws were replaced recently. The Indian Penal Code and the Code of Criminal Procedure gave way to new codes on 1 July 2024; the entries note this, because old and new names will both be met for years. Several short forms here have a second meaning in another field, which the entry mentions.

Explained in full: UPSC IAS ISRO FIR NGO LPG

  • UPSC

    Union Public Service Commission

    The constitutional body that holds the Civil Services Examination and other recruitment for the central government.

    UPSC explained

  • SSC

    Staff Selection Commission

    The body that recruits staff for central government departments through examinations such as CGL and CHSL.

  • IAS

    Indian Administrative Service

    The top administrative service of India; its officers serve as collectors, secretaries and heads of departments.

    IAS explained

  • IPS

    Indian Police Service

    The all-India service whose officers lead the police forces of the states and the central police organisations.

  • IFS

    Indian Foreign Service

    The diplomatic service of India. The same letters are also used for the Indian Forest Service.

  • IRS

    Indian Revenue Service

    The service that administers income tax and customs and indirect taxes.

  • PSC

    Public Service Commission

    The body that recruits officers for a state government; each state has its own.

  • CGL

    Combined Graduate Level

    The Staff Selection Commission examination for graduate-level posts in central government offices.

  • CHSL

    Combined Higher Secondary Level

    The Staff Selection Commission examination for posts open to those who have passed class 12.

  • RRB

    Railway Recruitment Board

    One of the boards that recruit staff for Indian Railways.

  • IBPS

    Institute of Banking Personnel Selection

    The body that holds common recruitment examinations for public sector banks.

  • PO

    Probationary Officer

    The entry-level officer post in a bank. PO is also short for Post Office.

  • ISRO

    Indian Space Research Organisation

    India's national space agency, which builds and launches satellites and space missions.

    ISRO explained

  • DRDO

    Defence Research and Development Organisation

    The government agency that develops military technology for India.

  • BARC

    Bhabha Atomic Research Centre

    India's main nuclear research centre, in Mumbai.

  • CSIR

    Council of Scientific and Industrial Research

    A national network of research laboratories.

  • ICMR

    Indian Council of Medical Research

    The apex body for biomedical research in India.

  • ICAR

    Indian Council of Agricultural Research

    The apex body for agricultural research and education in India.

  • NITI

    National Institution for Transforming India

    As in NITI Aayog, the policy think tank of the Government of India that replaced the Planning Commission in 2015.

  • CAG

    Comptroller and Auditor General

    The constitutional authority that audits the accounts of the central and state governments.

  • ECI

    Election Commission of India

    The constitutional body that conducts elections to Parliament and the state legislatures.

  • EVM

    Electronic Voting Machine

    The machine on which votes are cast in Indian elections.

  • VVPAT

    Voter Verifiable Paper Audit Trail

    The printer attached to a voting machine that shows the voter a slip of the vote cast.

  • EPIC

    Electors Photo Identity Card

    The official name of the voter ID card.

  • UIDAI

    Unique Identification Authority of India

    The authority that issues Aadhaar numbers.

  • NRC

    National Register of Citizens

    A register of Indian citizens.

  • RTI

    Right to Information

    The right of citizens, under the Right to Information Act, 2005, to ask public authorities for information.

  • PIL

    Public Interest Litigation

    A court case filed for the benefit of the public, not for a private grievance.

  • FIR

    First Information Report

    The first written record made by the police when they are told of a cognisable offence.

    FIR explained

  • IPC

    Indian Penal Code

    The criminal code of India from 1860 until it was replaced by the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita in 2024.

  • BNS

    Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita

    The criminal code of India that replaced the Indian Penal Code on 1 July 2024.

  • CrPC

    Code of Criminal Procedure

    The law on criminal procedure, replaced in 2024 by the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita.

  • CBI

    Central Bureau of Investigation

    The main investigating agency of the central government.

  • CID

    Crime Investigation Department

    The branch of a state police force that investigates serious crimes.

  • ED

    Enforcement Directorate

    The central agency that investigates money laundering and foreign exchange offences. Its full name is Directorate of Enforcement.

  • NIA

    National Investigation Agency

    The central agency that investigates terrorism-related offences.

  • RAW

    Research and Analysis Wing

    India's external intelligence agency.

  • IB

    Intelligence Bureau

    India's internal intelligence agency.

  • MLA

    Member of the Legislative Assembly

    An elected member of a state's legislative assembly.

  • MLC

    Member of the Legislative Council

    A member of the upper house of a state legislature, in the states that have one.

  • MP

    Member of Parliament

    A member of the Lok Sabha or the Rajya Sabha. MP is also the short form of Madhya Pradesh.

  • PM

    Prime Minister

    The head of the government of India. In clock time, PM means post meridiem.

  • CM

    Chief Minister

    The head of the government of a state. In length, cm means centimetre.

  • DM

    District Magistrate

    The senior officer in charge of a district, also called the collector. On social media, DM means direct message.

  • SDM

    Sub-Divisional Magistrate

    The officer in charge of a sub-division of a district.

  • BDO

    Block Development Officer

    The officer in charge of development work in a block.

  • SP

    Superintendent of Police

    The officer who heads the police of a district.

  • DSP

    Deputy Superintendent of Police

    A state police officer ranking below the superintendent.

  • DGP

    Director General of Police

    The highest-ranking police officer of a state.

  • SHO

    Station House Officer

    The officer in charge of a police station.

  • ASI

    Assistant Sub-Inspector

    A police rank below sub-inspector. ASI also stands for the Archaeological Survey of India.

  • PWD

    Public Works Department

    The government department that builds and maintains roads and public buildings. PwD also means persons with disabilities.

  • BPL

    Below Poverty Line

    A category of households identified for government benefits.

  • APL

    Above Poverty Line

    The category of households not classed as below the poverty line.

  • PDS

    Public Distribution System

    The system of ration shops that supplies subsidised food grains.

  • LPG

    Liquefied Petroleum Gas

    The cooking gas supplied in cylinders, mainly propane and butane.

    LPG explained

  • PNG

    Piped Natural Gas

    Natural gas supplied to homes through pipes. In computing, PNG is an image format.

  • CNG

    Compressed Natural Gas

    Natural gas stored under pressure and used as a vehicle fuel.

  • OBC

    Other Backward Classes

    A category of socially and educationally backward classes recognised for reservation.

  • SC

    Scheduled Caste

    Castes listed under the Constitution for special safeguards. SC also stands for the Supreme Court.

  • ST

    Scheduled Tribe

    Tribes listed under the Constitution for special safeguards.

  • EWS

    Economically Weaker Section

    A category for reservation based on family income, for those not covered by other reservations.

  • NGO

    Non-Governmental Organisation

    A non-profit organisation that works independently of the government.

    NGO explained

  • PSU

    Public Sector Undertaking

    A company owned by the central or a state government.

  • GoI

    Government of India

    The central government of the country.

  • UT

    Union Territory

    A territory administered by the central government, such as Chandigarh or Ladakh.

  • NCR

    National Capital Region

    Delhi together with the surrounding districts of Haryana, Uttar Pradesh and Rajasthan.

  • NCT

    National Capital Territory

    The official name of the territory of Delhi.

  • LoC

    Line of Control

    The military control line between the Indian and Pakistani administered parts of Jammu and Kashmir.

  • BSF

    Border Security Force

    The force that guards India's borders with Pakistan and Bangladesh.

  • CRPF

    Central Reserve Police Force

    The largest central armed police force, used for internal security.

  • CISF

    Central Industrial Security Force

    The central force that guards airports, metro systems and major industrial sites.

  • NDRF

    National Disaster Response Force

    The specialist force for rescue and relief in disasters.

  • NDA

    National Defence Academy

    The joint training academy of the armed forces at Khadakwasla, Pune. In politics NDA is the National Democratic Alliance.

  • CDS

    Chief of Defence Staff

    The senior-most military officer of India. CDS is also the Combined Defence Services examination.

How to read an entry

The letters in bold are the short form as it is normally written. Under it is the full form, and then a line of meaning in plain words. Where the same letters mean something else in another field, the entry mentions it. "Copy" puts the short form, the full form and the meaning on your clipboard, ready to paste into notes or a message.
