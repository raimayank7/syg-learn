Full forms: office and business

34 full forms, each with a line on what it means.

Job advertisements, offer letters, salary slips and office emails take these short forms for granted. A new employee who does not know them can misread an offer: CTC, the cost to company, is not the same as the salary that reaches the bank account. This page covers job titles, the parts of a salary, kinds of leave, kinds of company, and the common words of management and trade. Many began in large companies and are now used in offices of every size. Where a short form has a second meaning elsewhere, as SOP and MD do, the entry says so.

Explained in full: CEO

  • CEO

    Chief Executive Officer

    The most senior manager of a company.

    CEO explained

  • CFO

    Chief Financial Officer

    The senior manager in charge of a company's finances.

  • COO

    Chief Operating Officer

    The senior manager in charge of day-to-day operations.

  • CTO

    Chief Technology Officer

    The senior manager in charge of technology.

  • MD

    Managing Director

    The director who runs a company day to day.

  • HR

    Human Resources

    The department that deals with hiring, pay and staff matters.

  • CV

    Curriculum Vitae

    A document that sets out a person's education and work history. Latin for "course of life".

  • JD

    Job Description

    A written statement of the duties of a job.

  • CTC

    Cost to Company

    The total yearly amount an employer spends on an employee, including benefits.

  • PF

    Provident Fund

    The retirement savings to which employee and employer contribute.

  • ESI

    Employees' State Insurance

    The social security scheme that gives medical and cash benefits to workers.

  • WFH

    Work From Home

    Doing one's job from home.

  • KPI

    Key Performance Indicator

    A measure used to judge how well a person or business is doing.

  • KRA

    Key Result Area

    A main area of responsibility on which an employee is assessed.

  • SOP

    Standard Operating Procedure

    A written set of steps for doing a routine task. In admissions, SOP means statement of purpose.

  • MoU

    Memorandum of Understanding

    A written record of an agreement between parties, usually less formal than a contract.

  • NOC

    No Objection Certificate

    A letter stating that the issuer has no objection to what the holder plans to do.

  • B2B

    Business to Business

    Trade between businesses.

  • B2C

    Business to Consumer

    Sales from a business to the public.

  • R&D

    Research and Development

    The work of creating new products and knowledge.

  • QA

    Quality Assurance

    The process of making sure a product meets its standards.

  • ROI

    Return on Investment

    The gain from an investment compared with its cost.

  • BPO

    Business Process Outsourcing

    Contracting business tasks, such as customer support, to another company.

  • Pvt Ltd

    Private Limited

    A company whose shares are privately held and whose owners have limited liability.

  • LLP

    Limited Liability Partnership

    A form of partnership in which partners have limited liability.

  • Inc

    Incorporated

    A word after a company name showing that it is a registered corporation.

  • AGM

    Annual General Meeting

    The yearly meeting of a company's shareholders.

  • CSR

    Corporate Social Responsibility

    A company's spending and work for the good of society.

  • TA

    Travelling Allowance

    Money paid to an employee for travel on duty.

  • DA

    Dearness Allowance

    An allowance paid to offset rising prices.

  • HRA

    House Rent Allowance

    The part of a salary meant for house rent.

  • LTA

    Leave Travel Allowance

    An allowance for travel while on leave.

  • CL

    Casual Leave

    Short leave for personal reasons.

  • EL

    Earned Leave

    Leave earned by days worked.

How to read an entry

The letters in bold are the short form as it is normally written. Under it is the full form, and then a line of meaning in plain words. Where the same letters mean something else in another field, the entry mentions it. "Copy" puts the short form, the full form and the meaning on your clipboard, ready to paste into notes or a message.
