What this site is

Full Forms is a collection of the short forms, abbreviations and acronyms that people in India actually meet: on school report cards and admission forms, on bank papers and bills, in government notices and the news, on phones and computers, and in messages. Unlike a bare list, every entry here carries a line of meaning, because knowing that NEFT is "National Electronic Funds Transfer" helps little unless you also know that it is a way of sending money between bank accounts.

Honest about the doubtful ones

Not everything in capitals is an abbreviation. Where a name has no full form, the entry says so, and popular invented expansions are collected on the page full forms that are not real.

Questions people ask

What is a full form?

It is the complete word or phrase that a short form stands for. The full form of ATM is Automated Teller Machine. "Full form" is the usual Indian English term; elsewhere people say "stands for".

Does every word in capitals have a full form?

No. Wi-Fi, SOS and FASTag are names, not initials, and popular expansions of ordinary words such as NEWS and POLICE are invented.

Why does one short form have several full forms?

Because different fields chose the same letters. TDS is tax deducted at source in banking and total dissolved solids in water testing. The subject tells you which is meant.

Can I use this for exam preparation?

Yes. The entries cover the full forms usually asked in school general knowledge, computer papers and competitive examinations, and the quiz lets you test yourself by subject.
