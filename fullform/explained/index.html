Full forms explained in full

The 31 short forms people look up most, each with a page of its own.

A full form alone often leaves the real question unanswered. Knowing that EMI is "equated monthly instalment" does not tell you what is inside an instalment or why a longer loan costs more. Knowing that PDF is "portable document format" does not tell you why a form must be uploaded in it. These pages go one step further. Each one gives the full form, takes the name apart where that helps, explains what the thing is and where you meet it, lists the related short forms, and points out the mistake people most often make.

They are written for students preparing for general knowledge and computer papers, and for anyone who has met one of these terms on a form, a bill or a screen and wants to understand it properly.
