CEO full form: Chief Executive Officer

How to say it: letter by letter.

CEO stands for Chief Executive Officer. The CEO is the most senior manager of a company, responsible for running it and for its results.

What a CEO does

  • Sets the direction of the company with the board of directors and carries it out.
  • Takes the major decisions: new products, new markets, large investments.
  • Chooses and leads the senior management team.
  • Speaks for the company to investors, the government and the public.
  • Answers to the board of directors for performance.

CEO, MD and Chairman

These titles overlap, which causes confusion.

  • The Chairman or Chairperson heads the board of directors, which represents the owners and supervises the management.
  • The CEO heads the management, which runs the business from day to day.
  • MD, Managing Director, is the title Indian company law uses for a director entrusted with substantial powers of management. In many Indian companies one person is "MD and CEO".

In some companies one person is both chairman and chief executive; in others the two posts are kept apart on purpose.

The "C-suite"

Senior posts whose titles begin with "Chief" are together called the C-suite.

  • CFO: Chief Financial Officer.
  • COO: Chief Operating Officer.
  • CTO: Chief Technology Officer.
  • CIO: Chief Information Officer.
  • CMO: Chief Marketing Officer.
  • CHRO: Chief Human Resources Officer.

The other CEO

In government, CEO can also mean Chief Electoral Officer, the official who supervises elections in a state, or the chief executive officer of a body such as a zila parishad.

Saying and writing it

CEO is said letter by letter and written in capitals without full stops. The plural is CEOs, with no apostrophe.
