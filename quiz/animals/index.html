Animals and Nature quiz questions with answers

Thirty questions on animals: the biggest, the fastest, baby names, group names and wildlife found in India. Children love the easy round; the tricky one tests the grown-ups.

  • Easy round

    Ten easy questions to warm up. Most players should get seven or more.

    • Which is the largest land animal?
    • Which is the fastest land animal?
    • Which animal is called the king of the jungle?
    Play this round

  • Medium round

    Ten questions that need a little thought. A good round to split the teams.

    • Which is the largest animal that has ever lived?
    • Which mammal can truly fly?
    • Which bird lays the largest egg?
    Play this round

  • Tricky round

    Ten harder questions. Give double points here if you like a dramatic finish.

    • Which Indian state is the only wild home of the Asiatic lion?
    • Kaziranga National Park, famous for the one-horned rhinoceros, is in which state?
    • What is a group of crows called?
    Play this round

How to play

Open a round and tap the option you think is right. The answer and a short note appear at once, and your score is counted at the bottom. To play with a group, send the round on WhatsApp and paste the answer key when the time is up.

Who this topic suits

Animals and Nature is the topic small children ask for again. It suits ages six and above, family groups, primary classes and nature clubs. The Easy round is about animals a child has seen or read about; the later rounds bring in birds, insects, sea life and some surprising habits.

Hosting tips

Let children act out the answer, or make the animal's sound, for a bonus point; it keeps the youngest players in the game even when the questions get harder. Many animals have more than one common name, so accept any name that plainly means the same animal. After the quiz, ask each child for their favourite new fact. Parents can turn the round into a game for a visit to a zoo, a park or a walk: who can spot something from tonight's questions?
