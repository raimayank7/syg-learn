How spaced repetition and study boxes work

This page explains how our boxes work. It is our own explanation; the gaps are our choice and are not a scientific rule.

The idea

You forget things over time, and you remember a thing better if you meet it again just as you are starting to forget it. So a card you know well can wait longer, and a card you miss should come back soon.

What happens when you answer

You pressWhat happens to the card
I knew itIt moves up one box (to box 5 at most) and waits the box's gap
Not yetIt goes back to box 1 and comes back tomorrow

The gaps

BoxGap
11 day
22 days
34 days
48 days
516 days

A card that you know every time reaches box 5 after four correct answers, and then shows up only every 16 days.

A worked example

You study a new card on Monday and know it: it goes to box 2 and is due on Wednesday. On Wednesday you know it again: box 3, due four days later on Sunday. On Sunday you miss it: it returns to box 1 and is due on Monday.

What the tool does not do

  • It does not know how hard a card is. A hard card that you miss goes to box 1 each time.
  • It does not remind you; open the page when you can. Cards that are overdue wait until you come.
  • It does not measure how well you learn. Only you can say if the answers are in your head.

All advice on this page is our own writing, not research. The box intervals are our own choice, not a rule. This site is independent and is not connected with any flashcard service. Your cards stay in your browser.
