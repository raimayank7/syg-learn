A simple daily flashcard study routine

A small routine done every day beats a long session once a week. This is our own advice.

The ten-minute routine

  1. Open the page and see how many cards are due.
  2. Go through the due cards. Say the answer aloud before you press Show the answer.
  3. Be honest: if you hesitated for long, press Not yet.
  4. Add a few new cards from today's lesson, no more than ten.
  5. Stop when the time is up.

Say it, do not just think it

Saying or writing the answer is harder than recognising it, and it works better. If you only look at the answer and nod, the card teaches little.

When many cards are due

After a break, the number due can look large. Do the oldest first and stop after ten minutes; the rest wait for tomorrow. Do not add new cards until the pile is small.

Before an exam

Study your weakest cards (box 1 and 2) more often in the last week. Use the print page to take the hardest cards with you.

Keep a copy

The cards are stored in the browser only. Copy the text of your deck into a note or a mail to yourself now and then.

All advice on this page is our own writing, not research. The box intervals are our own choice, not a rule. This site is independent and is not connected with any flashcard service. Your cards stay in your browser.
