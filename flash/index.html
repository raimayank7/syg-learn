Flashcard maker and study tool with spaced boxes

Type your cards, one on each line as front | back, and study the ones due today.

How to use it

  1. Type your cards in the box, one on each line, with the front, a | sign and the back.
  2. Press Study the cards due today. Read the front, say the answer, then press Show the answer.
  3. Press I knew it or Not yet. A card you knew moves up a box and comes back later; a card you missed goes back to box 1.
  4. Come back each day. The page shows how many cards are due.

Common questions

Where are my cards kept?

In your browser on this device only. Nothing is sent to us. Clearing site data removes them.

Can I use two sides of text, a picture or sound?

Text only. Each card is one line.

How many cards can I have?

Up to 300 in one deck.
