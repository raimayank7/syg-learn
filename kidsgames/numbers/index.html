Number Friends

Find the number named, then say what comes before, after or between. Age 3 to 6

Loading the game...

How to play

  1. Read the question aloud.
  2. Tap the number that is named.
  3. In level 3 the question is about the order of numbers, such as "What comes after 7?"
  4. A wrong tap fades so the child can try again.

A round has eight questions and no clock. The best stars for each level are kept on this device only.

Levels

  • Level 1: Tap the number from one to five.
  • Level 2: Tap the number from one to ten.
  • Level 3: What comes after, before or between, up to twenty?

Start at level 1. Move up when most answers are right the first time.

What this game practises

Knowing the written form of a number is called numeral recognition, and it is a different skill from counting. Level 1 and 2 build it. Level 3 builds number order: after, before and between, which is the beginning of the number line and of adding one more.

Good to know

Numerals such as 6 and 9, or 12 and 21, are easy to mix. The game does not trick children on purpose and keeps similar numbers apart where it can, but it cannot prevent a mix-up. Seeing numerals around the home (clocks, doors, calendars, bus numbers) matters more than any game.

Tips for grown-ups

  • Point to numbers on a calendar or a clock during the day.
  • In level 3, say the numbers in a row aloud first: "five, six, seven".
  • Writing the number in the air or on a tray of sand helps it stick.
  • If a child confuses 6 and 9, pick up a real card and turn it round together.

Play it without a screen

Write numbers on cards and put them in order on the floor. Hide a number card and ask which one is missing. Hop along a number line made of chalk on the ground. Hunt for the number five on doors and signs on a short walk.

A word of care

Level 3 expects a child to know the numbers up to twenty in order. For a child who knows only to ten, level 2 is plenty.
