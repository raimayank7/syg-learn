Games for 2-year-olds

Learning games for 2-year-olds.

Two is the age of "mine", "no" and "again". Children of this age learn from real things and from people: a spoon, a cup, a mother's voice. A game on a phone is a very small part of that learning. If you use one, use it together, sitting close, with the child touching the screen and you talking.

Games that suit this age

  • Colour MatchFind the colour that is asked for, then name the colour of everyday things.Age 2 to 5
  • Animal SoundsWhich animal says moo? What does the frog say? Match animals with their sounds.Age 2 to 5
  • Big and SmallPick the bigger or smaller one, the biggest or smallest, the longest or the shortest.Age 2 to 5

How to play well at this age

  • Play only level 1. Many two-year-olds will still not match colours by name. That is quite normal.
  • Let the child tap anything, and name what happens: "You touched the red one."
  • Keep each play to a few minutes. Stop while the child is still happy.
  • Never use a game to calm a crying child as a habit. Cuddles, songs and a walk work better.

What to expect

A two-year-old may tap for the cause and effect alone, may not name colours, and will probably leave after a minute or two. That is the right result. If your child enjoys tapping and hearing you name things, the game is doing its job. Do not count the stars.

Screen time at this age

Two-year-olds learn many words very fast, but each child has their own pace, and talking late is not unusual. If you are worried about how your child talks, hears or sees, ask a doctor. A game cannot tell you.

A sample five-minute session

  1. Sit next to your child and say what you will play.
  2. Open Colour Match and choose level 1.
  3. Read each question aloud and let your child tap. Say the right answer out loud together.
  4. When a round ends, praise the trying, not only the stars.
  5. Stop, and do something with your hands or your feet: build, draw, hop, dance.
