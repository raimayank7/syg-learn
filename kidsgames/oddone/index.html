Odd One Out

Three pictures belong together and one does not. Which one is different? Age 3 to 6

Loading the game...

How to play

  1. Look at the four pictures.
  2. Three are the same kind of thing. One is not.
  3. Tap the one that does not belong.
  4. A wrong tap fades; the child can look again.

A round has eight questions and no clock. The best stars for each level are kept on this device only.

Levels

  • Level 1: Fruit, animals and vehicles.
  • Level 2: Adds clothes, flowers and sweets.
  • Level 3: Harder kinds: pets, jungle animals, birds and sea animals.

Start at level 1. Move up when most answers are right the first time.

What this game practises

Grouping things by kind is called classifying, and it is the base of sorting, science and even grammar later. A child must find what the pictures share and notice the one that does not fit. Level 3 uses smaller groups inside one big group (all animals), so the child must think about what kind of animal.

Good to know

Children can sort by colour or size long before they sort by kind. A fruit and a vehicle differ in many ways, so level 1 is easy. In level 3 all four pictures are animals, so the child must find a deeper link, such as "lives at home" or "lives in the sea". There are other ways to group animals; the game uses the plain ones: pets, jungle animals, birds and sea animals.

Tips for grown-ups

  • Ask "why is this one different?" and accept any sensible reason, even if it is not the one in the game.
  • Make your own sets with toys: three cars and one doll.
  • If level 3 is too hard, stay on level 2 for a while.
  • Do not tell the answer at once. Ask "what do these three have in common?"

Play it without a screen

Gather four things from the kitchen where three can go in the fridge and one cannot. Make a set from toys, shoes or fruit and let the child spot the odd one. Take turns to make the puzzle for each other, which is the best part.

A word of care

Where a grown-up sees a second right answer, the game still has only one. For instance, in level 3 a hen is a bird and a farm animal. The game never places a picture where it could fit two groups.
