Count the Things

Count apples, stars, fish and more, then tap the right number. Age 3 to 6

Loading the game...

How to play

  1. Look at the picture of things.
  2. Touch each one with a finger on the screen, saying a number for each. This is the way to count.
  3. Tap the number that tells how many there are.
  4. The right answer is praised; a wrong tap fades and the child counts again.

A round has eight questions and no clock. The best stars for each level are kept on this device only.

Levels

  • Level 1: One to five things.
  • Level 2: Three to ten things.
  • Level 3: Eight to twenty things.

Start at level 1. Move up when most answers are right the first time.

What this game practises

Counting has two parts that are learned together: saying the numbers in order, and matching one number to one thing. The game checks the second part, because the child must give the total. Level 3 has twenty things, which is where a child needs a system, such as touching each item or counting in rows.

Good to know

Children often recite "one, two, three, four, five" long before they can count five objects. Counting is only meaningful when each thing gets one number and the last number said is the total. This game is more useful with a finger counting along the screen than with eyes alone, so encourage touching.

Tips for grown-ups

  • Let the child touch each item as they count, even on a phone screen.
  • Ask "how many altogether?" after counting, so the last number becomes the answer.
  • If the child guesses, say "let us count them together" and do so slowly.
  • Do not rush level 3. Many five-year-olds are still building towards twenty.

Play it without a screen

Count everything: stairs, spoons, biscuits, steps to the gate. Lay out ten coins or pebbles and count them, then hide some under a cup and ask how many are left. Count out snacks one by one, which makes counting matter.

A word of care

Do not worry if a child skips or repeats numbers. This happens often. Go back to counting small amounts of real things before trying the game again.
