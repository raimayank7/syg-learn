How this set works

Ten four-option questions a round on quantitative aptitude and data reading at a simple level, marked +1 and −0.25. Questions are made new each time. A right answer scores +1 mark, a wrong answer −0.25 and a skipped one 0, which follows the IBPS rule of taking off one-fourth of a question's marks. After each answer you see the working.

How to review a round

In arithmetic, most wrong answers come from using the wrong formula, a slip in a unit, or an arithmetic error. After each wrong answer, redo the question without looking at the working. If you now get it right, the slip was careless. If you get it wrong again, the method is shaky: do five similar questions from a school textbook.

Because a wrong answer costs a quarter of a question's marks, a round with three wrong answers and two skips can be better than one with no skips and five wrong. Compare rounds by marks, not by attempts.
