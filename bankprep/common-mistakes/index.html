Common IBPS PO mistakes and how to avoid them

Before the exam

  • Not reading the notification. The structure, penalty and cut-off rule are in it.
  • Preparing only one test. You must qualify in each of the three Preliminary tests.
  • Ignoring the Main. Its marks alone decide merit, so the Preliminary is only a gate.
  • Waiting for the last day to apply. The fee and form can only be done online in the window.

On the application

  • Wrong category. The notification says category and eligibility cannot be changed after the edit window.
  • Graduation result after the date. The result must be declared on or before 21 July 2026 for this cycle.
  • Mismatched names. The photo ID must carry the same name as the call letter.

In the exam hall

  • Blind guessing. It gains almost nothing and may cost marks.
  • Spending too long on one question. Each test is separately timed; move on.
  • Arriving late. The notification says late candidates are not allowed in.
  • Forgetting documents. A valid call letter and original photo ID are needed.

How to keep a mistake from coming back

Write each mistake with its reason in a notebook and review it every week. A mistake you name is easier to stop.

Source: IBPS, Detailed Notification for the Common Recruitment Process CRP PO/MT-XVI (vacancies of 2027-28), dated 30 June 2026, from ibps.in, read on 14 November 2026. It covers only the PO/MT process. IBPS also runs clerk, rural bank and specialist officer processes with their own notifications, which we have not read, and SBI and other banks run their own tests. Rules can change by notice: check ibps.in. This site is independent and is not connected with IBPS or any bank.
