Bank Exam Guide

IBPS PO marks calculator

Choose the stage and enter your right and wrong counts for each test. Each wrong answer takes off a quarter of a question's marks.

How it works

The notification (read on 14 November 2026) gives each test its questions and marks, for example 35 questions for 30 marks. The tool divides the marks by the questions to get the marks of one question, gives that for each right answer, and takes a quarter of it off for each wrong one.

Using it well

  • Use it on mock papers, not to predict your result.
  • Look at each test, not only the total: the notification asks for a minimum in each.
  • Check how many marks you lose to wrong answers, and whether skipping them would have been better.

An example

TestQuestionsRightWrongMarks
Quantitative Aptitude3522318.21 of 30
Reasoning Ability3525028.57 of 40
English Language3020818.00 of 30

In Quantitative Aptitude 22 right and 3 wrong give (30/35) × (22 − 0.75) = 18.21. A question is worth 30/35 = 0.86 marks there, and each wrong answer takes off a quarter of that. In English, 8 wrong answers cost 2 right answers' worth of marks, so 20 right and 8 wrong is worth 18 marks.

Questions people ask

Do Preliminary marks count for the final merit?

No. The notification says only the Main marks count for shortlisting and merit. The Preliminary is to qualify.

Are there cut-offs for each test?

Yes. The notification says a candidate must qualify in each of the three Preliminary tests and get minimum scores in each test of the Main; IBPS decides the cut-offs.

Does the tool include the descriptive paper?

No. It adds only the objective tests.
