What is on this site

IBPS runs the Common Recruitment Process for Probationary Officers and Management Trainees in 11 public sector banks. This site gathers the structure of both online stages, the age, degree and fee rules, the participating banks and indicative vacancies, and the schedule, with plain guides to negative marking, the Preliminary and the Main.

Alongside the facts are practice sets for the quantitative, data and reasoning topics, and tools that turn your counts into marks, a target into attempts, and each test's time into seconds per question.

Where the facts come from

Every figure about the exam comes from IBPS's Detailed Notification CRP PO/MT-XVI of 30 June 2026 on ibps.in, read on 14 November 2026. We read nothing else: not the clerk, rural bank or specialist notifications, not SBI. The notification's schedule is tentative. Rules and dates change, so check the official site before you act. Our study advice is our own and makes no promise about results.

Common questions

What is the IBPS PO exam pattern?

A 100-mark Online Preliminary of three separately timed tests (60 minutes), a 200-mark objective Online Main plus a 25-mark descriptive paper (3 hours 10 minutes), a Personality Test and a 100-mark Interview. Final score is Main and Interview in the ratio 80:20. Source: the CRP PO/MT-XVI notification, read on 14 November 2026.

Is there negative marking in IBPS PO?

Yes. One-fourth (0.25) of the marks of a question is deducted for a wrong answer in the objective tests, and nothing for a blank.

What is the age limit?

20 to 30 years as on 1 July 2026 in the XVI notification, with 5 years more for SC and ST, 3 for OBC (non-creamy layer) and 10 for persons with benchmark disabilities.

What is the fee?

Rs 850 (including GST) for all others and Rs 175 for SC, ST and PwBD candidates in the XVI notification.

How many vacancies are there?

The notification gives an indicative 6,715 for 2027-28 across 11 banks, with three banks not reported when it was published.

Are you connected with IBPS?

No. This is an independent site, not connected with IBPS or any bank.
