IBPS PO exam pattern: Prelims, Mains and interview

The Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) runs the Common Recruitment Process for Probationary Officers and Management Trainees (CRP PO/MT) for the participating public sector banks, once a year. The notification says the online examination is two tier: an Online Preliminary and an Online Main. Those who clear both are called for a Personality Test and then a common Interview run by the banks. The 2026 notification (CRP PO/MT-XVI) is for the vacancies of 2027-28.

Each test is timed separately. The notification says candidates must qualify in each of the three tests by getting the minimum cut-off marks IBPS decides, and a sufficient number of candidates in each category is then shortlisted for the Main.

Together the Main takes 3 hours 10 minutes (160 minutes plus 30 minutes). The notification says the Descriptive Paper may be broadly based on economic and social issues, emerging trends in banking and technology, current events and ethics, and that it may be evaluated by an automated scoring mechanism that looks at features of writing proficiency.

Personality Test and Interview

  • Those who qualify in the Main are called for a Personality Test (self-report). It is non-qualifying, but appearing in it is mandatory to be eligible for the Interview.
  • The Interview carries 100 marks; the minimum qualifying marks are not less than 40% (35% for SC, ST, OBC and PwBD candidates).
  • The final score combines the Main and the Interview in the ratio 80 : 20.

Which marks count

The notification is clear: only the marks of the Online Main are used for shortlisting for the Personality Test and Interview and for the final merit list. The Preliminary is for qualifying into the Main only.

Negative marking

In the objective tests of both stages, one-fourth (0.25) of the marks assigned to a question is deducted for a wrong answer. A blank costs nothing. See our page on negative marking.

Sessions and scores

If the examination is held in more than one session, the corrected scores are normalized by the equi-percentile method, and scores are taken up to two decimal places.

Source: IBPS, Detailed Notification for the Common Recruitment Process CRP PO/MT-XVI (vacancies of 2027-28), dated 30 June 2026, from ibps.in, read on 14 November 2026. It covers only the PO/MT process. IBPS also runs clerk, rural bank and specialist officer processes with their own notifications, which we have not read, and SBI and other banks run their own tests. Rules can change by notice: check ibps.in. This site is independent and is not connected with IBPS or any bank.
