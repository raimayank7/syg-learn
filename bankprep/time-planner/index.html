IBPS PO time planner

Choose a test and the minutes for a final check. The tool shows the seconds a question gets.

The numbers behind it

Each test has its own time limit in the notification. The planner takes your final-check minutes off that time and divides the rest by the number of questions.

How the minutes divide

Each test of both stages is timed separately. The table shows the seconds per question for each test, and with two minutes kept for a final check.

The Preliminary tests allow about 34 seconds a question for Quantitative Aptitude and Reasoning, so there is little time for long working. The descriptive paper of the Main has 30 minutes for two answers.

Three sample plans

  • Two passes: answer what you can do at once, then use the rest of the time on the marked ones.
  • Hard last: start with the types you are fastest at to bank marks, and leave long calculations to the end.
  • Time boxes: give each question at most twice the average time, then move on.

Whichever plan you pick, check the clock every five minutes in a 20-minute test.

Questions people ask

Can I use leftover time from one test in another?

The notification says each test is separately timed, so time does not carry over from one test to the next.

How long should I keep for a final check?

Find out by practice. In a 20-minute test, one or two minutes is usual.
