IBPS PO score planner

Choose a test, a target and your accuracy. The tool works out how many questions you would need to attempt.

What it assumes

It assumes equal marks for each question in the test, a deduction of one-fourth of a question's marks for a wrong answer, and the same accuracy throughout.

Reading the result

  • At 100% accuracy the number to attempt is the least that reaches the target.
  • Each point of accuracy you lose forces you to attempt more questions for the same score.

Look along a row: higher accuracy cuts the questions you need to attempt. Look down a column: each rise in target needs many more right answers. "Not reachable" means even attempting all 35 at that accuracy falls short.

How to use the planner in practice

After a timed test, note how many questions you attempted and how many were right. Divide right by attempted for your accuracy, put it in the tool with your target, and compare with what you actually attempted. If you attempted far more than the tool says, you may be losing marks to wrong answers.

Questions people ask

Why does accuracy matter so much?

A wrong answer costs a quarter of the marks you could have earned on top of losing the chance of a right one.

Does it predict the cut-off?

No. IBPS decides cut-offs. The tool only turns marks and accuracy into counts.
