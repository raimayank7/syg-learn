IBPS PO Main exam guide: four tests and descriptive

The Main Examination has four objective tests and a descriptive paper. The notification gives the structure; the topics are in the Information Handout that comes with the call letter. We list the structure and then our own advice.

The tests

The Main matters most: its marks alone decide shortlisting and merit (with the interview at 20%). The three 60-mark tests together make 180 of the 200 objective marks, so Reasoning, Awareness and Data Analysis decide most of the score, while English counts only 20.

General, economy and banking awareness

The notification names the areas: general awareness, economy, banking awareness, digital and financial awareness, including RBI circulars. Read the RBI and bank news regularly and keep short notes with dates. That is our advice, because the notification gives no list of topics.

Descriptive paper

Two questions, an essay and a comprehension, in 30 minutes and 25 marks. The notification says it may be based on economic and social issues, emerging trends in banking and technology, current events and ethics, and may be scored by an automated mechanism for writing proficiency. Write clearly, in plain correct English, in full sentences, and watch the time.

Passing

The notification says each candidate has to get a minimum score in each test of the Main and also a minimum total score. Cut-offs are decided from the number of vacancies, and the scores are not shared before the interview process ends.

Source: IBPS, Detailed Notification for the Common Recruitment Process CRP PO/MT-XVI (vacancies of 2027-28), dated 30 June 2026, from ibps.in, read on 14 November 2026. It covers only the PO/MT process. IBPS also runs clerk, rural bank and specialist officer processes with their own notifications, which we have not read, and SBI and other banks run their own tests. Rules can change by notice: check ibps.in. This site is independent and is not connected with IBPS or any bank.
