IBPS PO eligibility: age, degree, fee and pay scale

Age (as on 1 July 2026)

Minimum 20 years, maximum 30 years: born not earlier than 2 July 1996 and not later than 1 July 2006, both dates inclusive. The upper limit is relaxed:

CategoryRelaxation
Scheduled Caste / Scheduled Tribe5 years
Other Backward Classes (non-creamy layer)3 years
Persons with benchmark disabilities10 years
Ex-servicemen and commissioned officers (conditions in the notification)5 years

The notification says the SC, ST and OBC relaxation can be combined only with one of the disability or ex-servicemen relaxations, and that the maximum age applies to General and EWS candidates.

Education

A degree (graduation) in any discipline from a university recognised by the Government of India, or an equivalent qualification recognised by the Central Government. You must have the mark sheet or degree certificate on the day you register, and the final result must have been declared on or before 21 July 2026. You enter your graduation percentage in the application.

Nationality

A citizen of India, a subject of Nepal or Bhutan, a Tibetan refugee who came to India before 1 January 1962 to settle, or a person of Indian origin who migrated from the countries the notification lists with the intention of settling in India (with a certificate of eligibility in the last cases).

Fee

CategoryApplication fee / intimation charges
SC, ST, PwBDRs 175 (including GST)
All othersRs 850 (including GST)

Payment is online only, from 1 to 21 July 2026, and the bank charges for online payment are borne by the candidate.

Pay

The notification gives the scale of pay of a Probationary Officer or Management Trainee as basic Rs 48,480 rising by Rs 2,000 for 7 years, Rs 2,340 for 2 years and Rs 2,680 for 7 years to Rs 85,920, with allowances and perquisites as the participating bank's rules provide.

An important caution

The notification says that applying, qualifying and being provisionally allotted does not mean you will necessarily be employed: the ultimate authority for recruitment is the participating bank, which may reject a candidature. It also says category and eligibility details cannot be changed after the edit window closes, so fill the form with care.

Source: IBPS, Detailed Notification for the Common Recruitment Process CRP PO/MT-XVI (vacancies of 2027-28), dated 30 June 2026, from ibps.in, read on 14 November 2026. It covers only the PO/MT process. IBPS also runs clerk, rural bank and specialist officer processes with their own notifications, which we have not read, and SBI and other banks run their own tests. Rules can change by notice: check ibps.in. This site is independent and is not connected with IBPS or any bank.
