IBPS PO/MT XVI schedule and what is known now

The notification gives a tentative schedule for CRP PO/MT-XVI. It says the dates may change and that updates appear on ibps.in. The dates below are from that schedule, as it stood on 30 June 2026. We did not find a later notice on the IBPS home page on 14 November 2026, and we have not checked whether each stage ran on the listed month, so read this as the plan, not the record.

ActivityTentative schedule
Online registration and payment of fee1 July to 21 July 2026
Edit window for the applicationTwo days after registration closes (to be notified)
Pre-examination trainingAugust 2026
Online Preliminary: call letters and examAugust 2026
Online Preliminary resultSeptember 2026
Online Main: call lettersSeptember 2026
Online Main examOctober 2026
Main resultNovember 2026
Personality TestNovember 2026
InterviewNovember or December 2026
Provisional allotmentJanuary 2027

What this tells you

  • The process is a single yearly cycle that starts in July and ends with allotment the following January.
  • The notification says its validity expires one year after the date of provisional allotment, or when a fresh provisional allotment is made, whichever is earlier. So a result is for one cycle and does not carry over.
  • On the IBPS home page on 14 November 2026 CRP PO/MT-XVI was the latest PO/MT notification listed, so we do not give any date for the next one.

When the next notification comes

IBPS has said in this notification that it conducts the process once a year. The pattern of the schedule (registration in July) is the pattern for this cycle only, so do not plan around a July start without a notice. Check ibps.in and, if you like, the participating banks' pages.

Call letters and the day

The notification says a candidate needs a valid call letter for the date and session, and original photo ID with the same name as on the call letter. Candidates who report after the reporting time on the call letter are not allowed in. Although the Preliminary lasts one hour, you may be at the venue for two hours or more, and for the Main (3 hours 10 minutes) four hours or more.

Source: IBPS, Detailed Notification for the Common Recruitment Process CRP PO/MT-XVI (vacancies of 2027-28), dated 30 June 2026, from ibps.in, read on 14 November 2026. It covers only the PO/MT process. IBPS also runs clerk, rural bank and specialist officer processes with their own notifications, which we have not read, and SBI and other banks run their own tests. Rules can change by notice: check ibps.in. This site is independent and is not connected with IBPS or any bank.
