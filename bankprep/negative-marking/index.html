IBPS PO negative marking: the one-fourth rule

The notification says that in the objective tests of both the Preliminary and the Main, one-fourth (0.25) of the marks assigned to a question is deducted for a wrong answer, to give the corrected score. A blank has no penalty. This is milder than a one-third rule, but it still changes how you should choose.

What a question is worth

The marks per question differ by test. In the Preliminary, for instance, Quantitative Aptitude has 35 questions for 30 marks (about 0.86 each), Reasoning Ability 35 for 40 (about 1.14 each) and English 30 for 30 (1 each). Whatever the worth, one wrong answer takes off a quarter of it, so four right answers are cancelled by sixteen wrong ones: each wrong costs a quarter of one right.

The arithmetic of a guess

What you can rule outChance of a right answerAverage result of a guess (1-mark question)
Nothing (blind guess among 4)1 in 40.25 × 1 − 0.75 × 0.25 = +0.06
One option1 in 30.333 × 1 − 0.667 × 0.25 = +0.17
Two options1 in 20.5 × 1 − 0.5 × 0.25 = +0.38

A blind guess is worth only +0.06 on average, which is nearly nothing and carries the risk of a few lost marks in a short test. Ruling out even one option raises it to +0.17. Because each Preliminary test is separately timed, a few guesses that go wrong can also cost you a sectional cut-off.

A worked example (35-question test of 30 marks)

CandidateAttemptedRightWrongMarks
A25223(30/35) × (22 − 3/4) = 18.21
B30237(30/35) × (23 − 7/4) = 18.21
C352411(30/35) × (24 − 11/4) = 18.21

All three score the same, although they attempted 25, 30 and 35 questions. The more you attempt, the more wrong answers you must trade for the extra right ones. Your own best number to attempt comes from your accuracy.

A rule of thumb (our own advice, not IBPS's)

  • Answer everything you can justify.
  • Guess only when you can rule out at least one option.
  • Because each Preliminary test has its own cut-off, do not let a bad guess in one test spoil a safe score.

Source: IBPS, Detailed Notification for the Common Recruitment Process CRP PO/MT-XVI (vacancies of 2027-28), dated 30 June 2026, from ibps.in, read on 14 November 2026. It covers only the PO/MT process. IBPS also runs clerk, rural bank and specialist officer processes with their own notifications, which we have not read, and SBI and other banks run their own tests.
