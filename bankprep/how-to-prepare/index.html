How to prepare for IBPS PO

This is our own advice, built on the structure that the notification gives: a 60-minute Preliminary in three separately timed tests, a 3-hour-10-minute Main, a quarter penalty and a short interview. We cannot promise results.

1. Read the notification

Read the exam structure (clause D), the penalty (E), the cut-off rule (F) and the interview rule (J). It takes half an hour and removes most of the guesswork.

2. Work test by test

Because each test is separately timed and must be qualified separately, prepare in blocks: English, Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning, then (for the Main) Data Analysis, Awareness and writing.

3. Build speed in arithmetic

Learn tables, squares, simple percentages and fractions by heart, and practise the same question forms daily. In a test of 20 minutes for 35 questions, 34 seconds a question is not much time for long working.

4. Practise the penalty

Practise with the quarter penalty so that your habit of skipping and guessing is formed before the exam. Our practice sets use it.

5. Read for awareness every day

The Main has 50 questions on general, economy and banking awareness, including RBI circulars. Read one newspaper section and one banking news source every day, and write one line on each item you find important.

6. Write for the descriptive paper

Write one short essay and one comprehension answer a week to a 30-minute limit. Ask a friend to read it for clarity.

7. Prepare for the interview later

The interview has 100 marks and counts for 20% of the final score. Start preparing after the Main, but start your reading of banking news now.

8. Keep a mistake notebook

After each mock, write the question type, the reason for the error and the fix. Review the notebook each week.

Source: IBPS, Detailed Notification for the Common Recruitment Process CRP PO/MT-XVI (vacancies of 2027-28), dated 30 June 2026, from ibps.in, read on 14 November 2026. It covers only the PO/MT process. IBPS also runs clerk, rural bank and specialist officer processes with their own notifications, which we have not read, and SBI and other banks run their own tests. Rules can change by notice: check ibps.in. This site is independent and is not connected with IBPS or any bank.
