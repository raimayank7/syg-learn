IBPS PO ninety-day study plan

This is a ninety-day plan of our own. It is an example to adjust, not an official schedule. Because the date of the next CRP PO/MT is not out, count back from the exam date in the notice when it appears.

DaysFocusWhat to do
1 to 10Read the notification, set a baselineTake one timed Preliminary mock to see where you stand in each of the three tests. Fix three weak topics.
11 to 40Build the baseDaily: one hour of arithmetic and data practice, 40 minutes of reasoning, 30 minutes of English reading. One awareness page a day.
41 to 60Speed and accuracyTimed test-by-test practice. Ten minutes on mistakes after each.
61 to 80Full mocksTwo full Preliminary mocks a week, one Main mock a week. Review each.
81 to 90Revise and restLight revision of formulas, notes and awareness points. Sleep well. Check documents.

Each week

  • Two full timed tests
  • One review of the mistake notebook
  • Two descriptive answers written to the clock
  • One rest day

If you have less time

Drop the lowest-weight area first. In the Main, English Language has 20 marks while Reasoning, Awareness and Data Analysis have 60 each, so time on English comes after time on those three, but note that the Preliminary has 30 marks of English and needs a qualifying score.

If you work full time

Plan fewer hours but fix them: one hour before work and two after, and use the weekend for mocks. Regular hours beat bursts.

Source: IBPS, Detailed Notification for the Common Recruitment Process CRP PO/MT-XVI (vacancies of 2027-28), dated 30 June 2026, from ibps.in, read on 14 November 2026. It covers only the PO/MT process. IBPS also runs clerk, rural bank and specialist officer processes with their own notifications, which we have not read, and SBI and other banks run their own tests. Rules can change by notice: check ibps.in. This site is independent and is not connected with IBPS or any bank.

A sample weekday

  1. Morning, 60 minutes: arithmetic and data practice, one topic at a time.
  2. Midday or evening, 45 minutes: reasoning, with a timer on each set.
  3. Evening, 30 minutes: English reading and one awareness page.
  4. Night, 15 minutes: review the day and list the next three tasks.

When you fall behind

Do not try to catch up by doubling the next day. Drop the least valuable task of the week and keep the rest. Practice under the clock and review beat learning one more shortcut.

The last week

Stop starting new topics. Take one full Preliminary mock every other day, review every wrong answer, and keep your sleep regular. Check your documents: a valid call letter and original photo ID are needed on the day.
