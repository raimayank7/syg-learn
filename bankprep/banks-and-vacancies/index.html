IBPS PO participating banks and indicative vacancies

The notification lists 11 participating public sector banks. Provisional allotment is to one of them, on merit-cum-preference after the interview, keeping to the Government's reservation policy and administrative needs.

The participating banks

  1. Bank of Baroda
  2. Bank of India
  3. Bank of Maharashtra
  4. Canara Bank
  5. Central Bank of India
  6. Indian Bank
  7. Indian Overseas Bank
  8. Punjab National Bank
  9. Punjab & Sind Bank
  10. UCO Bank
  11. Union Bank of India

Indicative vacancies for 2027-28

The notification gives an indicative total of 6,715 posts and says the numbers depend on business needs, so the actual allotment may differ. Three banks (Indian Bank, UCO Bank and Union Bank of India) had not reported their numbers when it was published. Some banks' numbers may include backlog vacancies.

Persons with benchmark disabilities are counted within these totals: the table in the notification gives totals of 73, 70, 67 and 77 across the banks in its columns headed HI, OC, VI and ID. Treat all of these numbers as indicative.

How allotment works

The notification says shortlisted candidates are provisionally allotted to a participating bank on merit-cum-preference, on the actual vacancies reported and in the spirit of the reservation guidelines of the Government. If two candidates have the same score, the senior in age is placed higher. A reserved-category candidate selected on General category norms is treated as General and is not adjusted against a reserved post. The bank then has the final say on recruitment.

Source: IBPS, Detailed Notification for the Common Recruitment Process CRP PO/MT-XVI (vacancies of 2027-28), dated 30 June 2026, from ibps.in, read on 14 November 2026. It covers only the PO/MT process. IBPS also runs clerk, rural bank and specialist officer processes with their own notifications, which we have not read, and SBI and other banks run their own tests. Rules can change by notice: check ibps.in. This site is independent and is not connected with IBPS or any bank.
