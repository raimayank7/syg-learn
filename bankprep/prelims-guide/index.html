IBPS PO Prelims guide: three tests in 60 minutes

The Preliminary decides only whether you move to the Main. The notification does not list syllabus topics: it gives the three tests and says other detailed information is in an Information Handout, which candidates download with the call letter. So what follows is our own plan, built from the structure the notification gives.

The three tests

TestQuestionsMarksTimeSeconds a question
English Language303020 minutes40
Quantitative Aptitude353020 minutes34
Reasoning Ability354020 minutes34

Time per question is minutes times 60 divided by questions. At about 34 seconds a question the tests reward calm, quick work and clear shortcuts, not long reading.

Qualifying in each test

The notification says a candidate must qualify in each of the three tests by securing the minimum cut-off marks IBPS fixes. A very high score in two tests does not make up for a poor score in the third. Plan to be reliably above a basic level in all three before you push one test higher.

Where marks are

Reasoning Ability has the most marks (40 of 100), followed by English Language and Quantitative Aptitude at 30 each. But it also has the most questions with Quantitative Aptitude (35 each). If you are slow at arithmetic, practising shortcuts and the same question forms daily is the most direct way to gain marks.

How our practice sets help

Our practice covers numeracy and data questions and reasoning questions at a simple level, and applies a quarter penalty. English questions are not offered, because they need real passages that we do not copy.

Practice with the clock

Because each test is separately timed, practise one test at a time with a timer of 20 minutes. Use the time planner to see your seconds per question.

Source: IBPS, Detailed Notification for the Common Recruitment Process CRP PO/MT-XVI (vacancies of 2027-28), dated 30 June 2026, from ibps.in, read on 14 November 2026. It covers only the PO/MT process. IBPS also runs clerk, rural bank and specialist officer processes with their own notifications, which we have not read, and SBI and other banks run their own tests. Rules can change by notice: check ibps.in. This site is independent and is not connected with IBPS or any bank.
