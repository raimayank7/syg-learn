How to write a project report: a simple plan

A short report tells the reader what you did and what you found. This plan is our own suggestion. Always use your teacher's format if there is one.

HeadingWhat to write
TitleA short name for the project, for example "Do plants need light?"
Question or aimWhat you wanted to find out or to show.
MaterialsA list of everything you used.
StepsWhat you did, in order, in short numbered sentences.
ResultsA table, a chart or a drawing of what you saw. Use numbers if you can.
ConclusionWhat the results tell you. Say whether your guess was right.
What I learnedOne or two sentences in your own words.

Tips

  • Write in your own words and do not copy from a book or a website.
  • Use a ruler to draw tables and lines.
  • Check your spelling and your numbers before you hand it in.
