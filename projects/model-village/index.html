A Cardboard Model Village: a school project idea

Build a model village from boxes, with roads, houses and a key.

  • Social science
  • Classes 4 to 8
  • About 3 h of work
  • Time to finish: a weekend
  • Adult needed
An adult is needed. An adult must do the cutting of the boxes and cardboard. Please read all the steps before the child begins.

What this project shows

To show the main parts of a village and how people live and work in it.

What you need

You needHow many
Small cardboard boxes8
Large cardboard sheet for the base1
Scissors1
White glue1
Paints or crayons (set)1
Paper labels8

Steps

  1. Choose a base sheet and mark where the roads, the fields, a river and a school will go with a pencil.
  2. An adult cuts the boxes into houses, a school and a shop, with doors and windows drawn or cut.
  3. Paint or colour the buildings and the base, and let them dry.
  4. Glue the buildings in place and add roads and a river with paper or paint.
  5. Make small labels for each building and a key on a corner of the base.

What to observe and write down

Write a short paragraph on each place: what happens there and who uses it. Say which places a village needs and why.

How it works

A village has homes, places to work, a school, shops and roads. Making a model helps to see how the parts fit together and what each is for.

Presenting it

Walk your audience through the village as if they were visiting. Say who lives in each house.
