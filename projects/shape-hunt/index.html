A Shape Hunt: a school project idea

Find shapes at home, draw them and count them in a table.

  • Maths
  • Classes 1 to 3
  • About 40 min of work
  • Time to finish: one afternoon
  • No adult needed

What this project shows

To spot shapes in everyday things and to count and show the results.

What you need

You needHow many
Plain paper sheet4
Pencil1
Coloured pencils (set)1
Ruler1

Steps

  1. Choose four shapes to look for: circle, square, triangle and rectangle.
  2. Walk round your home with an adult and look for each shape. Draw each thing you find.
  3. Make a table with the four shapes and write how many things of each shape you found.
  4. Colour in a block graph with one square for each thing, one column for each shape.

What to observe and write down

Say which shape you found most often and which least often. Write two sentences that begin: "I found more...".

How it works

Shapes are everywhere: a plate is a circle, a window is a rectangle, a roof is a triangle. Counting and drawing them in a table and graph is a first step in handling data.

Presenting it

Show your graph and ask the audience to guess which shape is the most common in their own homes.
