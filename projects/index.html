School project ideas by class, with materials lists

Choose a class and a subject, tick the projects you like and print what you need.

How to use the finder

  1. Choose your class, and a subject if you have one.
  2. Choose how much time you have. Tick Needs no adult if you want projects to do alone.
  3. Tick Add to my list on the projects you like. The materials are added up for you.
  4. Press Print my list and take the list shopping, or put it on the table.

What is in the finder

There are 21 projects, from a shape hunt in class 1 to a red cabbage indicator in class 10. 12 of them need no adult at all. Every project has its own page with a table of materials, numbered steps, what to look at and write down, and how it works in simple words.

Before you start

Ask your teacher what your project must show, how big it may be, and when it is due. Read the steps through first and ask an adult for help with anything marked. See school project safety and how to choose a project.

The projects on this page are our own suggestions and are not a syllabus or a teacher's instructions. Always follow your teacher's rules for your own project. This site is independent and is not connected with any school, board or exam body. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
