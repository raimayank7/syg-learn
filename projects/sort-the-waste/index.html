Sorting the Waste: a school project idea

Sort clean household waste into groups and make a chart.

  • Environment
  • Classes 3 to 6
  • About 30 min of work
  • Time to finish: one session
  • Adult needed
An adult is needed. An adult must remove glass, tins and any sharp things before the child sorts, and children must wash their hands afterwards. Please read all the steps before the child begins.

What this project shows

To show how to sort waste, and which kinds of waste a family makes the most of.

What you need

You needHow many
Boxes or bags for sorting4
Marker pen1
Clean household waste20
Plain paper sheet1

Steps

  1. An adult takes out any glass, tins or sharp things, and keeps only clean, safe items for the sorting.
  2. Label four boxes: paper, plastic, metal and food waste.
  3. Pick up each item with a glove or a tissue and put it in the right box.
  4. Count how many items are in each box and write the numbers on the chart.
  5. Draw a picture graph with one square for each item in a column.

What to observe and write down

Say which box had the most and the fewest items. Write two ideas for using less of the biggest kind.

How it works

Sorting waste is the first step in recycling, because different materials are treated in different ways. Reusing things and making less waste are even better than recycling.

Presenting it

Show your chart and say one thing your family will do differently. Wash your hands after sorting.
