IOQM 2026: eligibility, fee and what comes next

IOQM 2026 is the first stage of the Mathematical Olympiad for the 2026-27 cycle. Its eligibility rules are in an official PDF from HBCSE. We summarise them here; read the PDF yourself, because you are responsible for meeting them.

Who can write IOQM 2026

  1. Born between 1 August 2007 and 31 July 2014 and studying in Class 8, 9, 10, 11 or 12. A student born on or after 1 August 2014 is not allowed.
  2. Eligible to hold an Indian passport. Students with OCI cards are provisionally eligible up to the camp stage with conditions, because of orders of the Madras High Court; they are not eligible for selection to the Indian team at the IMO, EGMO and APMO.
  3. Residing and studying in India since 30 October 2024 or earlier, or studying in an Indian school system since 30 October 2024 or earlier.
  4. Must not have qualified (or be scheduled to appear in) the Class 12 board examination earlier than 30 October 2026. Giving up a Class 12 result to meet this is not allowed and leads to disqualification.
  5. Must not have started (or plan to start) studies in a university or equivalent institution by 1 June 2027.

The PDF adds that INMO 2026 awardees may write INMO 2027 directly, under conditions, and gives the criteria for EGMO 2028. It says it is the student's responsibility to make sure the criteria are met, and that a student found later not to meet them can be disqualified.
