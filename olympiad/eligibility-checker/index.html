Olympiad eligibility checker

Enter your date of birth and class to check the age and class rules of IOQM 2026 and the science NSE 2026.

Rules read from HBCSE on 14 November 2026.

How the checker works

It compares your date of birth and class with the rules HBCSE publishes. For IOQM 2026: born between 1 August 2007 and 31 July 2014 (both days included) and in Class 8, 9, 10, 11 or 12. For the science NSE 2026: born between 1 July 2007 and 30 June 2012 (both days included) and in Class X, XI or XII. The tool tests those two rules only.

What it cannot tell you

  • Whether you can hold an Indian passport, or the OCI conditions.
  • Whether you have lived and studied in India since the date HBCSE names (30 October 2024 for IOQM, 30 November 2024 for the science NSE).
  • Whether you have passed or are scheduled for the Class 12 board examination too early.
  • Whether you plan to start university study before 1 June 2027.

HBCSE says it is the student's responsibility to make sure every condition is met. The enrolment windows for IOQM 2026 and NSE 2026 had closed on 14 November 2026, so the tool is mainly for planning the next cycle, whose age rules will differ.

Questions people ask

Will the next cycle use the same dates of birth?

No. Each cycle has its own dates, which HBCSE publishes. Do not reuse these.

Is the result final?

No. Only HBCSE decides eligibility.
