What is on this site

The national Olympiad programme in India is run by the Homi Bhabha Centre for Science Education. It leads, in stages, to the International Olympiads in Mathematics, Astronomy, Biology, Chemistry and Physics. This site gathers the stages, the eligibility rules, the 2026-27 dates and the syllabus areas that HBCSE publishes, and adds practice for the numerical style of the first mathematics stage.

Where the facts come from

Every figure comes from the HBCSE Olympiad pages, the HBCSE Science Olympiad Brochure and the IOQM 2026 eligibility PDF and site, read on 14 November 2026. We did not read past papers or the IOQM marking scheme. Dates and rules change by announcement, so check the official pages before you act. Our study advice is our own and makes no promise about results.

Common questions

What are the stages of the Maths Olympiad in India?

Five: IOQM (three hours, 30 questions with one- or two-digit answers), RMO (three hours, six proofs), INMO (third Sunday of January, six proofs), a training camp for about 65 students and the IMO team of six. Source: HBCSE, read on 14 November 2026.

What are the stages of the Science Olympiad?

Five: the National Standard Examinations (stage 1, fee Rs 300), the Indian National Olympiads (about 400 per subject qualify), camps at HBCSE for about 40 per subject, training, and the International Olympiads with four or five students per subject.

Who can write IOQM 2026?

Students born between 1 August 2007 and 31 July 2014 and studying in Class 8 to 12, who can hold an Indian passport and meet the residence and other conditions in the HBCSE PDF.

When is RMO 2026?

On 15 November 2026, 13:00 to 16:00, for students who qualified in IOQM 2026. INMO is on 17 January 2027.

When are the NSE 2026 exams?

NSEA on 21 November 2026, NSEB, NSEC and NSEP on 22 November 2026, per HBCSE. Enrolment had closed on 14 November 2026.

Are you connected with HBCSE?

No. This is an independent site, not connected with HBCSE, IAPT, MTA(I) or NBHM.
