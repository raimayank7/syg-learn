How this set works

Ten questions a round on progressions, roots and a right triangle, with answers from 0 to 99. Questions are made new each time. You work on paper and type a whole number from 0 to 99, as on the IOQM answer sheet. After each answer you see the working. Our practice does not give marks or a penalty; it only counts right answers.

What the questions test

The set draws on these topics. Each row says what is asked and the slip that most often goes with it.

TopicWhat is askedThe usual slip
Arithmetic progressionsThe number of terms from the first, the common difference and the last term.Off by one.
Roots of a quadraticp² + q² from the equation.Squaring the sum and stopping.
Geometry: inradiusThe inradius of a right triangle.Using the area formula without the semi-perimeter.

How to review a round

After a wrong answer, redo the question without looking at the working. If you now get it right, the slip was careless. If you get it wrong again, the idea is not clear: read about the topic in a recommended book and try five more questions on it. A round with a couple of skips is fine: it is better to skip honestly than to guess.

Please note

  • These are our own questions, made by a program. They are much easier than real IOQM questions and cannot practise proof writing, which RMO and INMO need.
  • Your best score is kept in your browser only. Nothing is sent to us.
