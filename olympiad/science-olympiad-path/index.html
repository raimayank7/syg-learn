Science Olympiad in India: NSE, INO and dates 2026-27

The national Science Olympiad programme covers Astronomy, Biology, Chemistry, Physics and Junior Science. HBCSE says it follows a five-stage selection process leading to the International Olympiads in 2027.

The five stages

StageWhat it isFacts from HBCSE
1National Standard Examinations (NSEs)Objective-type examinations held close to the end of November every year, organised by the Indian Association of Physics Teachers (IAPT), offered in English and Hindi with other languages also available. The registration fee is Rs 300, which HBCSE calls the only fee a student pays until the International Olympiads.
2Indian National Olympiads (INOs)About 400 students in each subject qualify from the NSEs. Held in late January at around 17 centres, with objective and long questions. From this stage HBCSE runs the programme.
3Orientation-cum-Selection Camps (OCSCs)About 40 top students from each INO are invited to HBCSE from April to June for orientation and several theoretical and experimental tests.
4Training of the selected teamsTraining at HBCSE in theory and experiment (and in observational astronomy).
5International OlympiadsFour or five Indian students in each subject, with two to four mentors.

HBCSE notes that training starts at the third stage and that the first two stages do not need specialised coaching outside the regular school system. It also says the questions at all levels, including the NSEs, may be expected to be harder than board examinations.

HBCSE gives the dates for the stage-1 examinations of Astronomy, Biology, Chemistry and Physics; we did not read the Junior Science dates.

How to read this path

Only the first two stages are open to every eligible student: the NSE at your school or online, and the INO if you qualify. From the third stage HBCSE trains and selects a small team. HBCSE says the first two stages do not need specialised coaching outside the regular school system, so the best preparation is a good grasp of your school books and plenty of problem solving.
