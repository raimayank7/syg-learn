How this set works

Ten four-option questions a round drawn from numerical ability and reasoning, as in a mixed General Aptitude Test. Questions are made new each time. A right answer scores +5, a wrong one −1 and a skipped one 0, as in the NTA bulletin. After each answer you see the working.

What the questions test

The set draws on these topics. Each row says what is asked and the slip that most often goes with it.

TopicWhat is askedThe usual slip
PercentagesThe percentage of a number.Dividing by 10 instead of 100.
Ratio and proportionSharing an amount in a ratio.Using the larger part as the number of parts.
AveragesThe missing number from an average.Forgetting to multiply the average by the count.
Simple interestSI from principal, rate and time.Using the amount instead of the interest.
Speed, time and distanceDistance from speed and time.Mixing km per hour with minutes.
Profit and lossThe profit per cent from cost and selling price.Dividing by the selling price instead of the cost.
MensurationArea or perimeter of a rectangle.Swapping area and perimeter formulas.
HCF and LCMHCF or LCM of two numbers.Giving the LCM when the HCF is asked.
Number seriesThe next number in an adding, multiplying or squares series.Checking only the first gap.
Direction senseDistance after two walks at right angles.Adding the walks instead of using Pythagoras.
ClocksThe angle between the hands at an exact hour.Giving the larger angle.
CalendarThe day of the week after some days.Counting the days instead of the remainder after sevens.
AgesAn age from the sum and the difference.Dividing the sum by 2 and stopping.
Odd one outThe number that is not a multiple of k.Checking only two of the numbers.

How to review a round

A mixed round shows where marks go across the two kinds of question. Sort the wrong answers by topic and by kind of slip. If one kind of question accounts for most of them, spend your next session on it with its own set.

Repeat the mixed set after a week and compare the number of wrong answers, not the score.
