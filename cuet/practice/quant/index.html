How this set works

Ten four-option questions a round on percentages, ratio, averages, simple interest, speed, profit and loss, area and HCF or LCM. Questions are made new each time. A right answer scores +5, a wrong one −1 and a skipped one 0, as in the NTA bulletin. After each answer you see the working.

What the questions test

The set draws on these topics. Each row says what is asked and the slip that most often goes with it.

How to review a round

In arithmetic, most wrong answers come from using the wrong formula, a slip in a unit, or an arithmetic error. After each wrong answer, redo the question without looking at the working. If you now get it right, the slip was careless; note the kind. If you get it wrong again, the method is shaky: do five similar questions from a school textbook.

Do not judge a round by score alone. A round with three wrong answers and four skipped can be better than one with no skips and five wrong, because a skip costs nothing.
