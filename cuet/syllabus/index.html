CUET UG syllabus: languages, domain subjects and GAT

The CUET (UG) bulletin describes the syllabus in three parts. This page sets out what the 2026 bulletin says about each part. The detailed syllabus for every domain subject is a separate NTA document that we have not reproduced.

Source: NTA, Information Bulletin CUET (UG) 2026, from cuet.nta.nic.in, read on 14 November 2026. On that day the CUET site showed documents for CUET (UG) 2026 and none for 2027, so these are the latest figures we could read. Rules can change by public notice: check cuet.nta.nic.in. This site is independent and is not connected with NTA, the Ministry of Education or any university.

Language papers

The bulletin says languages are tested through reading comprehension, based on different types of passages: factual, literary and narrative. It also lists literary aptitude and vocabulary. The thirteen language papers are listed on our subjects page.

Domain-specific subjects

The bulletin says domain subjects follow the NCERT syllabus. There are 23 of them, from Accountancy to Sociology, listed on the subjects page. For each, the detailed syllabus is published by NTA on the CUET website. Read it for the subjects you choose, and study from your Class 12 textbooks.

General Aptitude Test

General knowledge, current affairs, general mental ability, numerical ability, quantitative reasoning (simple application of basic arithmetic, algebra, geometry, mensuration and statistics) and logical and analytical reasoning. See our General Aptitude Test guide.

Using the syllabus

  • For each paper you take, download the NTA syllabus and tick off topics as you cover them.
  • Study from the textbook of your board for domain subjects.
  • For the General Aptitude Test, practise the small question types regularly rather than in one burst.
