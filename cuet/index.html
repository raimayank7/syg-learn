What is on this site

CUET (UG) is the common entrance test for undergraduate programmes of central, state, deemed and private universities that accept it. Because you choose your papers, the main skill is choosing well and then preparing paper by paper. This site gathers the pattern and marking, the full subject list with codes, the dates and fees, and a guide to choosing papers.

Alongside the facts are practice sets for the General Aptitude Test and tools that turn your counts into marks, a target into attempts, and 60 minutes into seconds per question.

Where the facts come from

Every figure about the exam comes from NTA's Information Bulletin for CUET (UG) 2026 and NTA's notices on cuet.nta.nic.in, read on 14 November 2026. The 2027 bulletin was not out on that day. Rules and dates change by public notice, and each university decides which papers it accepts, so check both before you act. Our study advice is our own and makes no promise about results.

Common questions

What is the CUET UG pattern?

Each paper has 50 compulsory multiple-choice questions in 60 minutes, on a computer, with +5 for a right answer and −1 for a wrong one. You can take up to five of the 37 papers (13 languages, 23 domain subjects and the General Aptitude Test), per the NTA bulletin read on 14 November 2026.

How is CUET UG marked?

A correct answer scores +5, an incorrect answer −1 and an unanswered question 0, so a full paper is out of 250.

When is CUET UG 2027?

On 14 November 2026 the NTA CUET site showed no bulletin or date for 2027. In 2026 the exam was held from 11 May to 7 June. Check cuet.nta.nic.in.

Is there an age limit?

The 2026 bulletin says there is no age limit for CUET UG, though a university may set its own for a programme.

Which subjects should I choose?

Start from the programmes and universities you want and check which papers each accepts. See our guide on choosing subjects.

Are you connected with NTA?

No. This is an independent site, not connected with NTA, the Ministry of Education or any university.
