CUET UG score planner

Choose a target for one paper and your accuracy. The tool works out how many questions you would need to attempt.

What it assumes

It assumes one paper of 50 questions with +5 and −1 marking, as in the 2026 bulletin, and the same accuracy throughout.

Reading the result

  • At 100% accuracy a target of 200 needs 40 right answers.
  • Each point of accuracy you lose forces you to attempt more questions for the same score.

Look along a row: higher accuracy cuts the questions you need to attempt. Look down a column: each extra 50 marks needs about ten more right answers plus the wrong ones that come with them. "Not reachable" means even attempting all 50 at that accuracy falls short.

How to use the planner in practice

Take your last three timed papers and note how many questions you attempted and how many were right. Divide right by attempted for your accuracy, put it in the tool with a target, and compare with what you actually attempted. If you attempted far more than the tool says, you may be losing marks to wrong answers that you could have skipped.

Questions people ask

Why does accuracy matter so much?

A wrong answer costs the 5 marks you could have earned and 1 more on top, a swing of 6.

Does it predict my rank?

No. It only turns marks and accuracy into counts.
