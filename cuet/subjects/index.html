CUET UG subject list: all 37 papers with codes

CUET (UG) 2026 offered 37 papers in three groups. This page lists them all with the codes the bulletin uses. The code is what you select on the application form.

Source: NTA, Information Bulletin CUET (UG) 2026, from cuet.nta.nic.in, read on 14 November 2026. On that day the CUET site showed documents for CUET (UG) 2026 and none for 2027, so these are the latest figures we could read. Rules can change by public notice: check cuet.nta.nic.in. This site is independent and is not connected with NTA, the Ministry of Education or any university.

How to read this list

You can choose up to five of these 37 papers. A language paper tests reading comprehension and vocabulary in that language. A domain paper tests a school subject on the NCERT syllabus. The General Aptitude Test is the same for everyone and does not depend on a school subject. Which of them you need depends on the programme: see choosing subjects.

Notes on a few subjects

  • Mathematics / Applied Mathematics (319) is one paper in the list; check which of the two a programme accepts.
  • Biology / Biological Science / Biotechnology / Biochemistry (304) is also one paper that covers several names.
  • Computer Science / Information Practices (308) and Economics / Business Economics (309) likewise combine two names in one paper.
  • Performing Arts (320) and Physical Education (321) list their branches in brackets; the NTA syllabus says what each covers.

The bulletin gives a code to every paper, and the codes are not continuous (there is no 310, 311 or 317 in the list). Use the codes exactly as printed when you fill in the form.
