CUET UG marks calculator

Enter your counts for each paper. Each follows the official scheme: +5 for each right answer, −1 for each wrong one, 0 for skipped.

How it works

For each paper the tool multiplies your right answers by 5 and subtracts one mark for each wrong answer. A paper has 50 questions, so it is out of 250, and five papers make 1,250. The scheme is from the CUET (UG) 2026 bulletin (read on 14 November 2026).

Using it well

  • Use it on mock papers.
  • Try changing one number. Turning a wrong answer into a skipped one adds 1 mark; turning it into a right one adds 6.
  • These are raw marks. How universities use CUET scores is set by each university.

Three example papers

The table shows how three styles of attempting one 50-question paper end up, with +5 for right and −1 for wrong.

StyleRightWrongMarks (of 250)Note
Careful38218840 attempted, 2 wrong
Fast3020130All 50 attempted, 20 wrong
Balanced41520046 attempted, 5 wrong

The fast attempt tries all 50 questions but gets only 30 right, and its 20 wrong answers pull the total down to 130. The careful attempt tries 40 and scores 188. The balanced attempt tries 46 and scores 200. Attempting more helps only while accuracy stays high.

Questions people ask

Does this give a percentile or an NTA score?

No. It gives raw marks only.

Is a skipped question marked down?

No. An unanswered question scores 0.

How many papers can I enter?

Up to five, matching the most the bulletin allows.
