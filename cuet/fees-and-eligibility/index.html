CUET UG fees and eligibility

Fees for CUET (UG) depend on the category and on how many subjects you choose. This page gives the table in the 2026 bulletin and the eligibility rules.

Source: NTA, Information Bulletin CUET (UG) 2026, from cuet.nta.nic.in, read on 14 November 2026. On that day the CUET site showed documents for CUET (UG) 2026 and none for 2027, so these are the latest figures we could read. Rules can change by public notice: check cuet.nta.nic.in. This site is independent and is not connected with NTA, the Ministry of Education or any university.

Fee for CUET (UG) 2026

CategoryUp to 3 subjectsEach additional subject
General (UR)₹1,000₹400 each
OBC (NCL) or EWS₹900₹375 each
SC, ST, PwD, PwBD or Third Gender₹800₹350 each
Centres outside India₹4,500₹1,800 each

You can choose up to five subjects, including languages and the General Aptitude Test, so up to two are "additional". Payment is online by net banking, credit card, debit card or UPI. The bulletin says the OBC category is for the Central List OBC (NCL) only; State list OBC candidates who are not in the Central List should choose General.

Eligibility

  • The bulletin says there is no age limit for appearing in CUET (UG) 2026.
  • Candidates who have passed Class 12 or an equivalent examination, or are appearing in it in 2026, can appear.
  • Universities may set their own age and eligibility rules for the programme. The bulletin says you must check them yourself.
  • The bulletin lists the qualifying examinations (final examination of the 10+2 system of a recognised board, intermediate or pre-university, NIOS and others); read the list in the bulletin.

Things that cause trouble

  • Applying twice. The bulletin says multiple application forms will not be accepted and lead to cancellation of the result.
  • Choosing papers that the university you want does not accept. Check the university pages first.
  • Missing the correction window, in which only some fields can be changed.
