CUET UG dates: 2026 and what is known for 2027

This page gives the CUET (UG) 2026 dates from the bulletin, what NTA's public notices say about how the examination ran, and what the NTA site showed about 2027 on the day we read it.

Source: NTA, Information Bulletin CUET (UG) 2026, from cuet.nta.nic.in, read on 14 November 2026. On that day the CUET site showed documents for CUET (UG) 2026 and none for 2027, so these are the latest figures we could read. Rules can change by public notice: check cuet.nta.nic.in. This site is independent and is not connected with NTA, the Ministry of Education or any university.

How the examination actually ran

An NTA press release of 7 June 2026 says NTA conducted CUET (UG) 2026 from 11 to 31 May and 6 to 7 June 2026 across 321 examination cities, including 15 international cities, with 37 subjects (13 languages, 23 domain-specific subjects and the General Aptitude Test). The NTA site also lists notices about rescheduling the examination for candidates affected by a technical glitch, release of admit cards for rescheduled candidates, provisional and final answer keys, and a notice declaring the result and NTA scores, in June 2026.

CUET (UG) 2027

On 14 November 2026 the NTA CUET site showed no bulletin or dates for CUET (UG) 2027. Until NTA publishes them, nobody can give you the 2027 dates or fees with certainty. Treat any website that does as unconfirmed.

How to use this

  • Check the NTA site every week from the new year; in 2026 the application window opened in the first days of January.
  • When the new bulletin appears, read the "dates at a glance" and fee boxes first.
  • Also check each university's own admission pages, because they decide which papers count for each programme.
