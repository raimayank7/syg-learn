Magic tricks for kids: mind-reading cards and number magic

Print the cards, try the number tricks, and learn the secret of every trick.

Mind-reading cards

Try a number trick

The mind-reading cards

Print the five cards above and ask a friend to think of a number from 1 to 31. Show the cards one at a time and ask whether the number is on each. Add up the first number of every card with a yes, and you have the number. Read the full trick, including the secret and what to say.

How the number tricks work

Every number trick on this site is a piece of plain arithmetic. The tool above lets you pick a trick, type a number, and see every step with the working. Before you show a friend, try the tricks yourself. All of the tricks are explained in full, so you can learn how they work and then try your own.

All the tricks

  • Think of a NumberWhatever number a friend thinks of, you tell them the answer: it is always 5.Number trick, ages 8 to 13
  • The 1089 TrickA friend picks a number, does some turning around and subtracting, and the answer is always 1089.Number trick, ages 9 to 13
  • The Calendar SquareYou know the total of nine dates a friend circles on a calendar, without adding them.Number trick, ages 9 to 13
  • Mind-Reading CardsA friend thinks of a number from 1 to 31 and you name it after seeing which of five cards it is on.Number trick, ages 8 to 13
  • Always NineA friend subtracts the digits of a two-digit number and the answer always adds up to nine.Number trick, ages 8 to 13
  • The Missing DigitYou name a digit that a friend hides from their answer, without seeing the number.Number trick, ages 10 to 13
  • The 1001 TrickA friend writes a number twice and divides it three times, and gets the number back.Number trick, ages 10 to 13
  • Guess the BirthdayA friend does five small sums and the answer shows the month and day of their birthday.Number trick, ages 10 to 13
  • Even or Odd CoinsYou know whether the number of coins in a friend's right hand is even or odd.Number trick, ages 8 to 13
  • The Choice TrickThree small toys on a table, and whatever the audience says, they end up choosing the toy you wanted.Logic trick, ages 7 to 13
  • The Rubbery PencilA plain pencil seems to bend and wobble like rubber in your hand.Optical illusion, ages 5 to 12
  • The Hole in Your HandYou look through a tube and see a round hole through the palm of your hand.Optical illusion, ages 5 to 12
  • Dancing Paper BitsA balloon rubbed on your hair makes tiny pieces of paper jump up to it.Science trick, ages 7 to 12
