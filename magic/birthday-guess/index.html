Guess the Birthday: an easy magic trick

A friend takes the month of their birthday through a string of sums and gives you only the final answer. You read their birthday in it.

  • Number trick
  • Ages 10 to 13
  • About 10 min
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Paper1
Pencil1
Calculator (optional)1

What you do

  1. Ask your friend for the number of their birth month (January is 1, December is 12). They must not tell you.
  2. Ask them to multiply it by 5, add 6, multiply by 4, add 9, and multiply by 5.
  3. Ask them to add the day of the month they were born.
  4. Ask for the answer. Take away 165 from it in your head.
  5. The last two digits of what is left are the day, and the digits before them are the month.

The secret

Take month m. Times 5 is 5m, plus 6 is 5m + 6, times 4 is 20m + 24, plus 9 is 20m + 33, and times 5 is 100m + 165. Adding the day d gives 100m + 165 + d. Taking away 165 leaves 100m + d: the month in the hundreds and the day in the last two digits. The worked example checks this for every date.

Try it: the working, step by step

Change the boxes and see every step. This is a worked example for you to learn from.

What to say

Say: "Your birthday is hidden in the number, like a name hidden in a song. Let me find it."

Practice tips

Practise writing the answer as 100 times the month plus the day. For the days 1 to 9 the last two digits start with a 0: for example 305 means March 5. Ask only for dates the friend is happy to share, and do not keep or write down a birthday.

Be a kind magician

A good magician makes people smile and never makes anyone feel silly. Do not play a trick on someone who is upset, do not hold a secret over a younger child, and let your friend learn the trick when they ask politely. Magic is for sharing.
