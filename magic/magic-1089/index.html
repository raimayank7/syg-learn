The 1089 Trick: an easy magic trick

A friend chooses a secret number with three digits and does some sums on paper. You say you know the final answer, write it on a folded paper in advance, and it is 1089.

  • Number trick
  • Ages 9 to 13
  • About 10 min
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Paper1
Pencil1
Calculator (optional)1

What you do

  1. Write 1089 on a small piece of paper, fold it and put it where everyone can see it.
  2. Ask your friend to write down any number with three digits. The first and last digit must be different, for example 832.
  3. Ask them to reverse the digits and write that number below it (238). Then they take the smaller number from the bigger one.
  4. If the answer has only two digits, they put a zero at the front so it has three. They reverse that answer and add the two numbers together.
  5. Ask them to say the final answer aloud. Then unfold your paper to show 1089.

The secret

Take 832 and 238: the difference is 594. Reversed that is 495, and 594 + 495 = 1089. The secret is that the difference of a number and its reverse is always a multiple of 99 with a middle digit of 9, and the digits on each side add up to 9. Adding it to its own reverse then always makes 1089. This has been checked here for every three-digit number whose first and last digits differ.

Try it: the working, step by step

Change the boxes and see every step. This is a worked example for you to learn from.

What to say

Say: "I wrote my prediction before you picked your number, and not even I knew what you would choose."

Practice tips

Practise with five different numbers and check each answer with a calculator. Remember the rule: the first and last digits must be different, and a two-digit difference gets a zero in front. If it goes wrong, say you lost your magic touch and try another number.

Be a kind magician

A good magician makes people smile and never makes anyone feel silly. Do not play a trick on someone who is upset, do not hold a secret over a younger child, and let your friend learn the trick when they ask politely. Magic is for sharing.
