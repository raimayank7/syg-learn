The Calendar Square: an easy magic trick

A friend draws a square around nine dates, three across and three down, on a calendar page. You say the total in a moment.

  • Number trick
  • Ages 9 to 13
  • About 5 min
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Calendar page (one month)1
Pencil1

What you do

  1. Show a month of a calendar, with the seven days in a row across the page.
  2. Ask your friend to draw a square around nine dates, three across and three down, without showing you.
  3. Ask only for the smallest date inside the square, the one in the top left corner.
  4. Add 8 to that date in your head, then multiply the answer by 9. Announce the total.
  5. Let your friend add all nine dates on a calculator to see that you are right.

The secret

In a calendar with seven columns, the date below another date is 7 more, and the date to the right is 1 more. The middle date of the square is therefore the smallest date plus 8. The nine dates are spread evenly around the middle one, so their total is nine times the middle date. You can check this on the worked example on this page for any month.

Try it: the working, step by step

Change the boxes and see every step. This is a worked example for you to learn from.

What to say

Say: "I can feel the weight of the whole square at once. Let me tell you how heavy it is."

Practice tips

Practise on a real calendar until adding 8 and multiplying by 9 is quick. You can say "times 10 minus the middle date" if that is easier. Tell the friend that the square must not run over the side of the page or the end of the month.

Be a kind magician

A good magician makes people smile and never makes anyone feel silly. Do not play a trick on someone who is upset, do not hold a secret over a younger child, and let your friend learn the trick when they ask politely. Magic is for sharing.
