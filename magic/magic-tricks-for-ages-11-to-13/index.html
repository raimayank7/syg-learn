Magic Tricks for Kids

Magic tricks for ages 11 to 13

These 13 tricks suit children aged 11 to 13. The ages are only a guide: any child who enjoys a trick can learn it. Each page tells you what to say, what to do and how to practise.

  • Think of a NumberWhatever number a friend thinks of, you tell them the answer: it is always 5.Number trick, ages 8 to 13, about 5 min
  • The 1089 TrickA friend picks a number, does some turning around and subtracting, and the answer is always 1089.Number trick, ages 9 to 13, about 10 min
  • The Calendar SquareYou know the total of nine dates a friend circles on a calendar, without adding them.Number trick, ages 9 to 13, about 5 min
  • Mind-Reading CardsA friend thinks of a number from 1 to 31 and you name it after seeing which of five cards it is on.Number trick, ages 8 to 13, about 10 min
  • Always NineA friend subtracts the digits of a two-digit number and the answer always adds up to nine.Number trick, ages 8 to 13, about 5 min
  • The Missing DigitYou name a digit that a friend hides from their answer, without seeing the number.Number trick, ages 10 to 13, about 10 min
  • The 1001 TrickA friend writes a number twice and divides it three times, and gets the number back.Number trick, ages 10 to 13, about 5 min
  • Guess the BirthdayA friend does five small sums and the answer shows the month and day of their birthday.Number trick, ages 10 to 13, about 10 min
  • Even or Odd CoinsYou know whether the number of coins in a friend's right hand is even or odd.Number trick, ages 8 to 13, about 5 min
  • The Choice TrickThree small toys on a table, and whatever the audience says, they end up choosing the toy you wanted.Logic trick, ages 7 to 13, about 10 min
  • The Rubbery PencilA plain pencil seems to bend and wobble like rubber in your hand.Optical illusion, ages 5 to 12, about 3 min
  • The Hole in Your HandYou look through a tube and see a round hole through the palm of your hand.Optical illusion, ages 5 to 12, about 3 min
  • Dancing Paper BitsA balloon rubbed on your hair makes tiny pieces of paper jump up to it.Science trick, ages 7 to 12, about 10 min

Choosing for this age

Older children can learn the tricks that need several steps of working, such as the missing digit and the birthday trick, and can explain the maths behind them to younger friends.

The tricks on this page are our own explanations and suggestions. Every child is different, so you decide what suits yours. This site is independent and is not connected with any magician, magic shop or society. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
