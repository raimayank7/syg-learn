Mind-Reading Cards: an easy magic trick

You hand a friend five cards covered with numbers. They think of a secret number from 1 to 31 and say which cards it is on. You name the number at once.

  • Number trick
  • Ages 8 to 13
  • About 10 min
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Card or paper sheet5
Pencil1

What you do

  1. Print the five cards from the home page of this site, or copy them by hand onto five cards, each with its numbers in rows.
  2. Ask your friend to think of any number from 1 to 31 and to keep it secret.
  3. Show them each card in turn and ask only: "Is your number on this card?"
  4. Add up the first (smallest) number of every card that has a yes: the cards start with 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16.
  5. Announce the total as the secret number.

The secret

Each number from 1 to 31 can be made by adding some of 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16, in exactly one way. Card 1 holds every number whose total includes 1, card 2 every number whose total includes 2, and so on. A number that is on cards 1 and 4 must be 1 + 8 = 9. The build of this site checked that every number from 1 to 31 is on exactly the right cards.

The five cards

What to say

Say: "I cannot see your number, but I can see the cards you tick. My mind reads which of them your number lies on."

Practice tips

Learn the first numbers 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 by heart, and the order of the cards. Make a set of cards with your own pictures and decorations on the back so they look special. Do not show the first number of each card; put them in the top left corner without making a fuss.

Be a kind magician

A good magician makes people smile and never makes anyone feel silly. Do not play a trick on someone who is upset, do not hold a secret over a younger child, and let your friend learn the trick when they ask politely. Magic is for sharing.
