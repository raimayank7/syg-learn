The Rubbery Pencil: an easy magic trick

You hold an ordinary pencil and it appears to turn into rubber, bending as it moves.

  • Optical illusion
  • Ages 5 to 12
  • About 3 min
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Pencil1

What you do

  1. Hold a pencil loosely between your thumb and first finger, near one end, with the rest of the pencil sticking out.
  2. Move your hand up and down in small, fast movements, about as fast as shaking a bottle of sauce.
  3. Keep your eyes on the pencil. It will seem to bend in the middle as if it were made of rubber.
  4. Stop and show the pencil. It is straight and hard again.

The secret

The pencil does not bend. Your eyes see the pencil in many positions in a very short time, and your brain joins them into a bendy shape. It is an illusion of the eye and brain and works best when the pencil is held loosely and moved at the right speed. We have not tested it on everyone: some people see it more clearly than others.

What to say

Say: "This pencil is made of a special rubber. Watch what happens when I give it a little shake."

Practice tips

Practise at the speed that works: try slower and faster until it looks best. Use a long pencil with a round shaft, not a sharp one, and hold it by the blunt end. Show it to a friend from the side, as it looks best when you view it with a little distance.

Be a kind magician

A good magician makes people smile and never makes anyone feel silly. Do not play a trick on someone who is upset, do not hold a secret over a younger child, and let your friend learn the trick when they ask politely. Magic is for sharing.
