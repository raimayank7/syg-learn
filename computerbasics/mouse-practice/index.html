Mouse practice game

Press Start and click each circle as it appears. Three levels, from easy to harder.

How to play

  1. Choose a level and press Start.
  2. A circle appears in the practice area. Click it with the left button.
  3. A new circle appears each time. After ten circles the game shows your accuracy.
  4. On level 3, double-click each circle: two quick clicks on it. The circle turns amber after the first click.

The levels

LevelWhat to do
1Click big circles
2Click smaller circles, which needs a steadier hand
3Double-click circles, two clicks no more than a little over half a second apart

Tips

  • Go slowly. Accuracy is the aim, not speed.
  • Rest your hand on the table and move your whole hand.
  • On a phone or tablet the game works with a finger tap.
  • If a double-click will not work, keep the mouse still between the two clicks.
