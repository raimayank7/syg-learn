Computer basics for beginners and older learners

Plain lessons, a typing drill and a mouse game. Free, with no sign-in, and nothing leaves your browser.

Practise

  • Typing drillFour levels, from the middle row of letters to numbers and signs. Shows speed and accuracy.
  • Mouse gameClick big circles, then small ones, then double-click. Good for first-time mouse users.

Lessons

  • Mouse and touchpad basics for beginnersLearn how to hold a mouse, click, double-click, right-click, scroll and drag, and how a laptop touchpad works.
  • Keyboard basicsA beginner's guide to the keyboard: Enter, Space, Backspace, Shift, Caps Lock and Tab, plus copy, paste, undo and save shortcuts and simple typing habits.
  • Files, folders and saving your work on a computerWhat files and folders are, how to save with a clear name, where downloads usually go, how to copy to a pen drive and why a second copy is worth keeping.
  • Email basicsHow email works for beginners: the address, To, Cc and Bcc, subject lines, attachments, reply and reply all, the spam folder and signs of a fake message.
  • Browsing the internet safelyPlain habits for safe browsing: the address bar, the padlock sign, pop-ups that claim a virus, safe downloads, updates and logging out on a shared computer.
  • PasswordsSimple password habits: long and different passwords, why not to reuse them, what two-step sign-in is, and why you should never share a one-time code.
  • How to spot a scam call, message or websiteThe warning signs of online and phone scams: hurry, secret codes, prizes, fake officials, remote access and strange links.
  • Safe habits for UPI and phone payment appsGeneral habits for using UPI and other phone payment apps safely: PIN secrecy, checking the name, QR codes, fake support calls, screen sharing and updates.
  • Teaching a parent or grandparent to use a computerPatient ways to teach computer skills to an older family member: short sessions, let them do it, written steps, safe practice and how to handle mistakes kindly.

How to use this site

  1. If you have never used a mouse, start with the mouse lesson and then the mouse game.
  2. Learn the keyboard and practise with the typing drill.
  3. Learn to save your work and to write an email.
  4. Read the safety lessons before you pay or log in to anything.

Safe habits come first

Three of the lessons are about staying safe: spotting scams, UPI and phone payment habits and passwords. If you read nothing else, read those. The safest rule of all is simple: if someone is rushing you, stop, and ask a person you trust.

All advice on this page is our own writing: general habits, not rules. We do not quote any bank, payment, police or government rule, limit, fee or helpline; check your own bank's official page for those. This site is independent and is not connected with Microsoft, Apple, Google or any bank.
