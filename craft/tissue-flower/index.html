How to make a tissue paper flower

Tissue flowers are big and cheerful and make a good decoration for a party, a school stage or a gift. The more sheets you use, the fuller the flower.

  • Ages 6 to 12
  • About 25 min
  • Adult needed
  • 6 steps
An adult is needed. An adult must trim the petals and check the pipe cleaner ends. Please read all the steps before the child begins.

What you need

You needHow many
Tissue paper sheet6
Scissors1
Pipe cleaner (soft craft wire)1

Steps

  1. Stack the six sheets neatly on top of each other, with the edges lined up.
  2. Starting at one short edge, fold the stack back and forth in narrow folds, about two finger-widths wide, like a concertina.
  3. Fold the stack in half. Twist the pipe cleaner tightly around the middle and leave the two ends as a stem.
  4. An adult trims both ends into a curve with the scissors, for round petals.
  5. Open the folded fan out on each side into a circle. Gently pull up one sheet at a time towards the middle, and fluff the layers.
  6. Bend the stem and put the flower in a cup or a jar.

Tips

Tissue paper tears easily, so pull each layer gently. Choose two colours and alternate the sheets for a flower with stripes. The ends of the pipe cleaner can be sharp, so fold them over and keep them away from faces.
