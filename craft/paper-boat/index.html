How to make a paper boat

This is the boat in nearly every childhood story. It takes some careful folding, and each step is a small lesson in following instructions in order.

  • Ages 6 to 12
  • About 15 min
  • No adult needed
  • 8 steps
A child can do this alone, but sit nearby for the first one.

What you need

You needHow many
A4 paper sheet1

Steps

  1. Put the sheet down with a short side at the top. Fold it in half so that the short edges meet, press the crease flat, and keep it folded with the fold at the top.
  2. Fold the two top corners down so that they meet in the middle. The paper now looks like a triangle roof on top of a strip.
  3. Fold the strip at the bottom up over the triangles on the front. Turn the paper over and fold the other strip up the same way. This makes a paper hat.
  4. Open the hat at the bottom, pull the sides apart and press it flat the other way, so that it becomes a diamond.
  5. Fold the bottom corner of the front layer up to the top corner. Turn over and fold the other bottom corner up too. You now have a smaller triangle.
  6. Open this triangle and flatten it into a diamond again, as you did before.
  7. Hold the two top points and gently pull them apart. The middle of the diamond opens and a boat appears.
  8. Press the bottom of the boat flat so that it sits steadily on a table or on the water.

Tips

A boat made from ordinary paper floats only for a short time, because paper soaks up water. A sheet from a magazine or a wax-coated paper stays afloat longer. Float it in a large bowl or a shallow tray with an adult nearby, and never leave a young child alone near water. Make two boats from different paper and see which one floats longer.
