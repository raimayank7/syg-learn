Paper Crafts for Kids

Paper crafts for ages 7 to 9

These 14 crafts suit children aged 7 to 9. Age ranges are only a guide: some children will want an easier craft and some a harder one, and any craft is better with a friendly adult nearby. Crafts marked with an adult need an adult for the cutting or the pins.

  • Paper PlaneA fast, straight-flying dart made from one sheet.Ages 5 to 12, 10 min
  • Paper BoatThe classic folded boat that floats for a while on a bowl of water.Ages 6 to 12, 15 min
  • Paper FanA folded fan for a hot day, made by folding the paper like a concertina.Ages 4 to 10, 10 min
  • Paper Fortune TellerA folded game where friends choose colours and numbers and read a kind message.Ages 7 to 13, 20 min
  • Newspaper HatA big folded hat for dressing up, made from one sheet of newspaper.Ages 5 to 12, 10 min
  • Paper Bag PuppetA talking puppet made from a paper bag, with a mouth that opens and closes.Ages 4 to 9, 25 min
  • Paper PinwheelA spinning pinwheel that turns when you blow on it or hold it in the wind.Ages 6 to 12, 25 min, adult needed
  • Paper LanternA hanging paper lantern with slits, to decorate a room.Ages 6 to 12, 30 min, adult needed
  • Paper ChainA long, colourful chain of paper loops to decorate a room.Ages 5 to 12, 20 min, adult needed
  • Tissue Paper FlowerA fluffy flower made by folding and fanning out sheets of tissue paper.Ages 6 to 12, 25 min, adult needed
  • Paper SnowflakeA lacy snowflake cut from a folded square, different every time.Ages 5 to 12, 15 min, adult needed
  • Spinning Paper HelicopterA paper helicopter that spins like a seed as it falls.Ages 6 to 12, 15 min, adult needed
  • Torn Paper CollageA picture made by tearing coloured paper and gluing the pieces down.Ages 3 to 10, 30 min
  • Handprint PeacockA bright peacock made from your own handprint, for a wall or a card.Ages 2 to 8, 20 min

Choosing for this age

Children from seven to nine can follow numbered steps and enjoy making something that works, like a plane or a boat. Let them read the steps aloud and make two, so that the second one comes out better than the first.

The crafts on this page are our own suggestions and are not a lesson plan or professional advice. Every child is different, so you decide what suits yours. This site is independent and is not connected with any school or craft brand. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
