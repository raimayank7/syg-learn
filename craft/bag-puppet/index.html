How to make a paper bag puppet

A puppet gives a shy child a voice and gives a group a way to tell stories. The paper bag does the hard work: the flap at the bottom already folds open like a mouth.

  • Ages 4 to 9
  • About 25 min
  • No adult needed
  • 6 steps
A child can do this alone, but sit nearby for the first one.

What you need

You needHow many
Small paper bag1
Crayons or markers (set)1
Glue stick1
Coloured paper scraps4

Steps

  1. Lay the paper bag flat on the table with the folded flap at the bottom of the bag towards you. This flap will be the head and mouth.
  2. Draw two big eyes and a nose on the flap. Draw a mouth along the fold underneath it.
  3. Tear coloured paper scraps into small pieces. Tear rather than snip: torn edges look soft and furry.
  4. Glue the torn pieces on as hair, ears or a hat. Wait a few minutes for the glue to dry.
  5. Colour the body of the bag with shirt stripes or spots. Slip your hand into the bag with your fingers in the flap and your thumb under it.
  6. Open and close your hand to make the puppet talk. Give it a name and a funny voice.

Tips

Put on a short show for the family. Plan three lines for your puppet before you begin: a greeting, a question and a joke. Make a second puppet and let the two talk. Keep the puppets in a drawer for the next rainy day.
