Starter craft box: what to keep for paper crafts

A craft box is just a shoe box or a drawer where the paper and tools live together. When it rains, the box is already there, and nobody has to hunt for the glue. This is a starter box of 10 things. With it alone you can make 8 of our crafts straight away.

What goes in the starter box

  • A4 paper sheet
  • Square paper sheet
  • Crayons or markers (set)
  • Sticky tape (roll)
  • Glue stick
  • Scissors
  • Ruler
  • Pencil
  • Newspaper sheet (double page)
  • Paper clip

Crafts you can make from the box

  • Paper Plane
  • Paper Boat
  • Paper Fan
  • Paper Fortune Teller
  • Newspaper Hat
  • Paper Lantern (an adult must help)
  • Paper Snowflake (an adult must help)
  • Spinning Paper Helicopter (an adult must help)

Keeping the box tidy

Keep scissors and pins in a small closed tin that an adult keeps on a high shelf, and take them out only for crafts that need them. Put the rest in the box and let the children get it themselves. Sort paper by colour or size so that it does not crumple.
