How to make a torn paper collage

The collage needs no scissors, so the youngest children can do it alone. It also reuses old magazines and paper that would otherwise be thrown away.

  • Ages 3 to 10
  • About 30 min
  • No adult needed
  • 5 steps
A child can do this alone, but sit nearby for the first one.

What you need

You needHow many
Old magazine2
Glue stick1
Card or thick paper sheet1

Steps

  1. Look through the magazines and find pages with big areas of one colour: sky blue, grass green, sun yellow.
  2. Tear the pages into pieces of different sizes. Tearing gives soft, uneven edges which look good in pictures.
  3. Lay the pieces on the card to plan a picture: a house, a garden, an animal or a pattern. Move them around before you glue.
  4. Put glue on the back of each piece, press it down and smooth it with your hand.
  5. Leave the collage flat to dry. Write your name and the date in a corner.

Tips

Start with the background and add the smaller pieces at the end. Overlapping pieces make new colours and shapes. Keep a box for torn pieces, sorted by colour, for your next collage. Wash your hands after using glue.
