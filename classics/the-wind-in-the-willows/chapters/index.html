The Wind in the Willows: chapter-by-chapter summary

The book has 12 chapters. They alternate between quiet river-bank scenes and the adventures of Mr. Toad. The headings below are as printed in the text we checked.

Chapter 1: The River Bank

Mole is tired of spring-cleaning and runs out to the sunshine. He reaches a river and meets Water Rat, who takes him out in a boat, shows him the water and gives him a picnic lunch. Mole upsets the boat but is forgiven.

Chapter 2: The Open Road

Rat and Mole visit Toad Hall, where Toad shows off his new passion, a gaily-painted caravan. They set out on the open road with him. A motor-car runs them off the road and wrecks the caravan, and Toad is struck with a love of motor-cars.

Chapter 3: The Wild Wood

Mole slips off alone to the Wild Wood in the hope of meeting Mr. Badger. He is frightened by faces and noises, and is found by Rat. As snow begins to fall, they come by chance to Badger's door.

Chapter 4: Mr. Badger

Badger takes them in, feeds them and shows them his underground home. He tells them about his plans for reforming Toad and the long tunnels under the Wild Wood.

Chapter 5: Dulce Domum

On the way home from Badger's, Mole catches the smell of his own old home. Rat leads him to the burrow, which is dusty and bare. The field-mice come to sing carols, and Mole is happy.

Chapter 6: Mr. Toad

Toad's craze for motor-cars grows worse, and his friends try to keep him indoors. He escapes, steals a car and is arrested. He is tried and sentenced to twenty years in prison.

Chapter 7: The Piper at the Gates of Dawn

Otter's small son, Portly, is missing. Rat and Mole search all night by boat, and at dawn they hear the music of a piper. They are given a moment of awe, and find the lost child.

Chapter 8: Toad's Adventures

In prison, Toad is helped by the jailer's daughter, who lends him a washerwoman's clothes. He escapes disguised, boards a train and is pursued by the police, and in the end gets on a locomotive and escapes.

Chapter 9: Wayfarers All

Rat meets a sea-going Rat on his way from the south, who tells such stories of far shores that Rat is nearly persuaded to leave with him. Mole brings him back.

Chapter 10: The Further Adventures of Toad

Still dressed as a washerwoman, Toad is taken in by a barge-woman, whom he cheats, and then steals a horse. He is picked up by a motor-car and is thrown out, and then falls into the river, which carries him to a hole in the bank.

Chapter 11: "Like Summer Tempests Came His Tears"

Rat pulls Toad from the water and listens to his boasting. Rat then tells him that the weasels and stoats now hold Toad Hall. Toad is shocked into tears, and Badger tells the friends of a secret way in.

Chapter 12: The Return of Ulysses

At night, the four friends enter Toad Hall through the secret tunnel. They surprise the weasels, stoats and ferrets and drive them out. Toad holds a banquet and finds that his adventures have changed him.
