The Call of the Wild: chapter-by-chapter summary

The book has 7 chapters, each titled with a short phrase about what Buck has to learn. The headings below are as printed in the text we checked, without the chapter number.

Chapter 1: Into the Primitive

Buck lives at Judge Miller's place in the Santa Clara Valley. A gardener's helper, Manuel, steals him and sells him to a stranger. Buck is shut up and beaten with a club, and learns that a man with a club must be obeyed, though not necessarily loved.

Chapter 2: The Law of Club and Fang

Buck arrives in the Northland and sees Curly, a friendly dog, killed by a pack of huskies. He learns the law of club and fang, to steal food, and to dig himself a nest in the snow. He joins a sled team driven by two couriers, Perrault and François.

Chapter 3: The Dominant Primordial Beast

The rivalry between Buck and Spitz grows during the long work on the trail. A chase after a rabbit leads to a fight. Buck wins, and takes his place as the leader of the team.

Chapter 4: Who Has Won to Mastership

Buck becomes a strong leader, and the team makes record-breaking runs. The party reaches Dawson. Buck is sold to new drivers, and one of the team, Dave, becomes ill and is killed.

Chapter 5: The Toil of Trace and Trail

Buck's new owners, Hal, Charles and Mercedes, know nothing of the trail and overload the sled, and the dogs starve. On the spring ice, John Thornton warns them not to go on. They refuse, and Thornton cuts Buck free just before the sled falls through the ice.

Chapter 6: For the Love of a Man

Buck recovers with Thornton, who treats his dogs as though they were his own children. Buck loves him with a deep devotion. When Thornton wins a bet that Buck can start a heavy sled, Buck's fame grows and the money helps Thornton go prospecting.

Chapter 7: The Sounding of the Call

Thornton and his partners go in search of a lost mine and find gold. Buck feels the call of the wild more strongly. When he returns from a hunt he finds that the Yeehats have killed Thornton and his friends, and Buck kills some of them. He then runs with the wolves.
