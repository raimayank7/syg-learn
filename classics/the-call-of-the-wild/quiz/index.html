The Call of the Wild quiz

9 questions about The Call of the Wild. Each answer can be found in the text, and when you answer you are shown which chapter to look in. The questions come in a random order each time.

Quiz

Before you play

Read the chapter summaries first if you have not read the book. Quiz answers are names, places and things named in the text, so a careful reader will know them. A wrong answer tells you the right one and where it is in the book.

Back to the The Call of the Wild study guide.

How the questions were made

Each question was written by us. When the site is built, a program checks that the correct answer occurs in the text of the chapter the question points to.

Where in the book the questions come from

The questions point to these parts of the book: Into the Primitive; The Law of Club and Fang; The Dominant Primordial Beast; Who Has Won to Mastership; The Toil of Trace and Trail; For the Love of a Man. If you miss one, the answer screen tells you the right place to look.
