What each guide contains

  • A short overview and the facts of the edition we checked against.
  • The main characters, with a line on each.
  • The main themes, explained in plain words.
  • A chapter-by-chapter summary, with the chapter headings exactly as they appear in the book.
  • A quiz whose answers are all in the text.

Why trust the summaries

A computer program checks every guide against the full text before the site is built. See how we check. We still make mistakes sometimes: if you find one, tell us.

Independent study aid: not affiliated with any publisher, school board or exam body. Summaries are our own words, checked against the Project Gutenberg text.
