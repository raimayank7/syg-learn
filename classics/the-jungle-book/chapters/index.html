The Jungle Book: story-by-story summary

The book is made of 7 stories. Verses sit between them in the original; the summaries below cover the stories only, and the headings are as printed in the text we checked.

Story 1: Mowgli's Brothers

A wolf family in the Seeonee hills finds a human baby wandering into their cave. The tiger Shere Khan demands the child, but Mother Wolf refuses and the Pack accepts him as Mowgli. Baloo teaches him the Law of the Jungle and Bagheera helps him. When the old leader Akela fails to make a kill, Mowgli fetches fire from the village in a pot and drives Shere Khan back.

Story 2: Kaa's Hunting

Mowgli is learning with Baloo when the Bandar-log, the monkeys, carry him off to their ruined city. Bagheera and Baloo are too few to fight them, so they ask for help from Kaa, the rock python. Kaa's dance holds the monkeys still, and Mowgli is saved just before he is hypnotised himself.

Story 3: "Tiger! Tiger!"

Mowgli goes to live in a man village and is taken in by Messua. He herds the buffalo, but Gray Brother warns him that Shere Khan is waiting. Mowgli and the wolves trap the tiger in a ravine and the buffalo stampede over him. The villagers then drive Mowgli away as a sorcerer, and he returns to the jungle.

Story 4: The White Seal

Kotick is a white seal pup in a crowded seal beach where men come to kill the young males. He decides to find a beach that hunters cannot reach, and searches the seas for years. At last he finds an island sheltered by cliffs, and leads the other seals there.

Story 5: "Rikki-Tikki-Tavi"

A mongoose washed out of his burrow by a flood is taken in by an English family and becomes the household's guardian. He kills the cobra Nag, then fights Nagaina, who has eggs in the melon bed. He saves the boy Teddy and destroys the eggs, with help from the bird Darzee.

Story 6: Toomai of the Elephants

Little Toomai is the son of an elephant driver and longs to be a driver himself. He rides his elephant Kala Nag into the forest at night and sees the elephants dance in a secret place. The next morning the hunters believe him when they see the trampled ground, and he is given a new name.

Story 7: Her Majesty's Servants

On a night before a parade, the animals of an army camp (camels, bullocks, mules, elephants and horses) talk about their work and how they bear it. Each tells what it has learned about army life and what it thinks of the others, and the camp settles for the night.
