How the breathing circle times work

The breathing circle follows a simple timetable. This page shows the numbers. It describes timings only; it does not say any pattern is good for your health.

The four patterns

PatternIn (s)Hold (s)Out (s)Rest (s)One roundRounds in a minute
Even40408 s7.5
Slow out406010 s6.0
Long out408012 s5.0
Square444416 s3.8

"Rounds in a minute" is just 60 divided by the length of one round.

How the circle size is worked out

The circle is at its smallest, 40 percent of full size, at the start. While you breathe in it grows to full size, in a smooth curve that starts and ends gently. During a hold it stays full size. While you breathe out it shrinks the same way, and during a rest it stays small. The seconds shown are whole seconds left in the current part, counted down.

Stop after

If you choose a time, the session ends at the end of the round that reaches that time, so you are never stopped halfway through a breath. For example, "about 1 minute" with the Slow out pattern (10 seconds a round) ends after 60 seconds, which is six rounds, and with the Square pattern (16 seconds a round) it ends after 64 seconds, which is four rounds.

How it is checked

When this site is built, a separate program works out the circle size, the part of the breath and the seconds left for thousands of moments in many patterns, and the build stops if the page code gives a different answer.
