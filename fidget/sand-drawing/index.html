Calm Toys

Draw in the sand

Drag on the sand to draw, or choose Rake lines to make parallel ridges.

This toy needs JavaScript.

How to use it

  1. Drag on the dark area to draw with grains of sand.
  2. Change the colour of the sand with the Sand box.
  3. Choose Rake lines to draw five parallel lines at once, like a rake in a sand garden.
  4. Press Smooth the sand to start again.

What it is doing

As you drag, the page puts small grains along your path. The grains are scattered within a small circle around each spot, using a repeatable pattern of numbers, so a stroke always looks a little rough in the same way. Rake lines are five lines the same length as your stroke, spaced evenly on either side of it.

Saving

Nothing is saved unless you press Save picture, which puts a picture file in your own downloads folder. The page itself never sends the picture anywhere.

Tips

  • Try one slow rake line across the whole picture.
  • On a touch screen, one finger draws.
