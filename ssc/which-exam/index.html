Which SSC exam: CGL, CHSL, MTS or Steno compared

This page compares the examinations only by what their scheme PDFs say. It does not compare qualifications, ages or pay, because the PDFs do not give them.

ExaminationStagesFirst stagePenalty stated
CGLTier-I, Tier-II100 questions, 200 marks, 60 minutes0.50 per wrong answer (Tier-I)
CHSLTier-I, Tier-II100 questions, 200 marks, 60 minutes0.50 per wrong answer (Tier-I)
MTS (NT) and HavaldarComputer based exam (and PET/PST for Havaldar)90 questions, 270 marks, two sessions of 45 minutesNot stated in the PDF
Stenographer C and DComputer based exam, then a skill test200 questions, 200 marks, 2 hoursNot stated in the PDF

Questions to ask yourself

  1. Which qualification do I have? Read it in the notice of each examination, since the scheme PDFs do not list it.
  2. Do I want a typing or stenography skill test? CHSL has a typing or skill test in Tier-II, and the Stenographer examination has a dictation test.
  3. How do I do against the clock? CGL and CHSL Tier-I give 36 seconds a question; MTS gives about 40 seconds a question.
  4. Do I need the Havaldar physical tests? Only the post of Havaldar has them.

Where the overlap is

General Intelligence or Reasoning, General Awareness, Quantitative or Numerical ability and English appear in every one of these examinations in some form, so a base in those four helps whichever you choose. If you are unsure, start there, then read the notice when one opens.
