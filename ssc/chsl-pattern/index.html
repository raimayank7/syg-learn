SSC CHSL exam pattern: Tier-I and Tier-II scheme

The Combined Higher Secondary (10+2) Level Examination, known as CHSL, also has two tiers of Computer Based Examination, per its scheme PDF.

Tier-I

PartSubject (not in sequence)Questions / marks
IEnglish Language (basic knowledge)25 / 50
IIGeneral Intelligence25 / 50
IIIQuantitative Aptitude (basic arithmetic skill)25 / 50
IVGeneral Awareness25 / 50
  • One 60 minutes for all four parts (80 minutes for candidates eligible for a scribe).
  • Objective type, multiple choice questions only.
  • Negative marking of 0.50 marks for each wrong answer.
  • Total: 100 questions, 200 marks.

Tier-II

SectionContentQuestionsMarksTime
Section-IModule-I Mathematical Abilities (30) and Module-II Reasoning and General Intelligence (30)6060 × 3 = 1801 hour
Section-IIModule-I English Language and Comprehension (40) and Module-II General Awareness (20)6060 × 3 = 1801 hour
Section-IIIModule-I Computer Knowledge1515 × 3 = 4515 minutes

Session-I of Tier-II lasts 2 hours 15 minutes. In Session-II, Section-III Module-II is a skill test or typing test: Part A (skill test for data entry operators in the departments named in the notice) and Part B (skill test for data entry operators elsewhere) of 15 minutes each, and Part C, a typing test for LDC and JSA, of 10 minutes. Candidates eligible for a scribe get more time.

Reading the scheme

Tier-I of CHSL is the same size as Tier-I of CGL: 100 questions, 200 marks, 60 minutes and 0.50 off for a wrong answer. Only the part order and the level differ: CHSL says its English Language is of basic knowledge and its Quantitative Aptitude is of basic arithmetic skill.

Source: Staff Selection Commission, the scheme-of-examination PDF for this examination from ssc.gov.in/for-candidates/scheme-of-examination, read on 14 November 2026. Those PDFs give the scheme only. They do not give age limits, fees, vacancies or the syllabus topics, so this site does not state them: read the notice of each examination on ssc.gov.in. Rules can change by notice.
