SSC Tier-I guide: four parts in 60 minutes

Tier-I of CGL and CHSL is a 60-minute test of 100 questions in four parts of 25. The scheme PDFs say the whole test is timed together, so you decide how to divide the 60 minutes.

The four parts

PartCGLCHSL
ReasoningGeneral Intelligence and Reasoning, 25 questionsGeneral Intelligence, 25 questions
AwarenessGeneral Awareness, 25 questionsGeneral Awareness, 25 questions
MathsQuantitative Aptitude, 25 questionsQuantitative Aptitude (basic arithmetic skill), 25 questions
EnglishEnglish Comprehension, 25 questionsEnglish Language (basic knowledge), 25 questions

How to split the time

Sixty minutes for 100 questions is 36 seconds each. Awareness questions take a few seconds if you know the fact, so many candidates take them first and keep more time for the arithmetic. This is our advice. Try different orders in timed papers and keep the one that gives you the best marks.

Marks and penalty

Each question is 2 marks and a wrong answer costs 0.50, one quarter of its marks, as in our negative marking page.

Using our practice

Our practice sets cover reasoning and arithmetic questions of our own, in the style of the General Intelligence and Quantitative Aptitude parts, with the same penalty ratio. We do not offer awareness or English questions.

A sample 60-minute plan

MinutesWhat to do
0 to 8General Awareness: answer what you know, mark the rest
8 to 22Reasoning: answer straight, skip the long ones
22 to 40Quantitative Aptitude: the biggest block of time
40 to 52English
52 to 60Return to marked questions and check your marking

This is only an example. Try it in a timed mock and change the order if it does not suit you.
