SSC sixty-day plan: a study schedule for Tier-I

This is a sixty-day plan of our own for a Tier-I style test. It is an example to adjust, not an official schedule. Count back from the closing date and exam month in the notice.

DaysFocusWhat to do
1 to 7Read and baselineRead the scheme and the notice. Take one timed 100-question mock to see where you stand in each part.
8 to 30Build the baseDaily: 45 minutes arithmetic, 30 minutes reasoning, 20 minutes English, 15 minutes awareness notes.
31 to 45Speed and accuracyPart-wise timed sets of 25 questions in 15 minutes. Review every wrong answer.
46 to 55Full mocksOne full 100-question mock every other day, with the penalty. Review each one.
56 to 60Revise and restRevise your notebook and awareness notes. Sleep well. Check documents.

Each week

  • Two full or part-wise timed tests
  • One review of the mistake notebook
  • One rest day

If you have less time

Drop the lowest-value task. Practice under the clock and review beat learning one more shortcut.

If you work full time

Plan fewer hours but fix them: one hour before work and two after, with a full mock on the weekend.

A sample weekday

  1. Morning, 45 minutes: arithmetic drill.
  2. Evening, 45 minutes: reasoning set with a timer.
  3. Night, 30 minutes: English reading and one awareness page, then review.

Source: Staff Selection Commission, the scheme-of-examination PDF for this examination from ssc.gov.in/for-candidates/scheme-of-examination, read on 14 November 2026. Those PDFs give the scheme only. They do not give age limits, fees, vacancies or the syllabus topics, so this site does not state them: read the notice of each examination on ssc.gov.in. Rules can change by notice. This site is independent and is not connected with the Staff Selection Commission.

What to do on a bad mock day

A weak score on one mock says little. Look at the pattern over three mocks. If the same part is low each time, give it an extra session that week. If marks fall because of wrong answers, practise skipping. If they fall because of unfinished questions, practise speed on short sets.

The last three days

Stop new topics. Read your mistake notebook, solve a few easy sets for confidence, check your admit card and route, and sleep well.
