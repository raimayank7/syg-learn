SSC Tier-I time planner

Choose the time allowed and the minutes for a final check. The tool shows the seconds a question gets.

The numbers behind it

Tier-I has 100 questions in 60 minutes (80 for scribe candidates). The planner takes your final-check minutes off that time and divides the rest by 100.

How the minutes divide

Tier-I of CGL and CHSL is 100 questions in 60 minutes for all four parts together (80 minutes for candidates eligible for a scribe). The table shows the time per question for different amounts kept for a final check.

With nothing kept back, 60 minutes gives 36 seconds a question. Awareness questions are usually fast, so you can give arithmetic more.

Three sample plans

  • Awareness first: do the 25 awareness questions in the first 8 minutes, then reasoning, maths and English.
  • Two passes: answer what you can do at once, then return to the marked ones.
  • Time boxes: give each part about 14 minutes and the last 4 to check.

Whichever you pick, check the clock at 15, 30 and 45 minutes.

Why seconds matter

A slip of ten seconds a question adds up to more than sixteen minutes over a hundred questions. Practise with a clock, and notice which kinds of question you answer fastest and slowest. Then plan to spend your extra seconds on the slow kind and to bank marks quickly on the fast kind.

Questions people ask

Is each part separately timed?

For Tier-I of CGL and CHSL the scheme PDFs give one time for all four parts, so you can move between parts.

How long should I keep for a final check?

Find out by practice. Five minutes is a common choice.
