SSC MTS and Havaldar exam pattern and physical tests

The scheme PDF for the Multi-Tasking (Non-Technical) Staff examination, which also covers Havaldar in CBIC and CBN, says the examination has a Computer Based Examination and a Physical Efficiency Test (PET) or Physical Standard Test (PST), the latter only for the post of Havaldar.

Computer Based Examination

SessionPartSubjectQuestions / marks
Session-I (45 minutes)INumerical and Mathematical Ability20 / 60
IIReasoning Ability and Problem Solving20 / 60
Session-II (45 minutes)IGeneral Awareness25 / 75
IIEnglish Language and Comprehension25 / 75

Each session is 45 minutes (60 minutes for candidates eligible for a scribe). Every question is worth 3 marks: 20 questions for 60 marks and 25 questions for 75 marks. The total is 90 questions and 270 marks.

What the scheme PDF does not say

It does not state a penalty for wrong answers, the age limit or the qualification. We did not read a notice, so we do not state them.

Source: Staff Selection Commission, the scheme-of-examination PDF for this examination from ssc.gov.in/for-candidates/scheme-of-examination, read on 14 November 2026. Those PDFs give the scheme only. They do not give age limits, fees, vacancies or the syllabus topics, so this site does not state them: read the notice of each examination on ssc.gov.in. Rules can change by notice. This site is independent and is not connected with the Staff Selection Commission.

How the time works out

SessionQuestionsMinutesSeconds per question
Session-I (numerical and reasoning)404567.5
Session-II (awareness and English)504554

The numbers show that the first session, with arithmetic and reasoning, gives more seconds a question than the second. Awareness and English questions are shorter, so the second session needs speed. These are our own readings of the scheme PDF.

Marks per question

Because 20 questions are worth 60 marks and 25 questions 75 marks, every question carries 3 marks. A candidate who gets 70 of the 90 questions right earns 210 of 270 marks before any penalty the notice may set.
