SSC Stenographer C and D pattern and skill test

The scheme PDF for the Stenographer Grade C and D examination has a Computer Based Examination and a Skill Test in Stenography.

Computer Based Examination

PartSubjectQuestionsMarks
IGeneral Intelligence and Reasoning5050
IIGeneral Awareness5050
IIIEnglish Language and Comprehension100100

The total duration is 2 hours (2 hours 40 minutes for candidates eligible for a scribe), for 200 questions and 200 marks.

Skill Test in Stenography

Candidates shortlisted in the computer based examination take a skill test. The candidate gets one dictation of 10 minutes in English or Hindi, as chosen in the application, at 100 words per minute for Grade C and 80 words per minute for Grade D, and has to transcribe it on a computer. The time allowed for transcription is:

PostLanguageTime (minutes)Time for candidates eligible for a scribe
Grade DEnglish5070
Grade DHindi6590
Grade CEnglish4055
Grade CHindi5575

What the scheme PDF does not say

It does not state a penalty for wrong answers or the qualification. We did not read a notice, so we do not state them.

Source: Staff Selection Commission, the scheme-of-examination PDF for this examination from ssc.gov.in/for-candidates/scheme-of-examination, read on 14 November 2026. Those PDFs give the scheme only. They do not give age limits, fees, vacancies or the syllabus topics, so this site does not state them: read the notice of each examination on ssc.gov.in. Rules can change by notice. This site is independent and is not connected with the Staff Selection Commission.

How the marks split

Part III, English Language and Comprehension, has 100 of the 200 marks, so half of the computer based examination is English. General Intelligence and Reasoning and General Awareness have 50 each. A candidate who is strong in English has the biggest single lever on the score.

Planning your practice

The dictation is at 80 words per minute for Grade D and 100 for Grade C. Practise dictation for ten minutes a day at the speed of your post and then transcribe with a timer that matches the table above. Keep a note of the kinds of mistake: spelling, omitted words and punctuation.
