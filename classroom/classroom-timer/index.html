Classroom countdown timer with full screen

Set the minutes and seconds and press Start. The clock can fill the screen.

How to use it

  1. Set minutes and seconds (up to 180 minutes).
  2. Press Start. The time counts down in big numbers.
  3. Pause, or Reset to go back to the time you set.
  4. Press Full screen to show only the clock on the board.

What happens at zero

The clock turns red and says that the time is up. On phones that allow it, the phone gives a short buzz. We do not play a sound because a sudden noise in a classroom is not always welcome; keep an eye on the clock.

Tips

  • For an activity, set the timer a minute shorter than you think, and extend it if needed.
  • For a test, tell the class when half the time has gone.
  • If the page is in a tab you leave, the time still counts correctly when you return.
