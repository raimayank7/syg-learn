Seating chart maker: random seats for your class

Type your names, say how many rows and seats, and make the chart.

How to use it

  1. Type or paste the names, one on each line.
  2. Choose the number of rows and seats in a row. There must be enough seats for everybody.
  3. Press Make seating chart. The names are placed in random seats, row by row, and any empty seats are at the back.
  4. Press Print for a copy for the desk.

Good to know

  • The chart shows the front of the room at the top, as you see the class from the board.
  • If a child needs a seat near the front, move them by hand after the chart is made.
  • Change the chart every few weeks if you like; pressing the button again gives a new one.

A worked example

A class of 22 in a room of 5 rows with 5 seats has 25 seats, so 3 are empty. They are shown as empty seats at the end of the last row. With 6 rows of 4 you would have 24 seats and 2 empty.
