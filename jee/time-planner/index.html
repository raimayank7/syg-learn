JEE Main time planner

Set the time for a final check and for the numerical questions. The tool divides the 180 minutes of Paper 1 among the subjects.

The numbers behind it

Paper 1 lasts 3 hours (180 minutes) and has 75 questions, 25 in each subject: 20 multiple-choice and 5 numerical. On average that is 2.4 minutes a question. The planner takes your final-check minutes off the total, shares the rest equally between the three subjects, and splits each subject's time between Section A and Section B.

How to use it

  • Try a plan in a timed mock paper, then adjust. Many students find the numerical questions take longer per question than the multiple-choice ones.
  • Decide in advance which subject you will begin with. Starting with your strongest builds confidence and saves minutes.
  • Write the plan on the rough sheet at the start of the paper. A time check at the end of each subject is a good habit.

More on this in how to prepare.

Even with nothing kept back, the average is only 2.4 minutes a question. That is why the order in which you attempt questions matters: a question that takes six minutes must be paid for by two that take one.

Questions people ask

Should every question get the same time?

No. Short formula questions take less and multi-step ones more. The planner gives averages that you adjust as you practise.

How long should I keep for a final check?

That is for you to find out by practice. Many students keep between ten and twenty minutes. Try a few values in timed papers.
