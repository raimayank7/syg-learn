What is on this site

JEE (Main) is the national entrance test conducted by the National Testing Agency for B.E., B.Tech., B.Arch. and B.Planning admissions. This site gathers in one place what a candidate needs to know and practise: the pattern and marking of Paper 1, the dates of both 2027 sessions, the fee table and eligibility rules, and the syllabus unit by unit.

Alongside the facts are practice sets and tools. The sets use the same +4 and −1 marking as the real paper, so that you get used to the cost of a wrong answer. The calculators turn your right and wrong counts into marks, a target into the number of attempts, and 180 minutes into minutes per question.

Where the facts come from

Every figure about the exam comes from NTA's Information Bulletin for JEE (Main) 2027 and the NTA syllabus document, both on jeemain.nta.nic.in. We read them on 13 November 2026. Dates and rules can change by public notice, so check the NTA website before you act. Our study advice is our own and makes no promise about results.

Common questions

What is the JEE Main 2027 exam pattern?

Paper 1 for B.E. and B.Tech. has 75 questions and 300 marks: 25 questions in each of Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry, with 20 multiple-choice and 5 numerical questions per subject. The time is 3 hours. This is from the NTA bulletin read on 13 November 2026.

How is JEE Main marked?

A correct answer scores +4, an incorrect answer scores −1 and an unanswered question scores 0. This applies to both multiple-choice and numerical questions.

When is JEE Main 2027?

Session 1 is on 20, 21, 22, 23, 24 and 28 January 2027 and Session 2 on 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7 April 2027, according to the bulletin. Check NTA for changes.

Is there an age limit?

The bulletin says there is no age limit for JEE (Main) 2027. Candidates who passed Class XII in 2025 or 2026, or are appearing in 2027, are eligible.

Are your practice questions from past papers?

No. They are our own, made by a program, and are easier than real JEE questions. Use them for habit and accuracy, and use NTA's papers for a real measure.

Are you connected with NTA?

No. This is an independent site and is not connected with NTA, the Ministry of Education or any coaching institute.
