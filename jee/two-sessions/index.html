JEE Main two sessions: which to attempt?

JEE (Main) 2027 has two sessions, January and April. How do you decide whether to appear in one or both? This page lists what the bulletin says and then offers a way to think about it. It is a guide, not advice about your personal case.

Source: NTA, Information Bulletin, JEE (Main) 2027, read on 13 November 2026. Rules and dates can change, so check jeemain.nta.nic.in before you act. This site is independent and is not connected with NTA, the Ministry of Education or any coaching institute.

What the bulletin says

  • Session 1 is in January 2027 and Session 2 in April 2027. Dates are on our dates page.
  • The fee is paid for each session. You can apply for Session 1 now and apply for Session 2 later using the same application number.
  • If you want only Session 2, you can register when its application form opens.
  • The overall merit list is made by merging the NTA scores of all shifts of both sessions.
  • Candidates may not submit more than one application form. Multiple forms are treated as unfair means and can lead to cancellation of the result.

The bulletin does not describe on this page how scores from the two sessions are combined for a candidate who appears in both. Read the appendix on score normalisation and the instructions on the NTA website for that.

How to think about it

The first session is a real test of where you stand, and the second session gives time to repair what went wrong. A candidate who is fairly ready can use Session 1 as a trial that counts. A candidate who is far from ready may prefer to use the months before April, and treat January as a rehearsal or skip it. Either way, the choice is yours, and fees are modest compared with the cost of a poor sitting.

Questions to ask yourself

  • How many units of the syllabus have I finished once, properly?
  • In my last three timed papers, was my accuracy steady?
  • Do I know how I behave in the exam hall for three hours, including when I am stuck?
  • Do my board examinations fall near the Session 2 dates?
  • Will I be able to accept the result of Session 1 if it is lower than I hoped?

Practical tips for the day

  • Print and keep the admit card and a photo ID ready the night before.
  • Check the city slip date and plan travel.
  • Do not change your method on the exam day. Use what you have practised.
  • After the paper, do not rewrite it in your head for a week; use that time to prepare for the next step.

For a calm plan, see the twelve-week plan and the page on common mistakes.
