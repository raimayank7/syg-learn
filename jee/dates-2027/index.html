JEE Main 2027 dates for both sessions

Here are the dates the 2027 information bulletin gives for the two sessions of JEE (Main) 2027. The bulletin says that the second session has its own application window and that a separate notice will be issued for it. All form deadlines run until 11:50 p.m. on the last day.

Source: NTA, Information Bulletin, JEE (Main) 2027, read on 13 November 2026. Rules and dates can change, so check jeemain.nta.nic.in before you act. This site is independent and is not connected with NTA, the Ministry of Education or any coaching institute.

Things to know about these dates

  • The bulletin was published in October 2026. On the day we read it, 13 November 2026, the Session 1 window was still open.
  • NTA can change dates by public notice. Always look at the NTA website for notices before you rely on a date.
  • The bulletin also says that the question paper you attempted and the answer keys will be shown on the NTA website so that candidates can challenge them. No date is given for this in the table.
  • You can apply for Session 1 and pay for it, and later apply separately for Session 2 using the same application number. If you only want Session 2, you can register when its form opens.

Working backwards from an exam day

A date is useful once you count the weeks to it. From 13 November 2026, when we read the bulletin, the first January exam day (20 January 2027) was about ten weeks away, and the first April exam day (2 April 2027) about twenty. Your own count will differ, so do the sum for today.

  • Count the weeks from today to the day you are aiming for.
  • Subtract the last week, which is for rest and light revision, and the weeks your school examinations will take.
  • Share what is left between finishing the syllabus, practice sets and full papers. A common split is half for the syllabus, a quarter for sets and a quarter for full papers.

If the count is short, it is better to choose the later session than to rush; if you are far ahead, you can use the earlier one as a trial that counts.

A date table is only helpful if it is turned into a plan. Our twelve-week plan works backwards from an exam day, and the page on the two sessions explains how to think about attempting one or both.
