JEE Main numerical questions: how to approach them

In each subject, five of the 25 questions are numerical: no options are shown, and you type in a number. The bulletin says the answer is rounded off to the nearest integer and that a wrong numerical answer costs 1 mark, just as in the multiple-choice questions. This page explains how to work with that.

Source: NTA, Information Bulletin, JEE (Main) 2027, read on 13 November 2026. Rules and dates can change, so check jeemain.nta.nic.in before you act. This site is independent and is not connected with NTA, the Ministry of Education or any coaching institute.

Why these questions feel different

With options, you can check backwards, eliminate, or at least see if your answer looks sensible. With a number to type, none of that is available. You must be right by your own working, and the answer must be exact.

Habits that help

  • Write units and check them. If you are asked for a speed and get metres squared, something is wrong.
  • Estimate first. Before you calculate, say what size of answer to expect. If you get 4,000 where you expected about 40, look for a slipped power of ten.
  • Keep the working tidy. Write one step per line and keep the numbers in a column. Most arithmetic errors come from squeezing steps together.
  • Round only at the end. The bulletin asks for the nearest integer for the final answer; rounding earlier causes drift.
  • Re-read what is asked. "How many" and "what is the value of" are not the same as "what is x".

Check by another route

If a question is worth 4 marks and you have time, solve it a second way, or substitute your answer back. For chemistry, check that the number of moles fits the mass. For physics, test a limiting case. For mathematics, plug in a simple value of the variable.

Practice

Our practice sets include numerical-value questions in each subject. Type your number and press Check. Because they are original and easier than the real paper, they are for building the habit of working carefully and getting the answer exact. Try the mathematics set, the physics set or the chemistry set.

When to skip

A wrong numerical answer loses a mark, and an unanswered one loses none. If you cannot get started within a minute or two, mark it and move on. You may come back with fresh eyes. See negative marking for how to decide.
