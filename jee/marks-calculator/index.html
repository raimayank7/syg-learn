JEE Main marks calculator

Enter your counts for each subject. The total follows the official scheme: +4 for each right answer, −1 for each wrong one, 0 for skipped.

How it works

For each subject the tool adds your right answers in Sections A and B, multiplies by 4, and subtracts one mark for each wrong answer. Section A has 20 multiple-choice questions and Section B has 5 numerical questions, so each subject is out of 100, and the paper is out of 300. The scheme comes from the NTA bulletin for 2027 (read on 13 November 2026).

Using it well

  • Use it on your own mock papers: count right, wrong and skipped in each section, and see what total they give.
  • Try changing one number. Turning a wrong answer into a skipped one adds exactly 1 mark; turning it into a right one adds 5.
  • Remember that the marks here are raw marks. The NTA score and rank are worked out from them by NTA across all shifts, so the same raw marks can map to different NTA scores in different shifts.

See also the score planner and the page on negative marking.

The fast attempt tries all 25 questions but gets only 14 right, so its 11 wrong answers pull the total down to 45. The careful attempt tries only 18 and ends at 67. The balanced one tries 23, with 4 wrong, and ends at 72. Attempting more helps only while accuracy stays high.

Questions people ask

Does this give my NTA score or rank?

No. It gives raw marks only. NTA converts raw scores to NTA scores across shifts, and ranks come from those.

Is a skipped question marked down?

No. The bulletin gives 0 marks to a question that is unanswered, or marked for review without an answer.

Do Section B questions have negative marking?

Yes. The 2027 bulletin says there is negative marking in both Section A and Section B.
