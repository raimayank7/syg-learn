JEE Main Paper 2A and 2B: B.Arch. and B.Planning

JEE (Main) also has papers for architecture and planning. This page summarises what the 2027 bulletin says about them. If you want B.Arch. or B.Planning, read the bulletin itself too, because these papers have more parts.

Source: NTA, Information Bulletin, JEE (Main) 2027, read on 13 November 2026. Rules and dates can change, so check jeemain.nta.nic.in before you act. This site is independent and is not connected with NTA, the Ministry of Education or any coaching institute.

Paper 2A: B.Arch.

Mathematics and the aptitude test are on a computer. The drawing test is taken on paper, on an A4 drawing sheet, in the offline mode. The marking for mathematics and aptitude is +4 for a correct answer, −1 for an incorrect or multiple answer and 0 for no answer. The two drawing questions are evaluated out of 100 marks.

All three parts are computer based. Marking is the same +4, −1 and 0 as in Paper 1.

Time

Paper 2A alone, or Paper 2B alone, lasts three hours. A candidate who takes both B.Arch. and B.Planning papers has three and a half hours (9:00 a.m. to 12:30 p.m., or 3:00 p.m. to 6:30 p.m.).

Syllabus

The mathematics part uses the same fourteen mathematics units as Paper 1; you can read them on our mathematics syllabus page. For B.Arch., the aptitude test (as the syllabus document lists it) covers awareness of buildings, materials and textures, visualising three-dimensional objects from two-dimensional drawings, analytical reasoning and mental ability (visual, numerical and verbal), three-dimensional perception (scale, proportion, colour and texture), designing and drawing geometrical or abstract patterns in pencil, and transformation of forms. The drawing test is sketching of scenes and activities from memory, such as urban spaces, landscapes and rural life, on a drawing sheet; the syllabus advises candidates to bring pencils, a geometry box, erasers, and colour pencils or crayons. The B.Planning aptitude and planning-based parts have their own syllabus (including social sciences and thinking skills such as reading a passage, maps, charts and graphs). For the exact wording, read the syllabus PDF on the NTA site.

Ties

The tie-breaks for Paper 2A run through the NTA scores in mathematics, aptitude and drawing, then through the proportion of incorrect to correct answers. For Paper 2B they run through mathematics, aptitude and planning-based questions, then the same proportions.

Our practice questions on this site are for the mathematics, physics and chemistry of Paper 1 and for Paper 2's mathematics part. We do not offer practice for the aptitude and drawing tests.
