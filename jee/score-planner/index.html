JEE Main score planner

Choose a target and your accuracy. The tool works out how many questions you would need to attempt, under +4 for right and −1 for wrong.

What it assumes

It assumes a paper of 75 questions with +4 and −1 marking, as in the 2027 bulletin, and that you answer correctly at the same rate in every subject. Real papers are uneven; use the result as a rough guide.

Reading the result

  • At 100% accuracy you need to attempt a quarter of the marks, so a target of 150 needs about 38 right answers.
  • Each point of accuracy you lose forces you to attempt more questions for the same score, and every extra wrong answer costs 5 marks of swing.
  • If the tool says the target cannot be reached at your accuracy, either the target is too high or the accuracy must go up first.

Accuracy usually improves faster from reading your mistakes than from attempting more. See how to prepare.

Questions people ask

Why does accuracy matter so much?

A wrong answer costs the 4 marks you could have earned and 1 more on top, so a 5 mark difference. The cost of mistakes grows with every extra attempt.

Does the tool predict my rank?

No. It only turns marks and accuracy into counts. Rank depends on everyone else, and on the conversion of raw scores to NTA scores.
