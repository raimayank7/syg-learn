Equations of motion and force formulas

Use standard units together: metres, seconds and kilograms, so that forces come out in newtons and energy in joules. The equations of motion hold only for constant acceleration.

v = u + at

v = u + at (constant acceleration)

Final speed v after time t from starting speed u with acceleration a, all in the same units (for example metres, seconds).

Worked example: With Start speed u = 5, Acceleration a = 2, Time t = 4: Final speed v = 13.

s = ut + ½at²

s = ut + ½ a t² (constant acceleration)

Distance s travelled in time t.

Worked example: With Start speed u = 5, Acceleration a = 2, Time t = 4: Distance s = 36.

v² = u² + 2as

v² = u² + 2as (constant acceleration)

Final speed from distance without needing the time. The calculator checks it against the first two equations.

Worked example: With Start speed u = 5, Acceleration a = 2, Time t = 4: v² from v = u + at = 169; u² + 2as with s = ut + ½at² = 169.

Force

F = m a (force = mass × acceleration)

In newtons when mass is in kilograms and acceleration is in metres per second squared.

Worked example: With Mass m = 10, Acceleration a = 3: Force F = 30.

Work, kinetic energy and momentum

Work W = F × d (force along the movement). Kinetic energy = ½ m v². Momentum p = m v

Joules for work and energy and kilogram metres per second for momentum, in standard units.

Worked example: With Force F = 20, Distance d = 5, Mass m = 4, Speed v = 3: Work W = 100; Kinetic energy = 18; Momentum p = 12.

Density

Density = mass / volume

How much mass there is in each unit of volume. Keep the units the same (for example kilograms and cubic metres).

The volume cannot be 0.

Worked example: With Mass m = 540, Volume V = 200: Density = 2.7.

Remember by using

  • Check units first: mixing kilometres an hour with seconds is the most common error.
  • The third equation can be got from the first two; the calculator checks that for you.
  • Acceleration is negative when something slows down.
