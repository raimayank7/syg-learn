Mind map mistakes to avoid and how to fix them

Maps go wrong in a few typical ways. Here are the ones we see most often, with a fix for each. This is our own advice.

ProblemWhy it hurtsFix
Whole sentences in boxesThe map becomes a page of textCut to a key phrase; add details as child ideas
Ten or more main branchesYou cannot see the shapeGroup related branches under a new one
One branch very deepOften means that branch is its own topicMake a separate map for it
Never read backMistakes stay hiddenSay the map aloud from the middle
Beautiful but never usedTime spent on looks, not thinkingUse Auto-arrange and move on

Branch count

Between three and seven main branches usually works, but there is no law. Trust your own eyes: if you cannot take in the map at a glance, simplify it.

Mixed topics

If two unrelated subjects are in one map, split them. A map shows connections, so unrelated things only add noise.

Starting again

It is fine to throw a map away. The thinking you did while making it was the useful part. The New map choices and starter maps on the home page make a fresh start quick.

The advice on this page is our own. It is not a rule from a school, a board or a publisher, and this site is independent and is not connected with any of them. Your map stays in your own browser: nothing you write is sent to us.
