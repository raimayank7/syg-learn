Mind map maker

Add ideas, drag them, arrange them neatly, and save a picture of your map. Free, no sign-in, and your map stays in your browser.

The map maker needs JavaScript. Without it you can still read the guides below.

How to use it

  1. Click the middle idea and type your topic in the text box.
  2. Press Add idea under selected, or press Enter in the text box, and type the next idea.
  3. Drag ideas to tidy them yourself, or press Auto-arrange and let the page do it.
  4. Save the picture as PNG or SVG, or print the map. Save map file keeps a copy you can open again later.

Starter maps

The Start from box fills the page with a starting map that you can change: blank (1 ideas), essay (16 ideas), project (14 ideas), revision (14 ideas), trip (14 ideas).

Is it private?

Yes. Everything happens inside your browser. Nothing you write is sent to us and there is no sign-in. The map is kept in your browser storage on this device so it is there when you come back; clear your site data and it is gone, so use Save map file for a copy.

The advice on this page is our own. It is not a rule from a school, a board or a publisher, and this site is independent and is not connected with any of them. Your map stays in your own browser: nothing you write is sent to us.
