What is on this site

International days and weeks appear in many general-knowledge sections, and they are a small, exact part of current affairs. This site gives the full United Nations list by month with dates and the authority behind each day, a finder that also shows what the list has for your device date, a quiz, and a notes keeper for your own current affairs notes.

Where the facts come from

The list is copied from the United Nations page read on 14 November 2026. We publish no news. Our guides are our own advice and make no promise about results.

Common questions

Is this a news site?

No. It does not publish news. It offers the United Nations list of international days, a quiz made from it, a notes keeper and guides on following current affairs.

Where does the list come from?

From the United Nations page "List of International Days and Weeks", read on 14 November 2026. It has 248 entries with a fixed date.

Does it include days whose date moves each year?

No. The UN page we read lists fixed-date observances only. Days that fall on a different date each year are not in our data.

Are my notes private?

Yes. They stay in your own browser and are never sent to us. They do not follow you to another device.

Will this help me with current affairs questions?

Only a little: exams ask about events, which this site does not cover. The days list helps with questions on dates and themes of observances, and the guides give a method for the news.

Are you connected with the United Nations?

No. This is an independent site.
