International days quiz

Three sets made from the United Nations list: dates, names and mixed.

  • Dates of daysTen questions from the UN list
  • Names of daysTen questions from the UN list
  • MixedTen questions from the UN list

Why only the days list?

Current affairs change every day and a program cannot write them honestly. The UN list of international days is fixed, official and checkable, so a quiz made from it is exact. It covers a small part of what exams may ask, but it is a part you can learn completely.

How the questions are made

A small program picks an entry from the list and asks either its date or the day on its date. It asks about a date only when the list has exactly one entry on it, so each answer is single. A second program re-checks every question against the data file when the site is built.

How to use it

  1. Learn one month from its page.
  2. Take a round.
  3. Study the misses.
  4. Repeat the next day.

A habit that works

Five minutes a day with one month is better than an hour once a week. After two weeks, take the mixed set and see which months still catch you out. Then go back to those month pages. The quiz keeps your best score in your own browser, so you can see progress over time.
