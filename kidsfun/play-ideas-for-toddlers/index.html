Play ideas for toddlers and under-fives

Little children need very little: your attention, a few safe things and time. These are ideas for children aged two and up. Stay close and watch all the time, because small children can put things in their mouths and move fast.

  • Action SongsAges 2 to 6, 15 min, indoors, active
  • Roll the BallAges 2 to 5, 10 min, indoors or outdoors, active
  • Stacking CupsAges 2 to 4, 15 min, indoors, calm
  • Box PlayAges 2 to 6, 30 min, indoors, calm
  • Toddler Garden WalkAges 2 to 5, 25 min, outdoors, calm
  • Water PlayAges 2 to 6, 30 min, outdoors, calm
  • Bubble ChaseAges 2 to 7, 15 min, outdoors, active
  • Freeze DanceAges 2 to 8, 15 min, indoors, active

Action Songs

Sing songs together and do the actions with your hands and feet.

  • Ages 2 to 6
  • About 15 min
  • Indoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You need no materials for this one.

How to play

  1. Choose a song the child knows well, such as a counting or animal song.
  2. Sing it slowly and add an action for every line: clap, stamp, wave or turn around.
  3. Sing it again faster and let the child choose the next song.

Tip: Toddlers learn by copying, so make your actions big and your face cheerful.

Roll the Ball

Sit on the floor and roll a soft ball back and forth to each other.

  • Ages 2 to 5
  • About 10 min
  • Indoors or outdoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Soft ball1

How to play

  1. Sit on the floor facing the child with your legs apart, a short way away.
  2. Say "ready" and roll the ball gently to the child.
  3. Ask the child to roll it back to you. Move further apart a little at a time.

Tip: Count the rolls aloud. Choose a ball that is too big to swallow.

Stacking Cups

Build towers from plastic cups and knock them down.

  • Ages 2 to 4
  • About 15 min
  • Indoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Plastic cups6

How to play

  1. Turn the plastic cups upside down on the floor or table.
  2. Show the child how to put one cup on top of another to make a tower.
  3. Knock the tower over together and say "crash", then build it again.

Tip: Use big, sturdy cups. Count the cups as you stack them.

Box Play

A big cardboard box becomes a car, a house or a boat.

  • Ages 2 to 6
  • About 30 min
  • Indoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Large cardboard box1
Crayons1

How to play

  1. Put a large, empty cardboard box on the floor, open side up or on its side.
  2. Let the child climb in and out, and draw on the outside with crayons.
  3. Ask what the box is today: a boat, a car or a house. Play along with the story.

Tip: Check that the box has no staples or sharp edges before the child plays.

Toddler Garden Walk

A slow walk to look for bugs, leaves and flowers.

  • Ages 2 to 5
  • About 25 min
  • Outdoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You need no materials for this one.

How to play

  1. Hold the child's hand and walk slowly round the garden or park.
  2. Stop whenever the child sees something and say what it is: a leaf, a stone, a bird.
  3. Take a fallen leaf home and put it on a piece of paper.

Tip: Do not let the child put leaves, berries or stones in their mouth.

Water Play

Pour, scoop and splash with a bowl of water outside.

  • Ages 2 to 6
  • About 30 min
  • Outdoors
  • Adult needed
An adult is needed. An adult must stay with the child the whole time they are near water.

What you need

You needHow many
Large bowl1
Plastic cups and spoons4
Towel1

How to play

  1. Put the large bowl on a flat spot outside and half fill it with water.
  2. Give the child cups and spoons to pour, scoop and splash.
  3. Dry the child and the floor with the towel when the game is over, and empty the bowl at once.

Tip: Stay with the child all the time. Empty the water as soon as you finish.

Bubble Chase

Blow bubbles and chase and pop them.

  • Ages 2 to 7
  • About 15 min
  • Outdoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Bubble mixture (from a shop)1
Bubble wand1

How to play

  1. Pour a little bubble mixture into its container and dip the wand.
  2. Blow gently or wave the wand to make a stream of bubbles.
  3. Let the child chase and pop the bubbles. Count how many they pop.

Tip: Keep the bubble mixture out of eyes and mouths, and wipe up spills, as they make floors slippery.

Freeze Dance

Dance when the music plays and freeze when it stops.

  • Ages 2 to 8
  • About 15 min
  • Indoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Music player1

How to play

  1. Clear a space so that nobody can trip on a toy or a rug.
  2. Play music and dance. Stop the music without warning.
  3. Everyone freezes like a statue. Anyone who moves is out, or does a silly dance.

Tip: For little ones, nobody is out: the aim is to freeze and laugh.

Kitchen Band

Make music with pots and wooden spoons.

  • Ages 2 to 6
  • About 15 min
  • Indoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Pots and pans (unbreakable)3
Wooden spoons3

How to play

  1. Put the pots upside down on the floor and give everyone a wooden spoon.
  2. Tap out a rhythm and let the children copy it.
  3. Play a song together, loudly and then softly, and stop when you say "stop".

Tip: Keep it short because it is loud, and protect little ears.
