Playing together when children are different ages

Brothers and sisters of different ages can play together with a few changes. These are our own suggestions, based on how the ideas on this site work.

Give each child a job

Let the older child be the leader, the scorekeeper or the helper, and let the younger child do the part they can do. In a city of blocks, the older child plans, the younger one places the blocks.

Games that stretch

  • I Spy with colours for the little one and letters for the older.
  • Freeze dance works for all ages.
  • A treasure hunt with picture clues for the little one and word clues for the big one.

Keep it fair

Give a head start or a shorter run to the younger child in a race. Praise effort, not winning. Do not ask an older child to play with a younger one for too long: ten minutes of kindness is better than an hour of resentment.

The ideas on this page are our own suggestions and are not research or professional advice. Every child is different, so you decide what suits yours and you stay in charge of safety.
