Outdoor play ideas for kids: garden, park and street

Fresh air and room to run help children sleep and feel good. These ideas suit a garden, a park or a safe open space. Read the safety notes, and see our page on playing safely outdoors.

Treasure Hunt

Follow written clues from one hiding place to the next.

  • Ages 4 to 10
  • About 30 min
  • Indoors or outdoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Paper2
Pencil1
Small prize or snack1

How to play

  1. A grown-up hides the prize and writes four or five clues, each leading to the next hiding place.
  2. Give the first clue to the children and let them read it together.
  3. They follow the clues until they find the prize, and then share it.

Tip: Draw a picture clue for children who cannot read yet.

Hopscotch

Draw squares in chalk and hop through them.

  • Ages 4 to 10
  • About 20 min
  • Outdoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Chalk3
Small stone or beanbag1

How to play

  1. Draw a column of squares on the ground with chalk and number them from 1 to 8.
  2. Throw the stone on square 1 and hop over it to the end, then back.
  3. Pick up the stone on the way back. Throw to square 2 next, and so on.

Tip: Draw it on a flat, dry place away from traffic.

Chalk Roads

Draw a town with roads and ride toy cars on it.

  • Ages 3 to 9
  • About 30 min
  • Outdoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Chalk6
Toy cars3

How to play

  1. Draw a long road on the ground with a junction and a bridge.
  2. Add shops, a school, a park and a house along the road.
  3. Drive the toy cars round the town and make up a story.

Tip: Pick a flat, quiet place and draw away from traffic.

Nature Scavenger Hunt

Find ten things from nature in the garden or park.

  • Ages 5 to 12
  • About 40 min
  • Outdoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Paper bag1
Paper2
Pencil1

How to play

  1. Write a list of things to find: a smooth stone, a feather, a round leaf, a seed.
  2. Walk round the garden or park and collect the things that have fallen on the ground.
  3. Compare your finds with the list and tick them off. Put everything back or take home only what you found on the ground.

Tip: Do not pick flowers or put anything in your mouth, and wash your hands afterwards.

Sack Race

Hop in a pillowcase to the finish line.

  • Ages 5 to 12
  • About 20 min
  • Outdoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Old pillowcases or cloth sacks4

How to play

  1. Mark a start and a finish line with chalk or a stick on soft grass.
  2. Everyone stands in a pillowcase and holds it up with both hands.
  3. On "go" hop to the finish line. Fall over safely and laugh.

Tip: Play on grass, not on concrete, and clear stones away.

Skipping Games

Jump rope alone, in twos or in a group.

  • Ages 6 to 13
  • About 20 min
  • Outdoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Skipping rope1

How to play

  1. Check that the rope reaches from your armpits to the floor when you stand on the middle.
  2. Swing the rope over your head and jump over it as it comes down.
  3. Count how many jumps you can do without stopping and try to beat the number tomorrow.

Tip: Skip on a flat surface and wear shoes.

Relay Race

Teams carry an object to a marker and back.

  • Ages 7 to 13
  • About 20 min
  • Outdoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Plastic bottles or cones4
Spoon and ball2

How to play

  1. Put a bottle or cone as a marker about twenty steps from the start.
  2. Make two teams. The first runner carries a ball on a spoon to the marker and back.
  3. Hand over to the next runner. The first team to finish wins.

Tip: Make it fair by giving younger children a shorter run.

Soft Ball Cricket

A backyard game of cricket with a soft ball.

  • Ages 6 to 13
  • About 40 min
  • Outdoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Soft ball1
Bat1
Stumps or a stack of three bags1

How to play

  1. Set up the stumps and mark the bowling spot with chalk.
  2. The bowler bowls underarm with the soft ball and the batter tries to hit it.
  3. Take turns to bat, bowl and field. Count the runs on paper.

Tip: Play in an open space away from windows and cars, and use a soft ball only.

Garden Picnic

Eat lunch on a blanket in the garden or park.

  • Ages 3 to 12
  • About 1 h
  • Outdoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Blanket1
Snacks and a water bottle1
Basket or bag1

How to play

  1. Pack snacks, a water bottle and a blanket in a bag.
  2. Choose a shady, flat spot and spread the blanket.
  3. Eat, play a game such as I Spy, and take all the rubbish home.

Tip: Keep food covered, and take care with nuts for any child with an allergy.

Paper Plane Contest

Fold paper planes and see whose flies furthest.

  • Ages 6 to 12
  • About 25 min
  • Indoors or outdoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
A4 paper sheet4
Tape measure or ruler1

How to play

  1. Fold a paper plane from each sheet, choosing your own way to fold.
  2. Stand on a line and throw your plane. Never throw towards a person.
  3. Measure the distance each plane flew and write the results in a table. Change one thing and try again.

Tip: Throw only in a space with nobody in front of you.

Backyard Camp

Sleep or play in a tent or under a sheet in the garden.

  • Ages 6 to 13
  • About 2 h
  • Outdoors
  • Adult needed
An adult is needed. An adult must be present all evening and night.

What you need

You needHow many
Tent or large sheet1
Blankets3
Torch1

How to play

  1. An adult chooses a flat, safe spot in the garden and puts up the tent or sheet.
  2. Lay out the blankets inside and put the torch where everyone can reach it.
  3. Tell stories, look at the stars and go inside if the weather changes.

Tip: There must be no fire and no heater. Keep the garden gate locked.

Kite Flying

Fly a kite on a windy day in an open space.

  • Ages 6 to 13
  • About 45 min
  • Outdoors
  • Adult needed
An adult is needed. An adult must come along to pick a safe open place away from roads and power lines.

What you need

You needHow many
Kite1
Reel of cotton string1

How to play

  1. Choose a wide open space with no roads, power lines or trees.
  2. Stand with the wind at your back, hold the kite up and let the line out as it lifts.
  3. Steer by pulling the line gently. Wind it back in before you leave.

Tip: Use only plain cotton string, never glass-coated or metal string, which can cut people and birds.

Safe Cycling Time

Ride a bicycle in a safe place with an adult.

  • Ages 6 to 13
  • About 45 min
  • Outdoors
  • Adult needed
An adult is needed. An adult must come along and watch, and choose a place away from traffic.

What you need

You needHow many
Bicycle1
Helmet1

How to play

  1. Put on a helmet and check the tyres and the brakes with an adult.
  2. Ride in a park or a quiet area with no traffic, with the adult watching.
  3. Practise stopping, turning and signalling with your arm.

Tip: Wear a helmet every time. Never ride on a busy road.
