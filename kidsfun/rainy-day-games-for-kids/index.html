Rainy day games for kids: active indoor fun

When it pours, children still need to move. These games burn energy indoors. Clear the floor first and put away anything breakable.

  • Musical StatuesAges 3 to 9, 15 min, indoors, active
  • Cushion Obstacle CourseAges 3 to 9, 20 min, indoors, active
  • Sock Ball TossAges 5 to 12, 15 min, indoors, active
  • Indoor BowlingAges 4 to 10, 20 min, indoors, active
  • Warmer and ColderAges 4 to 10, 15 min, indoors, active
  • CharadesAges 6 to 13, 20 min, indoors, active

Musical Statues

Walk to the music and stand still the moment it stops.

  • Ages 3 to 9
  • About 15 min
  • Indoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Music player1

How to play

  1. Ask everyone to walk in a circle while the music plays.
  2. Stop the music. Everyone stands as still as a statue.
  3. Anyone who moves sits down. The last child standing starts the music next time.

Tip: Walk, do not run, so that no one bumps into anyone.

Cushion Obstacle Course

Make a course from cushions and chairs to crawl, jump and climb.

  • Ages 3 to 9
  • About 20 min
  • Indoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Cushions6
Chairs2
Scarf1

How to play

  1. Lay out cushions in a line to jump over and chairs to crawl under.
  2. Lay a scarf flat on the floor as a line to balance along.
  3. Race through the course one at a time and try to beat your own time.

Tip: Do it on a carpet and not on a polished floor. Clear the space first.

Sock Ball Toss

Roll socks into balls and toss them into a basket.

  • Ages 5 to 12
  • About 15 min
  • Indoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Pairs of clean socks6
Basket or box1

How to play

  1. Roll each pair of socks into a ball and put the basket on the floor.
  2. Stand a few steps away and take turns to toss the sock balls into the basket.
  3. Score a point for each one in, then move back one step and play again.

Tip: Move the basket further away for older children and closer for younger ones.

Indoor Bowling

Knock down plastic bottles by rolling a soft ball.

  • Ages 4 to 10
  • About 20 min
  • Indoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Empty plastic bottles with lids6
Soft ball1

How to play

  1. Stand the bottles in a triangle at one end of a clear hallway or room.
  2. Stand at the other end and roll the soft ball along the floor.
  3. Count the bottles you knock down, set them up again and take turns.

Tip: Put a little water or sand in each bottle so they do not fall by themselves, and close the lids tightly.

Warmer and Colder

Find a hidden object by listening to "warmer" and "colder" clues.

  • Ages 4 to 10
  • About 15 min
  • Indoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Small object to hide1

How to play

  1. One person hides a small object while the searcher waits outside the room.
  2. The searcher comes in and walks around. The hider calls "warmer" when they get close and "colder" when they move away.
  3. When the object is found, the finder hides it next time.

Tip: Hide it where it can be seen without moving anything, so that nothing gets broken.

Charades

Act out a word or an animal without speaking and let the others guess.

  • Ages 6 to 13
  • About 20 min
  • Indoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Paper2
Pencil1

How to play

  1. Write animals, films or actions on small pieces of paper and fold them.
  2. One person picks a paper and acts it out without making a sound.
  3. The others guess. The one who guesses right acts next.

Tip: For younger children, use only animals.
