About this age

Children of two to four play beside you more than with you. They like repeating things, so do not be surprised if they want the same game ten times. Stay close, and keep small things out of reach.

The ideas on this page are our own suggestions and are not research or professional advice. Every child is different, so you decide what suits yours and you stay in charge of safety.
