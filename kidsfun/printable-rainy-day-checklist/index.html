Activity Ideas for Kids

Printable rainy day checklist for kids: 35 ideas

Press Print this page for a checklist of 35 things to do indoors, with a box to tick for each one. Stick it on the fridge and tick the ideas as you do them. Every idea can be done without an adult's full attention, and none uses anything unsafe.

DoneIdeaAgesTime
Action Songs
Sing songs together and do the actions with your hands and feet.		2 to 615 min
Roll the Ball
Sit on the floor and roll a soft ball back and forth to each other.		2 to 510 min
Stacking Cups
Build towers from plastic cups and knock them down.		2 to 415 min
Box Play
A big cardboard box becomes a car, a house or a boat.		2 to 630 min
Blanket Fort
Build a cosy den from blankets, chairs and cushions.		3 to 1040 min
Sock Puppet Show
Make puppets from socks and put on a show.		4 to 930 min
Shadow Puppets
Use your hands and a torch to make animal shadows on a wall.		4 to 1020 min
Memory Tray
Look at a tray of objects, then guess which one has gone.		5 to 1215 min
Play Dough Town
Make animals and houses from ready-made play dough.		3 to 830 min
Building a City
Build towns and towers from blocks or boxes.		3 to 840 min
Dress-Up Show
Dress up in old clothes and put on a play for the family.		4 to 1040 min
Draw a Comic
Draw a comic strip about a day in the life of a pet or toy.		8 to 1340 min
Draw a Treasure Map
Draw a map of your home or garden and mark a hidden treasure.		6 to 1230 min
Board Game Afternoon
Play a board game together, take turns and be a good sport.		6 to 1345 min
Card Games
Learn a simple card game such as Snap or Memory Pairs.		7 to 1330 min
Family Quiz Night
Make up questions for each other and keep score.		8 to 1340 min
Scarf Juggling
Learn to juggle with light scarves, which fall slowly.		7 to 1320 min
Freeze Dance
Dance when the music plays and freeze when it stops.		2 to 815 min
Musical Statues
Walk to the music and stand still the moment it stops.		3 to 915 min
Cushion Obstacle Course
Make a course from cushions and chairs to crawl, jump and climb.		3 to 920 min
Kitchen Band
Make music with pots and wooden spoons.		2 to 615 min
Sock Ball Toss
Roll socks into balls and toss them into a basket.		5 to 1215 min
Indoor Bowling
Knock down plastic bottles by rolling a soft ball.		4 to 1020 min
Warmer and Colder
Find a hidden object by listening to "warmer" and "colder" clues.		4 to 1015 min
Charades
Act out a word or an animal without speaking and let the others guess.		6 to 1320 min
Story Chain
Make up a story together, one sentence each.		5 to 1315 min
I Spy
Guess what someone sees from the first letter or the colour.		3 to 910 min
Hide and Seek
One child counts while the others hide.		3 to 1220 min
Simon Says
Follow the leader's orders, but only when they say "Simon says".		3 to 915 min
Car Journey Games
Spot colours, count signs and play twenty questions in the car.		5 to 1320 min
Treasure Hunt
Follow written clues from one hiding place to the next.		4 to 1030 min
Stone Painting
Paint smooth stones to make animals or garden decorations.		5 to 1240 min
Seed in a Cup
Plant a bean in a clear cup and watch it grow.		4 to 1220 min
Sink or Float
Guess which objects sink and which float in a bowl of water.		5 to 1020 min
Paper Plane Contest
Fold paper planes and see whose flies furthest.		6 to 1225 min

How to use the list

  • Choose one idea after lunch and one after tea.
  • Let the child choose the next one.
  • Write the date next to the ticks you finish.

The ideas on this page are our own suggestions and are not research or professional advice. Every child is different, so you decide what suits yours and you stay in charge of safety.
