Activity Ideas for Kids

Games for kids that need no equipment

Queues, car journeys and waiting rooms are easier with a game in your head. None of these games needs anything but people.

  • Story ChainAges 5 to 13, 15 min, indoors or outdoors, calm
  • I SpyAges 3 to 9, 10 min, indoors or outdoors, calm
  • Hide and SeekAges 3 to 12, 20 min, indoors or outdoors, active
  • Simon SaysAges 3 to 9, 15 min, indoors or outdoors, active
  • Car Journey GamesAges 5 to 13, 20 min, indoors, calm

Story Chain

Make up a story together, one sentence each.

  • Ages 5 to 13
  • About 15 min
  • Indoors or outdoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You need no materials for this one.

How to play

  1. Sit in a circle and choose who starts. They say: "Once upon a time..." and add one sentence.
  2. The next person adds the next sentence, and so on round the circle.
  3. Finish with "The end" after everyone has had three turns, and laugh at the story.

Tip: Do not worry if the story is silly. That is the fun.

I Spy

Guess what someone sees from the first letter or the colour.

  • Ages 3 to 9
  • About 10 min
  • Indoors or outdoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You need no materials for this one.

How to play

  1. One person looks around and chooses an object without saying what it is.
  2. Say: "I spy with my little eye something beginning with B" or "something red".
  3. The others guess. The one who guesses right chooses next.

Tip: With younger children, use colours instead of letters.

Hide and Seek

One child counts while the others hide.

  • Ages 3 to 12
  • About 20 min
  • Indoors or outdoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You need no materials for this one.

How to play

  1. Choose a seeker. The seeker closes their eyes and counts aloud to twenty.
  2. The others hide. Agree first on the places that are not allowed.
  3. The seeker looks for the others. The first one found is the next seeker.

Tip: Rule out places such as cupboards, cars and anything that can lock.

Simon Says

Follow the leader's orders, but only when they say "Simon says".

  • Ages 3 to 9
  • About 15 min
  • Indoors or outdoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You need no materials for this one.

How to play

  1. One person is Simon and gives orders: touch your toes, hop, clap.
  2. Do the action only when the order starts with "Simon says".
  3. Anyone who does an action without the words is out or has to be Simon next.

Tip: Start slowly and speed up so that everyone has a fair chance.

Car Journey Games

Spot colours, count signs and play twenty questions in the car.

  • Ages 5 to 13
  • About 20 min
  • Indoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You need no materials for this one.

How to play

  1. Choose a game: spot all the red cars, count the buses or find the letters of the alphabet on signs.
  2. Take turns to look and shout, and keep a score on a piece of paper if you like.
  3. For twenty questions, one person thinks of an animal and the others ask up to twenty yes or no questions.

Tip: Everyone must stay in their seat with the seat belt on. Games must never distract the driver.
