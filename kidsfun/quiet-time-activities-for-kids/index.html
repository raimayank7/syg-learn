Quiet time activities for kids: calm indoor ideas

Blanket Fort

Build a cosy den from blankets, chairs and cushions.

  • Ages 3 to 10
  • About 40 min
  • Indoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Blankets3
Chairs3
Clothes pegs or clips6

How to play

  1. Ask a grown-up which room and which chairs you may use, and put the chairs a little apart.
  2. Drape the blankets across the chairs and hold them in place with the pegs or clips.
  3. Put cushions and books inside, crawl in and read a story or tell one.

Tip: Make sure the chairs cannot tip over, and keep the fort away from heaters.

Sock Puppet Show

Make puppets from socks and put on a show.

  • Ages 4 to 9
  • About 30 min
  • Indoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Clean socks2
Felt tip pens (set)1

How to play

  1. Put a clean sock on your hand like a glove and find where the mouth is.
  2. Draw eyes, a nose and a mouth on the sock with pens.
  3. Give each puppet a name and a voice and make up a short show for the family.

Tip: Draw on the sock while it is on a flat table, not on your hand.

Shadow Puppets

Use your hands and a torch to make animal shadows on a wall.

  • Ages 4 to 10
  • About 20 min
  • Indoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Torch or lamp1

How to play

  1. Darken the room and shine the torch on a plain wall from a little way away.
  2. Put your hands between the light and the wall and make shapes: a bird, a rabbit, a dog.
  3. Make up a short story for your shadows and let the others guess each animal.

Tip: Never shine a torch into anyone's eyes.

Memory Tray

Look at a tray of objects, then guess which one has gone.

  • Ages 5 to 12
  • About 15 min
  • Indoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Tray1
Small everyday objects10
Cloth1

How to play

  1. Put ten different objects on the tray, such as a spoon, a key, a toy and a pencil.
  2. Let everyone look for one minute, then cover the tray with the cloth.
  3. Take one object away without anyone seeing and ask what is missing. Take turns being the one who hides.

Tip: Begin with five objects for younger children and add more as they get better.

Play Dough Town

Make animals and houses from ready-made play dough.

  • Ages 3 to 8
  • About 30 min
  • Indoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Ready-made play dough (non-toxic)2
Round bottle for rolling1
Plastic shape moulds3

How to play

  1. Roll the dough into a flat piece with the round bottle on a clean table.
  2. Press the moulds into it, or roll snakes and balls to build animals and houses.
  3. Make a town on a tray and tell its story. Put the dough back in its tub afterwards.

Tip: Wash hands afterwards, and keep dough out of mouths.

Building a City

Build towns and towers from blocks or boxes.

  • Ages 3 to 8
  • About 40 min
  • Indoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Building blocks or small boxes20

How to play

  1. Empty the blocks on the floor and decide what to build: a tower, a bridge or a whole city.
  2. Build it together, taking turns to add a block.
  3. Name the buildings and make roads, then knock down only the buildings the child chooses.

Tip: Build on a flat floor. Check that the blocks are big enough not to be swallowed.

Dress-Up Show

Dress up in old clothes and put on a play for the family.

  • Ages 4 to 10
  • About 40 min
  • Indoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Old clothes and scarves for dressing up1

How to play

  1. Put a box of old clothes, scarves and hats on the floor.
  2. Each child chooses a costume and says who they are.
  3. Make up a short play with a beginning, a middle and an end, and perform it for the family.

Tip: Keep clothes that trail on the floor off, so that no one trips.

Draw a Comic

Draw a comic strip about a day in the life of a pet or toy.

  • Ages 8 to 13
  • About 40 min
  • Indoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Paper2
Pencil1
Coloured pencils1

How to play

  1. Fold a sheet of paper into six boxes and number them in order.
  2. Think of a hero and a problem, and draw what happens in each box.
  3. Add speech bubbles and colour the pictures. Read the comic aloud.

Tip: Stick figures are fine. The story matters more than the drawing.

Draw a Treasure Map

Draw a map of your home or garden and mark a hidden treasure.

  • Ages 6 to 12
  • About 30 min
  • Indoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Paper2
Pencil1
Coloured pencils1

How to play

  1. Draw the plan of a room, the house or the garden from above.
  2. Mark landmarks such as a sofa or a tree, and put an X where the treasure is.
  3. Give the map to a friend and let them find the treasure.

Tip: Hide the treasure somewhere safe, not high up.

Board Game Afternoon

Play a board game together, take turns and be a good sport.

  • Ages 6 to 13
  • About 45 min
  • Indoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Board game of your choice1

How to play

  1. Choose a game that everyone knows or can learn quickly.
  2. Read the rules aloud together before you begin.
  3. Play, take turns, and shake hands at the end whoever wins.

Tip: Agree in advance how long you will play, so that nobody is upset by an unfinished game.

Card Games

Learn a simple card game such as Snap or Memory Pairs.

  • Ages 7 to 13
  • About 30 min
  • Indoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Pack of playing cards1

How to play

  1. Shuffle the cards and deal them out equally, face down.
  2. For Snap, turn the cards over one at a time and shout "Snap" when two match.
  3. Keep score on a piece of paper and play again.

Tip: For Memory Pairs, lay out ten pairs face down and turn two over at a time to find matches.

Family Quiz Night

Make up questions for each other and keep score.

  • Ages 8 to 13
  • About 40 min
  • Indoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Paper6
Pencil3

How to play

  1. Each person writes five questions on a sheet of paper, with the answer on the back.
  2. Take turns to ask a question. A correct answer scores a point.
  3. Add up the scores and give a small prize or a clap to the winner.

Tip: Ask about things everyone knows, such as family, school and favourite foods, not only about school subjects.

Scarf Juggling

Learn to juggle with light scarves, which fall slowly.

  • Ages 7 to 13
  • About 20 min
  • Indoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Light scarves3

How to play

  1. Hold one scarf in the middle and throw it up high with one hand.
  2. Catch it with the same hand. Do this ten times.
  3. Add a second scarf and throw one as the other is falling. Then try three.

Tip: Practise near a wall and away from furniture. Scarves are easier than balls because they fall slowly.

Stone Painting

Paint smooth stones to make animals or garden decorations.

  • Ages 5 to 12
  • About 40 min
  • Indoors or outdoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Smooth stones4
Washable paint (set)1
Brushes2

How to play

  1. Wash the stones and let them dry.
  2. Paint each stone with a base colour and let it dry for a few minutes.
  3. Add faces or patterns with a thin brush. Put the stones in the garden when they are dry.

Tip: Stones are not for mouths. Wash hands after painting.

Seed in a Cup

Plant a bean in a clear cup and watch it grow.

  • Ages 4 to 12
  • About 20 min
  • Indoors or outdoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Clear plastic cup1
Soil or damp cotton wool1
Bean or pea seeds3
Small jug of water1

How to play

  1. Fill the cup with soil or damp cotton wool.
  2. Push the seeds down against the side of the cup so that you can see them.
  3. Put the cup on a sunny window sill, add a little water each day and check it. Seeds often sprout in one or two weeks, but the time varies.

Tip: Draw the plant each day on a chart and see how it changes.

Sink or Float

Guess which objects sink and which float in a bowl of water.

  • Ages 5 to 10
  • About 20 min
  • Indoors
  • No adult needed

What you need

You needHow many
Large bowl of water1
Small safe objects (cork, leaf, spoon, plastic toy)5
Towel1

How to play

  1. Put the bowl of water on a towel on a table and line up the objects.
  2. For each object, guess whether it will sink or float and write down your guess.
  3. Put them in one by one and see if you were right. Sort them into two piles.

Tip: Wipe up spills at once. Empty the bowl when you have finished.
