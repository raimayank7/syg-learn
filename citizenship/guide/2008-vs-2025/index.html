The 2008 and 2025 question lists compared

We compared the two official USCIS lists question by question. Here is what differs, counted from the lists themselves.

Size and rules

The 2008 list has 100 questions; the officer asks up to 10 and you need 6. The 2025 list has 128; the officer asks up to 20 and you need 12. Both lists mark 20 questions for applicants aged 65 or older with 20 years of permanent residence.

What the 2025 list drops

The 2008 list has a Geography part: the longest rivers, the oceans on each coast, the territories, and the states that border Canada and Mexico. The 2025 list has no geography part. Questions about the month of the presidential election, the tax deadline of April 15 and the two major political parties are also in the 2008 list only.

What the 2025 list adds

  • More "why" questions. Examples: why there are three branches of government, why representatives serve shorter terms than senators, why each state has two senators, why Supreme Court justices serve for life, why the Electoral College is important.
  • More people. James Madison, Alexander Hamilton and Dwight Eisenhower each get a "famous for many things" question, alongside Franklin, Washington, Jefferson, Lincoln and Martin Luther King, Jr.
  • More history after 1900. The Great Depression, the reasons for entering World War I, World War II, Korea, Vietnam and the Persian Gulf War, and conflicts after September 11, 2001.
  • The Federalist Papers, with a question on why they were important.
  • American innovations and the meaning of the motto "E Pluribus Unum".

What stays the same

The heart of both lists is the same: the Constitution as the supreme law, three branches, 100 senators, 435 representatives, Jefferson and the Declaration of Independence, Lincoln and the Civil War, Martin Luther King, Jr., the flag, the anthem and the capital. A person who learned the 2008 list well already knows a large share of the 2025 list. But the wording of questions and accepted answers often differs, so study the list for your own version: 2025 or 2008.
