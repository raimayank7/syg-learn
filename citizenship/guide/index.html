Citizenship Test

Guides to the U.S. citizenship test

How the test works, which version you take, what changes with age, and how to study so the answers come out right when spoken.

The civics question lists tell you what to learn. These guides cover everything around them. The first group explains the rules as USCIS publishes them: which of the two versions applies to you, how many questions are asked, how the English part is judged, which exceptions exist for older applicants and for people with disabilities, and what happens if you do not pass at the first interview. Each of those pages names the official page it is based on and the date we read it.

The second group is study help of our own: a four-week plan, how to give short exact answers, and three pages that pull together the answers people mix up most, the numbers and dates, the named people, and the questions that need more than one answer.

If you have just filed, read which test will I take? and then how the test works. If your interview is close, go to answers that change and the practice test.
