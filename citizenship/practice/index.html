Citizenship Test

Civics practice test

Questions one at a time, in random order, scored the way a USCIS officer scores: it stops when you pass or fail.

Say each answer aloud, then show the accepted answers and mark yourself honestly.

How this practice test works

The real civics test is spoken. The officer asks a question, you answer aloud, and the officer decides whether your answer matches an accepted one. There are no options to choose from. A multiple-choice quiz therefore trains the wrong skill: recognising an answer is much easier than producing it. This practice test copies the real thing as closely as a web page can.

  1. A question appears. Say your answer aloud, as you would to the officer.
  2. Press "Show the accepted answers". Compare honestly. For questions that ask for two, three or five items, count them.
  3. Press "I was right" or "I was wrong".
  4. The test ends as soon as the result is certain, exactly as the official rules say.

Questions about office holders and your state

When a question asks who holds an office now, the test shows where to find the current answer instead of a name. Look the names up beforehand so that you can mark yourself: answers that change.

After the test

The result screen lists the questions you missed, with their answers and notes. Your last results are kept in this browser only, so you can watch your score rise. When you can pass five times running with few mistakes, you are ready. For the questions you keep missing, use the flashcards.
