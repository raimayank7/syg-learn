Why this site exists

The official syllabus of a board is free and public, but it is a long file written for teachers, and the page that matters most to a student, the table of marks by unit, is easy to miss. Meanwhile the internet is full of syllabus pages that are a year out of date, or that mix facts with guesses about "important chapters".

This site does one narrow thing. For each subject it sets out the units, the chapters under them and the marks each unit carries, exactly as the board's file gives them, with the date we read the file and a link to it. It adds a short note on anything in the file that changes what is examined, such as a chapter assessed only in school. It does not predict questions and it does not invent weightage for chapters where the board gives none.

Questions people ask

Which syllabuses are on this site?

CBSE classes 10 and 12, session 2026-27, for 12 subjects: Accountancy, Biology, Business Studies, Chemistry, Economics, English Core, English Language and Literature, Mathematics, Physics, Science, Social Science.

Is this an official CBSE website?

No. It is an independent site that summarises the official files and links to them. The official source is cbseacademic.nic.in.

Why are other boards and exams missing?

We add a syllabus only after reading the official document for it. More will be added in time; nothing is filled in from memory.

How current is it?

The CBSE files were read on 12 November 2026. The Board can issue changes during the year, so check the official site before an examination.
