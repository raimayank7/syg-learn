About this syllabus

English Core is divided by skill: reading, creative writing and literature. Literature, from the textbook Flamingo and the supplementary reader Vistas, is half the paper. The file gives the marks and the word limit of every writing task, and even how the marks of each are split between format, content and accuracy.

Units and chapters

  1. Section A: Reading Skills
    • Two unseen passages, 12 + 10 marks, with a combined length of 700 to 750 words; one may be case-based with data or charts
  2. Section B: Creative Writing Skills
    • Notice (4 marks)
    • Formal or informal invitation and reply (4 marks)
    • Letter: job application or letter to the editor (5 marks)
    • Article or report writing (5 marks)
  3. Section C: Literature
    • Extracts and questions from the textbook Flamingo (prose and poetry)
    • Extracts and questions from the supplementary reader Vistas

Where the marks are

The theory paper is 80 marks in 3 hours. The largest share goes to Section C: Literature with 40 marks, which is 50% of the paper. The smallest is Section B: Creative Writing Skills with 18 marks. The two largest entries in the table together carry 62 marks, 78% of the paper.

Using this to plan

  • Writing tasks are marked for format as well as content. A correct format earns a mark before a word of content is read.
  • Reading is 22 marks on passages you have not seen. Practise with timed passages, including ones with data.
  • Literature questions ask for inference and evaluation, so knowing the story is not enough. Know why characters act as they do.
