About this syllabus

Class 12 Mathematics has six units and thirteen chapters, and one unit dominates: Calculus carries 35 of the 80 marks. The question paper design in the file adds that there is no chapter-wise weightage within a unit and that care is to be taken to cover all the chapters. So the 35 marks of Calculus may fall on any of its five chapters.

Where the marks are

The theory paper is 80 marks. The largest share goes to Calculus with 35 marks, which is 44% of the paper. The smallest is Linear Programming with 5 marks. The two largest entries in the table together carry 49 marks, 61% of the paper. With 13 chapters in all, an average chapter is worth about 6.2 marks, but the table shows how unevenly they are spread.

Using this to plan

  • Calculus is 35 marks, 44 percent of the paper. A student who is weak in calculus cannot score well, whatever else is strong.
  • Vectors and Three-dimensional Geometry is the second block at 14 marks, and its methods are standard.
  • Linear Programming is a single chapter for 5 marks, usually one question of a fixed type.
