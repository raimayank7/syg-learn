About this syllabus

Business Studies has twelve units in two parts. Part A, Principles and Functions of Management, has eight units and 50 marks. Part B, Business Finance and Marketing, has four units and 30 marks. The Board gives marks for groups of units, not for each unit, so the table follows those groups. One project is done for the remaining 20 marks.

Where the marks are

The theory paper is 80 marks in 3 hours. The largest share goes to Part A, units 6 to 8: Staffing; Directing; Controlling with 20 marks, which is 25% of the paper. The smallest is Part A, units 4 and 5: Planning; Organising with 14 marks. The two largest entries in the table together carry 36 marks, 45% of the paper.

Using this to plan

  • Part A, management, is 50 of the 80 marks.
  • Staffing, Directing and Controlling together form the largest group at 20 marks.
  • Each pair in Part B is worth 15 marks, so the four finance and marketing units average 7.5 marks each, more per unit than Part A.
