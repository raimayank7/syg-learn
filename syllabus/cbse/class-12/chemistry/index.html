About this syllabus

Class 12 Chemistry has ten units, each of which is one chapter, and the marks are spread very evenly: no unit is below 6 or above 9. The first three units are physical chemistry (23 marks), the next two inorganic (14 marks), and the last five organic (33 marks). The practical examination carries 30 marks, of which volumetric analysis and salt analysis take 8 each.

Where the marks are

The theory paper is 70 marks in 3 hours. The largest share goes to Electrochemistry with 9 marks, which is 13% of the paper. The smallest is Haloalkanes and Haloarenes with 6 marks. The two largest entries in the table together carry 17 marks, 24% of the paper.

Using this to plan

  • Organic chemistry, the last five units, is 33 marks: the largest block.
  • Because marks are even across units, leaving out any one chapter costs 6 to 9 marks. There is no safe chapter to skip.
  • Volumetric analysis and salt analysis are 16 of the 30 practical marks. Practise both until they are routine.
