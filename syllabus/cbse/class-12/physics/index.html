About this syllabus

Class 12 Physics has nine units and fourteen chapters. The marks are not given unit by unit but for pairs of units, so the table below follows the groups exactly as the Board prints them. Electricity and magnetism, units I to IV, account for 33 of the 70 theory marks. The practical examination is separate, 30 marks, with a record of at least 8 experiments and 6 activities and an investigatory project.

Where the marks are

The theory paper is 70 marks in 3 hours. The largest share goes to Units V and VI: Electromagnetic Waves; Optics with 18 marks, which is 26% of the paper. The smallest is Unit IX: Electronic Devices with 7 marks. The two largest entries in the table together carry 35 marks, 50% of the paper. With 14 chapters in all, an average chapter is worth about 5.0 marks, but the table shows how unevenly they are spread.

Using this to plan

  • Units I to IV, electricity and magnetism, are 33 marks: nearly half of the theory paper.
  • Optics with Electromagnetic Waves is the single largest group at 18 marks.
  • The 30 practical marks are earned through the year: experiments, activities, record and project. They are much easier to secure than the last 30 theory marks.
