About this syllabus

The theory paper has two parts. Part A, Accounting for Partnership Firms and Companies, is 60 marks and is common to all. For Part B, 20 marks, there is a choice: Financial Statement Analysis, shown in the table, or Computerised Accounting as a single unit of 20 marks. Those who take Financial Statement Analysis do project work for the remaining 20 marks; those who take Computerised Accounting do practical work.

Where the marks are

The theory paper is 80 marks in 3 hours. The largest share goes to Part A, Unit 1: Accounting for Partnership Firms with 36 marks, which is 45% of the paper. The smallest is Part B, Unit 4: Cash Flow Statement with 8 marks. The two largest entries in the table together carry 60 marks, 75% of the paper.

Using this to plan

  • Partnership accounts alone are 36 marks, 45 percent of the theory paper.
  • Part A is 60 marks whichever option you choose in Part B.
  • The 20 marks for project or practical work are split 12 for the file and 8 for the viva voce.
