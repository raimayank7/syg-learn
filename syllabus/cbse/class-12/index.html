CBSE class 12 syllabus 2026-27: subjects and marks

Class 12 is the Senior School Certificate Examination. Subjects differ in how the 100 marks are made up. Subjects with laboratory work, such as Physics, Chemistry and Biology, have a theory paper of 70 marks and a practical examination of 30. Mathematics and English Core have a paper of 80 marks with 20 of internal assessment. Accountancy, Business Studies and Economics have a paper of 80 marks with a project of 20.

The eight subjects here cover the usual science and commerce combinations. Within each subject the marks are far from evenly spread: Calculus is 35 of 80 in Mathematics, and partnership accounts are 36 of 80 in Accountancy. Knowing this at the start of the year is worth more than any last-minute strategy.

  • Physics 9 units · 70 + 30 marks
  • Chemistry 10 units · 70 + 30 marks
  • Biology 5 units · 70 + 30 marks
  • Mathematics 6 units · 80 + 20 marks
  • Accountancy 4 units · 80 + 20 marks
  • Business Studies 12 units · 80 + 20 marks
  • Economics 8 units · 80 + 20 marks
  • English Core 3 units · 80 + 20 marks

Questions people ask

How many marks is each class 12 paper?

In the subjects summarised here: Physics 70 + 30; Chemistry 70 + 30; Biology 70 + 30; Mathematics 80 + 20; Accountancy 80 + 20; Business Studies 80 + 20; Economics 80 + 20; English Core 80 + 20.

Where can I download the official syllabus?

From cbseacademic.nic.in, under Curriculum, choose the academic year and then the subject. Each subject page here links straight to its file.
