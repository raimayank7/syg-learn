About this syllabus

Science is one paper covering chemistry, biology and physics. The syllabus groups its five units under themes: Materials, The World of the Living, Natural Phenomena, How Things Work and Natural Resources. The file marks some topics as included in the syllabus but assessed only formatively, that is, in school and not in the year-end paper. Periodic Classification of Elements and Evolution are named in this way. Read the notes in the official file to see exactly which topics these are for your year.

Units and chapters

  1. Chemical Substances: Nature and Behaviour
    • Chemical Reactions and Equations
    • Acids, Bases and Salts
    • Metals and Non-metals
    • Carbon and its Compounds
  2. World of Living
    • Life Processes
    • Control and Co-ordination in Animals and Plants
    • Reproduction
    • Heredity
  3. Natural Phenomena
    • Reflection of light by curved surfaces
    • Refraction and lenses
    • The human eye, prism, dispersion and scattering of light
  4. Effects of Current
    • Electric current, potential difference, Ohm's law and resistance
    • Magnetic effects of current
  5. Natural Resources
    • Our Environment

Where the marks are

The theory paper is 80 marks in 3 hours. The largest share goes to Chemical Substances: Nature and Behaviour with 25 marks, which is 31% of the paper. The smallest is Natural Resources with 5 marks. The two largest entries in the table together carry 50 marks, 62% of the paper. With 14 chapters in all, an average chapter is worth about 5.7 marks, but the table shows how unevenly they are spread.

Using this to plan

  • Chemistry and biology units carry 25 marks each: together, 50 of 80. Physics (light and electricity) carries 25.
  • Practical work is assessed inside the 20 internal marks, and the file lists the practicals unit by unit.
  • Natural Resources is only 5 marks, but it is one short chapter. Do not skip it.
