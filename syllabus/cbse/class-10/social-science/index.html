About this syllabus

Social Science has four parts of 20 marks each. In History the 20 is split as 18 for questions and 2 for map pointing; in Geography as 17 and 3. The file attaches conditions to some chapters, and these matter for revision. In History, only the first subtopics of The Making of a Global World are evaluated in the Board examination, the rest being part of an interdisciplinary project, and The Age of Industrialisation is to be assessed as part of periodic assessment only. In Geography, Lifelines of National Economy is evaluated in the Board examination for map pointing only. In Economics, part of Globalisation and the Indian Economy goes to the project, and Consumer Rights is project work.

Where the marks are

The theory paper is 80 marks in 3 hours, shared equally: each of the 4 parts in the table carries 20 marks, 25% of the paper. No part can be neglected, and none is worth more of your time than the others on marks alone.

Using this to plan

  • Each of the four parts is worth the same 20 marks, so no part can be left weak.
  • Map work is 5 marks in all (2 in History, 3 in Geography). The file lists the map items; practise exactly those.
  • Check the conditions on chapters in the official file before you plan revision. Some chapters are assessed in school and not in the Board paper.
