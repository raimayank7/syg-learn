Chore Chart and Pocket Money

Pocket money planner: weekly earnings and a saving goal

Type the amounts and read the week's total and the weeks to a goal.

How it works

The weekly total is the basic pocket money plus, for each chore, the money for one time multiplied by the times it was done. The weeks to a goal are the price minus the money saved so far, divided by the amount saved each week, rounded up to a whole week.

A worked example

Basic 100, tidy room 10 a time done 5 times, wash dishes 15 a time done 3 times: 100 + 50 + 45 = 195. For a goal of 1,500 with 200 saved and 75 saved a week: (1,500 − 200) / 75 = 17.3, so 18 weeks.

Use it with your child

  • Let the child type the numbers and see the weeks for themselves.
  • Agree the rules before the week starts, not after.
  • Write the goal on the fridge with a drawing of the thing.
